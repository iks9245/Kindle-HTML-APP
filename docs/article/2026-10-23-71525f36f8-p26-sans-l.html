I could be wrong. I am not rosy-tinted about the realities of experiments like these, how badly they can hurt people if done poorly. The way we live may not last. We commit, unlike monks and nuns, only in five-year increments. This season has been the most meaningful, the most beautiful and the most fun of my life, despite the challenges. It has made me braver, and freed up swathes of time and energy that I would have otherwise spent on home admin in a household of only two adults. It is working, really working, for now, but we are realists. We talk a lot about exits, about the fact that we will probably not live together forever. Perhaps we’re trying to have our cake and eat it, keeping one foot in the autonomy of liberal individualism even as we experience the benefits of rooted, committed mutual care. Our experiment is imperfect. My hunch, though, is that we are going to need more of them.
