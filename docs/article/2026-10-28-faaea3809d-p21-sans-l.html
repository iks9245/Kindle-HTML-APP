How to build real peace

What I witnessed in Rojava was something I had rarely seen in post-conflict contexts: peace built not as the absence of war – because war continues in Rojava – but as a way of daily life. Historically, millions of people were living a process of true self-governance, with their identities reconstituted and their voices active in collective decision-making. To build anticapitalist peace, Rojava shows, negative peace is not indispensable. The form of social organisation is what matters.
