A child can look intensely engaged while being given very little time to think

Don’t get me wrong, I am not saying that slow is always good or better than fast media. Some lively children’s media can be excellent; some slow media can be dull, inert or badly made. The point is that good children’s media give a child time to complete an act of attention. Bad media keep interrupting that act before it can become a memory or play.
