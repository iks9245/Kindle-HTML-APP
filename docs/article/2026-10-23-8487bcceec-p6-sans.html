Lina was feeding them sips of fortified milk from a tiny bottle, but Raad could not keep it down. He was cradled in her arms - his eyes wide and his cheeks sunken. Asked if she thought her twins would recover, Lina replied quietly, "God willing."

As we walked through the unit with its pink walls, some doors were padlocked shut.

There are 22 beds, but staff told us only 13 patients were admitted at a time - the most critical cases - due to the lack of international funding, staff, and equipment.

That's a bitter pill to swallow for Dr Noor Mohammed, a harried young doctor.

"It's sad to say, but we lose patients [because of the conditions]," she says.
