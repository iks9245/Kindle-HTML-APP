Deskilling the body

Machines now promised more than control. They offered a glimpse of immortality: the organism might be repaired, remade and ultimately replaced. The somewhat embarrassing body, with its impulses and eventual frailty, might then be cast aside. If the body is only hardware, why not upgrade to a better model or do away with it altogether?

Today, we can see this fear writ large in the technological quest for a bodiless life. If a person is ultimately a pattern of information, that pattern might be restored or even uploaded. AI is only the latest iteration of this ancient impulse to escape the vulnerability of the body.
