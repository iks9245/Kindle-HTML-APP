Barbara Ward’s vision

During the war, Ward joined the Sword of the Spirit, then a British Catholic organisation that encouraged believers to support the democratic battle against totalitarianism. This experience, as well as her friendship with Arnold J Toynbee, Christopher Dawson and Jacques Maritain, persuaded her of the importance of religion – and especially Christianity – as the moral foundation of political and economic internationalism. Ward drew inspiration less from the doctrinal authority of the Catholic Church (which she frequently criticised as rigid and conservative) than from her own universalist interpretation of Christianity, which she regarded as a source of moral principles applicable to all humanity. Well beyond a personal creed, Ward’s Catholicism reflects a fundamental aspect of her political thought.
