小中大切換為無襯線

Chasing la dolce vita

By upholding and publicising the symbols of Italy – fashion, art, food, football – the diaspora has helped turn them into a recognisable shorthand for the country abroad. Visiting London’s Italian restaurants with my family, I’m struck by how many still have straw-clad Chianti bottles on the tables, and a packet of grissini tucked next to the plates, not to mention waiters who make a show of noisily grinding pepper onto our food. The performative nature of this overseas Italianness, its theatricality, is reinforced by walls decorated with photos of the owners posing with Italian American celebrities like Al Pacino, Robert De Niro or Sylvester Stallone. It is as if we’ve all been given roles, customers and waiters alike, in a Pirandello play that challenges distinctions between illusion and reality.
