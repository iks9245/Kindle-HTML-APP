Hospitals deliberately targeted by Sudan's RSF fighters, BBC investigation finds

Doctors told the BBC they believed RSF fighters deliberately targeted medical facilities because army or allied fighters were among their patients.

The onslaught of shelling and bombing culminated on 25 October 2025 with the RSF storming the hospital amid allegations of a massacre that killed more than 460 patients and their companions.

The RSF has denied a massacre occurred, although the WHO condemned the reported killings and satellite imagery appeared to show clusters of bodies on the blood-soaked grounds of the hospital.

The BBC shared details of its investigation with the UN human rights chief, who said our findings were "deeply disturbing", and consistent with the analysis of his office.

"Our monitoring and documentation shows the RSF repeatedly struck el-Fasher's main hospitals and makeshift health facilities, destroying or seriously damaging them and severely impacting access to health services for thousands of civilians trapped in the city," Türk said.
