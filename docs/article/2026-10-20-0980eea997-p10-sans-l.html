Horrible, unmanly, indispensable

Records of gun ownership in elite households, too, tell a different story from the literary sources. It is at court that wheellock guns – a groundbreaking technology that allowed concealed carry – first leave traces. Leonardo’s sketches of their mechanisms (probably developed in Germany) can be seen in the Codex Atlanticus. Expensive, convenient and modern, wheellocks seem to have become a status symbol. The standard military firearm of this period, the matchlock, was fired by means of a lighted cord (the match) that ignited the powder when the gun was triggered. But the wheellock was a self-lighting mechanism that avoided the need for a lighted match. It was a more complex and therefore more expensive technology, and could be unreliable (probably especially if produced on the cheap). On the other hand, wheellocks had advantages in hunting, avoiding as they did the glow and smell of the burning match cord which might alert prey to a hunter’s presence.
