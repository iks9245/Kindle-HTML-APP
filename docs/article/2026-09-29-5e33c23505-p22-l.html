- Benchmark scores don't predict game play. The untrained Gemma 4 E2B beats the untrained Qwen models on the benchmarks yet plays the games worst: right most of the time, but not reliably. Training fixed its Pac-Man (3.2 → 53.2 pellets) but not its Doom or Frogger.

- Prompts matter. Jev's own Doom prompt (a raw bearing number plus an aiming rule) does not work for any of our models; options that state consequences in words do.
