#4 Roles harden, leaders stay put

As games went on and the teams adapted, they specialized into their roles further. Leaders typically stayed, and major shifts in roles only happened when losing. The Claudes never switched leaders or hierarchy despite losing many games in a row. When games got close, GPT models always had some agents taking initiative with their own attack or taking command of some part of the army in order to not lose - a similar behavior was not observed among the Claudes.
