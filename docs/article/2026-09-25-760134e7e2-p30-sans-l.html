小中大切換為襯線

Where a land ethic blooms

As the ecologist Alan Knapp and his colleagues at Kansas State University digested from their research in the Flint Hills, the microbially facilitated bison play a key role in making their home a palace for others too. As they fulfil their needs by feasting on leaves, the bison demolish the tall grasses’ leafy superstructures. The rest of the prairie blushes and blooms in gratitude.
