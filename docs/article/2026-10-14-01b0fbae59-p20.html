Switch the key to hear the same chords higher or lower, or in a minor key. Put a capo on in Settings and the same shapes sound higher, which is how guitarists play in keys like A or B with easy open chords. Drop D and Open G show how a tuning changes the whole sound.

Low E string on the left. Numbers are fingers, O is an open string, X is a string you don't play.
