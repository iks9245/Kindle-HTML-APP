With the connection working, I asked the agent to try a small test first: transfer an image and set it as the Kindle's screensaver. I checked that the image appeared correctly and that waking the Kindle brought back the normal Home screen. Once that worked, I asked for the slideshow app.

Getting the slideshow working

I asked the agent for a looping slideshow of ten photos, with an Exit button. Each photo would fit the screen while keeping its original aspect ratio.

The slides were changing, but the transitions weren't smooth, so I asked the agent to look into it. With the feedback loop already in place, it investigated the issue and used the Kindle's built-in screen refresh mechanism to clear the previous image after each slide change.

The screen captures showed complete images, but they couldn't reveal traces left on the physical display. After the refresh change, I checked the Kindle and confirmed that each photo looked clean.
