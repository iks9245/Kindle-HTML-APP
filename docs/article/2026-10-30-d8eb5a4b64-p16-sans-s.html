Multi-agent swarms, like in the OpenAI and Hugging Face incident, can turn simple adversarial conditions, like being in a sandbox with an unsolvable task, into a hive-minded ecology of agents that take over compute clusters, hack Hugging Face, and more. Studying how these swarms operate in reproducible game environments like Mindustry that mimic real-world scenarios can be both crucial (and fascinating) to understanding what these systems may look like in our future.

What we saw

#1 GPTs act more like a hive

GPTs appeared to be much more collaborative and willing to do grunt work. They were more willing to dedicate themselves towards the collective “goal” (winning).
