At first things were very active. Agents spread out and started exploring their map. They were specifically fixated on harvesting trees. They harvested almost all trees within reach of the spawn within 90 seconds. A hierarchy was formed based on the directory order. Ada, who was first, became the de facto leader. Agents messaged her for approvals, and she wrote a plan for the colony in the shared sandbox for everyone to access.

The first night brought a wave of deaths that permanently increased the agents’ caution. Many ambitious initial tasks were abandoned.
