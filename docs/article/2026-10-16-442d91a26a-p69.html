KIERS: Yeah, well, I think like a decade ago creating a blueprint of Earth’s living underground fungal networks would’ve seemed impossible, right? I think like historically, we understand the patterns of forests across the Earth. We understand ocean patterns. I mean, really our tools have been confined to what’s happening at the Earth’s surface.

But now we’ve got these amazing emerging techniques in remote sensing, in machine learning, in fungal sequencing, and in robotics that we can actually start to understand what’s happening underground. And by doing that, we’re learning how fungi can be leveraged to restore degraded lands, to increase agricultural sustainability, to draw down carbon.
