So I went through the references in the Nature paper and looked up topics I might not have given a second thought to before. The one that captivated me was muon-catalyzed fusion. I recognized it as the original meaning of the term “cold fusion” before it was co-opted by the 1989 studies. As I unpacked what it meant, I discovered a lively research program within physics that creates exotic breeds of atoms and molecules, sometimes to try to fuse them together, but more broadly to search for new fundamental laws of nature.
