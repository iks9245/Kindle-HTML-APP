Remember when you used to encounter swarms of insects around outdoor electric lights? Their well known attraction to outdoor lights is used by entomologists and moth lovers to conduct insect surveys. All you need is a light and a white sheet to see who’s flying at night.

Nowadays, if you want to attract insects, you have to be picky about the light you use. As everyone changes over to LED lights, we see the color difference but we can’t see the crucial difference in insect attraction between incandescent and LED.

Our guide Roland Rumm on the WINGS Veracruz trip explained what happens with insects in the switch to LED lights. His family has an outdoor dining space that is well lit with artificial light. As a child he loved to watch the geckos climb to the lights and eat the insects. (This gecko can change to a bland gray color when he needs to.)
