小中大切換為無襯線

Palestinians and US journalist injured by Israeli settlers in West Bank harvest attack

Yaser AlKam, a council member from the neighbouring village of Turmus Aya told me Majdi - an American citizen - was attacked by settlers with nail-studded sticks, and needed treatment for deep wounds on his head. His elderly mother was also injured.

Another man ferrying the injured to hospital told us that settlers had attacked them with sticks while they harvested their olives, and had burned three cars – including one of his own.

"As soon as we got to the land and put our things on the ground, they started to come, so we ran away" said a woman next to him.

Asked about the violence, a US State Department spokesperson said they were aware of the reports.
