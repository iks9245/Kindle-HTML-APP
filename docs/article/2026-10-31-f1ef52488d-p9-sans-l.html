You can see one method it used to make this map by holding your thumb in front of your face and closing your left eye, then your right. If you switch back and forth, you’ll notice that your thumb seems to move relative to whatever is behind it. The closer your thumb is to your face, the more your view of it will change. This phenomenon is called parallax.

Gaia observed almost 2 billion stars from different angles as the Earth moved from one side of the sun to the other during its yearly orbit. Studying the effects of parallax allowed researchers to determine the stars’ distances from Earth.
