- 2026 is already Florida's busiest year on file2,816 Florida court records have been filed so far in 2026, past the 2,549 of 2010, the previous record; 2025 had 2,476. 92 Florida communities have 50 or more court records each, more than any other state. Maryland is next with 73.

These are counts of records on file, not rates. Florida has 104,659 indexed associations — more than any other state — and its county courts publish deep case histories, so it counts more. Per hundred communities, Florida has 47 court records; Maryland has 603 and Texas has 231. A state whose courts hide older cases ranks lower whatever its associations do.

What a buyer, an owner or a legislator can do

- Before buying, read the association's record. Search it on HOA Spy: every lien, lis pendens and court case on file, each linked to the official record, and its corporate standing. A community that forecloses as a matter of routine, or whose corporation has lapsed, is a fact to price in before the inspection period ends.
