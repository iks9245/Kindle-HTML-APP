The Department of Water, Energy, and Environment Data Center Task Force is overseeing the execution of Governor Jim Pillen’s July 20th Executive Order requiring data centers in the state to self-report their impact on Nebraska’s resources; primarily water, power grids and local infrastructure.

Usage reports are due by Sept. 30. Submitted reports can be read here, simply enter “DCR” into the “DEQ Program” field.
