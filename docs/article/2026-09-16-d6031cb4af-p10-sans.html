On a visit to Emilia-Romagna some years ago, I met old people who recalled widowed Marco’s long stays every summer. Dressed in British suits, all the more impeccable to hide his suffering, he would declare that he was finally home. Then he would leave for London again, returning the summer following, then again setting off, over and over. Just like us, his descendants.
