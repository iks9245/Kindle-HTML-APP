The GPTs (GPT-6.1 Sol in Codex) won 6-1 against the Claudes (Claude Opus 5.5 in Claude Code) in the 7 games. The series ran for about 9.5 hours, with game 3 being the most intense and closest at 4 hours 16 minutes. Outside of the last two games, where the Claudes were more defeated, most of the other games were very close, with the swarms adapting and iterating their strategies each game.

The setup

Mindustry is an open source game by Anuken. Full credit to him for the amazing game and letting it be open source for cool experiments like this!

It’s a factory-building game with tower defense and real-time strategy: you mine resources, build conveyor supply chains and factories, and turn that economy into turrets and an army. In its PvP mode, which we used, two teams race to build the stronger economy and military and destroy the other team’s core.
