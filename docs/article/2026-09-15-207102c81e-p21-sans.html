小中大切換為襯線

The bombarding of childhood

The real distinction is whether a medium leaves room for the child’s own attention or continually redirects it. When every pause is filled, every uncertainty immediately resolved and every wandering thought pulled back by a brighter cue, waiting and reflection can begin to feel intolerable. The child learns to reach for the next stimulus before the previous one has had time to settle. To give space for a child’s attention to drift inward, to reflect, is therefore to leave room for the child’s inner life. Slow media changes the relation between screen and child by saying: you may arrive at your own pace.
