This wasn’t the first time ChatGPT had reproduced Loper’s signature. In May, a stranger reached out over Instagram to warn Loper that his pen name had appeared in an image output by the chatbot. Over the summer, Loper found additional examples in a Reddit forum about ChatGPT, where users had been posting similar New Yorker-style cartoons they had generated.

Loper already knew his work was “out there, getting harvested for all of this [AI] training,” he said. But an image generator faking his signature felt more personal. “I’m not a territorial person, but my name is my name. It felt very much like a violation of my personhood.”
