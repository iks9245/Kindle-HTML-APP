小中大切換為無襯線

Chasing la dolce vita

Despite endorsing the country as the paragon of human advancement, my father routinely turned down job offers at Italian universities and research centres, choosing instead to work in France. The resulting tension, played out in frequently announced but endlessly postponed homecomings, threw us into ever more convoluted cycles of l’eterno ritorno that had already tormented my family for two generations with their toing and froing between the peninsula and Britain. Now, with a family of my own, and living and working in London, I’m aware of how I’m resuscitating the dilemmas my parents and grandparents faced.
