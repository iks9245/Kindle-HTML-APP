I also expanded the collection of operating system CD-ROMs with items like the Rhapsody and Mac OS X developer previews. While they’re aspirational for now (DingusPPC can’t run them yet), they do satisfy my completionist tendencies. These CDs are mirrored to and served from my own Cloudflare R2 bucket – the Internet Archive is an incredible resource (you can donate to them), but streaming from it has been increasingly flaky. I don’t want to add to the problem, and thanks to my sponsors, I can afford to serve a few gigabytes.
