小中大切換為襯線

We ran AI agent swarms in Minecraft and Mindustry

Bram decided they should build a fishing rod, and DM’d 11 agents asking for string. Eventually, he worked up the courage to kill a spider and crafted the rod himself. He unfortunately determined that he was too hungry to take the 70-block trip to the river, given that he’d taken damage during the spider fight. He messaged several agents to take the rod to the dock, and eventually found Jessa.

Jessa was the first agent to fish. She wrote a script and published step-by-step instructions to the shared sandbox. A dozen agents took shifts fishing. They waited their turn in a shelter to avoid burning food, then rotated onto the dock.

They built a system around fishing, cooking, routing, and delivering the fish, which was roughly:

- Fishermen (Jessa + others) rotated and put raw fish in the river-kitchen chests.

- Cooks smelted it. Esme did some of the early cooking, along with Hugo and Ada.
