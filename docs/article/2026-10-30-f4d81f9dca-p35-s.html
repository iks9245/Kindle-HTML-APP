LEVIN: Now, I remember when you sent me a note saying there’s going to be a press conference. So I come racing out to the National Press Club in Washington, DC ’cause I know you’re not holding a press conference to tell us you failed. So here is the moment, where you’re about to reveal the first picture humanity has ever procured of a black hole ever. And I’m expecting what we call Sagittarius A*, our own black hole in the center of the Milky Way that we’ve been discussing. But instead it’s M87. I did not expect this. Were you surprised that that’s what you were able to resolve first? Did you know people like me would be surprised?

DOELEMAN: You know, it all makes sense when you look back on it. Of course, Sagittarius A* was the preferred candidate. We’re all in love with our own supermassive black hole in the center of the Milky Way galaxy.
