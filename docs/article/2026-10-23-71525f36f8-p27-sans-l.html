As a society, we are still largely in denial about the scale of the existential threats facing us, and against which individualism cannot keep us safe. In times of deep ecological, technological and political crisis, we may be forced to remember how to be each other’s safety. To unpick, laboriously, the conditioning of the weird, prosperous West and call bullshit on the lie that control, accumulation and a frictionless, precision-optimised life was ever going to make us happy. Though I know it’s a lie, a lot of me still wants it. However, as I learn every day how deeply the script of individualism has formed me, I also see how poorly it serves us.
