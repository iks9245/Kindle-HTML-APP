How Virus-like ‘Jumping Genes’ Became Our Partners in Evolution

The same features that allow DNA transposons and retrotransposons to move within a genome also predispose them to moving between genomes. By hitchhiking on a passing virus, transposons can defy species boundaries and land in a totally new evolutionary setting. “Virtually all of the [transposon types] we know can apparently go from species to species,” said Cedric Feschotte, a geneticist at Cornell University.
