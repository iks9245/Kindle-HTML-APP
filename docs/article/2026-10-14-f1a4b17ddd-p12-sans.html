Deskilling the body

First and foremost, life persists through physical plasticity. Consider how our bodies keep together. Living bodies do something unusual in the Universe: they perform work to preserve their own organisation. Life-forms use energy to remain discontinuous with the environment. In simple terms, organisms resist disintegrating into the background. While machines execute tasks, living beings pursue ends. Machines push energy around and produce things. They are, in this sense, allopoietic: they make something other than themselves. Organisms are autopoietic: they work to maintain and remake themselves. Living bodies take up and transform energy for this self-maintenance, and this is achieved by the body as a whole. Moment by moment, our bodies adapt to the environment and their current state in end-directed, physical transformations. It’s not so much that we think and therefore act but rather that we embody and therefore amend.
