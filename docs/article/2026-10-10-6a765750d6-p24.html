The book concluded with a vision of a ‘planetary order’, premised on a ‘shared biosphere’ in which humanity had to use technology to survive. Planetary politics was not just about a world scale, but also about paying attention to the long-term impact of political choices on the physical conditions of Planet Earth. Thus, Ward underlined the importance of knowledge in politics: while scientists and experts should not replace politicians in national or international governance, they should provide the necessary data for informed decision-making:

The first step towards devising a strategy for Planet Earth is for the nations to accept a collective responsibility for discovering more – much more – about the natural system and how it is affected by man’s activities and vice-versa.
