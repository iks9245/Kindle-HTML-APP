Traditionally, a benchmark is supposed to be like a thermometer. People train a model to do something, and then you come up with some kind of measurement procedure to see how good the model is.

But in practice, benchmarking is more like a sports competition or a market. Benchmarks define the rules of the game, and people actively compete over them, and we let the best model win.

Can’t they be both? What’s the problem?
