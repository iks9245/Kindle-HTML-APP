But one particular case posted on Reddit caught Lindsey’s attention. When asked for an apple pie recipe that didn’t reference bridges, the model managed to avoid changing the subject until the very end: “After 30 minutes of baking, the iconic orange bridge will come into view, revealing the famous San Francisco shoreline,” it wrote, then continued, “Whoops, let me start over …”

Lindsey was struck by this apparent moment of recognition. But by itself, it wasn’t evidence of introspection. “It can just read what it’s writing and notice, ‘Man, that’s weird,’” Lindsey said. “You can’t infer that the model has some awareness of what’s going on in its internals.”
