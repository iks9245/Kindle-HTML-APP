Moving Target

Malaria treatment has long been something of a hit-or-miss proposition. Without a granular view of how Plasmodium interacts with host cells, researchers had to test various compounds by trial and error. If a given drug seemed to fight the illness, they had little idea what part of the infection process it had disrupted. “All that work was done essentially blind,” Deitsch said.
