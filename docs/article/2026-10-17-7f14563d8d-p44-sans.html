- We can also use this regression objective to design short-term memories that a sequence model writes during the forward pass, like linear attention and self-attention. See my work on test-time regression. ↩︎

- For random keys, a ReLU reduces the interference by a factor of $\sqrt 2$. A random key’s similarity is equally likely to be positive or negative, so the ReLU zeroes about half of the weights and leaves the other half unchanged. That halves the interference’s variance, the sum of the squared weights, so the interference itself shrinks by a factor of $\sqrt{2}$. See Zhong et al. for a full derivation. ↩︎
