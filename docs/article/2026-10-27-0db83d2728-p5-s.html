In 2019, Guth, Wang, and Zhang(opens in a new window) proved the local smoothing conjecture in two spatial dimensions. In 2025, Wang and Zahl(opens in a new window) proved the Kakeya conjecture in three dimensions. Our models produced a proof of the local smoothing conjecture in every dimension and therefore also resolve the Kakeya conjecture and Stein’s restriction conjecture in all dimensions. The new proof is based on a novel auxiliary partial differential equation.

We obtained our AI output on October 18th, 2026, which was formalized in Lean the following day, and then we reached out to an expert to discuss forming a collaboration around this result. Following their recommendation, we decided to release the AI output publicly, following the same strategy for this result as our October 6th, 2026 release. We have released both an AI natural language output writeup(opens in a new window) produced by our models and a Lean proof (opens in a new window)in our public GitHub repository.
