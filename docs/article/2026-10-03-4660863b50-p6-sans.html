Life on a hair trigger

Violent crime has fallen steeply across the US since 2021, though not everywhere. According to Chicago’s crime dashboard, fatal shootings in Chicago were up 7 per cent this year as of late September. But even in cities where the decline has held, a citywide figure is a poor description of how violence is experienced. In US cities, most shootings concentrate in a few dozen blocks and among a few hundred people who account for less than 1 per cent of a city’s population. Thus, citywide numbers cannot capture what it is like to live inside that zone, where violence is not a discrete event but an experience that leaves stubborn traces on many lives.
