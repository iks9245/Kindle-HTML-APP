小中大切換為襯線

Where a land ethic blooms

The creatures of the soil also feed on the bodies of the gut microbes that helped ruminants digest their hay. The gut-borne microbes are the true herbivores in this system: the power of the sun is harnessed by plants and turned into grain or hay, which is gathered by the farmers and transported to the barn, then delivered to the habitat of the gut by a large mammal whose brain, mouth and muscles spend their days gathering food for its microbial tenants. However, the microbes are short-lived and quickly finish their shift at the top of the system. As they die, they pass through the threshold of hind gut and, finally, the anus. Shit.
