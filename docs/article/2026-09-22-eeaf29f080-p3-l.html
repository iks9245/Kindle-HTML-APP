Speaking at the news conference on Monday, Merz described the result in Mecklenburg-Vorpommern as "a heavy blow" for the centre-right CDU.

"This election is a profound turning point. It is a dramatic, unprecedented result for the CDU in Germany," he acknowledged.

Merz said there had been no discussion about his own position since the results and pushed back against the idea that his low popularity ratings alone were behind the election defeats.
