None of this is particular to places where violence is the problem. When my husband and I started having conversations about merging our finances, I would respond by shutting down and becoming frustrated. He studies financial decision-making for a living, and talking about money was second-nature to him. For me it was physically painful. My chest would tighten, and I would go quiet, get anxious or find a reason to end the conversation.
