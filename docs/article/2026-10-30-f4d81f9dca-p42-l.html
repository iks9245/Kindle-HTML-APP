DOELEMAN: Yeah. There’s a lot of different lines of evidence for this. Certainly for the galaxies that are radio loud and where you can do interferometry along the lines of what we did to take the picture of the black hole. There, you wind up seeing these jets of material, and those account for maybe 10% of the galaxies that we see. For the other galaxies, we see these cusps of stellar population that increase dramatically as you get to the center, and so you can postulate that there could be a supermassive black hole there.
