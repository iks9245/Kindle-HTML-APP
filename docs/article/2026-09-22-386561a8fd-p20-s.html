This is not relativism, the view that theories are mere social constructs. Quite the opposite: modern philosophy of science describes a process by which science converges on progressively better accounts of reality, not by marching from certainty to certainty but by eliminating worse hypotheses and refining better ones. The scientist, like the Academic Skeptic, never claims final truth, but it doesn’t follow that anything goes. Interestingly, the sociologist Robert K Merton in 1942 described science as a kind of ‘organised scepticism’.
