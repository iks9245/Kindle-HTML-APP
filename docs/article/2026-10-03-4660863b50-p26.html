A randomised controlled trial of READI estimated that participants were arrested for shootings and homicides 65 per cent less often than comparable men who were not offered the programme. Findings from a related programme point in the same direction. Becoming a Man, a school-based programme run in Chicago by the nonprofit organisation Youth Guidance, cut violent-crime arrests among high-school-age participants by nearly half. Our interviews complement these trials by showing what the changes these programmes brought felt like from the inside.

What seemed to be a threat from the man across the street might be coming from somewhere much older
