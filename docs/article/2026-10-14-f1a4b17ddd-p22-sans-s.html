Deskilling the body

We have engineered novel digital and commercial environments that can habituate harmful impulses in our bodies. As our bodies refit themselves in response to the unhealthy architectures we’ve engineered, we can find ourselves in a spiral of self-harm.

Recent work by the neuroscientist Kent Berridge and colleagues has shown how the brain systems that tell us we enjoy something and the systems that drive us to pursue this can drift apart. In other words, what we find ourselves craving and what we enjoy can end up at odds. When these systems become disrupted, the body’s learned patterns fall out of alignment with the satisfaction that once justified them.
