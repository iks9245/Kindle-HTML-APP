Hospitals deliberately targeted by Sudan's RSF fighters, BBC investigation finds

Dr Alaa Eldin Nugud, a prominent Sudanese surgeon who has joined the RSF-backed civilian government as health minister, insisted the South Hospital was being used as a military base.

According to international law, hospitals can lose their protection if they are used by a party to the conflict to commit an "act harmful to the enemy".

In an interview with the BBC, Dr Nugud declared that missile launchers were set up on the grounds and shared a video showing remains of weapons filmed more than a year after the hospital closed, which he said supported his claim.

He also showed us what he called maps of landmines planted around the Teaching Hospital, which is located next to Sudanese military headquarters.
