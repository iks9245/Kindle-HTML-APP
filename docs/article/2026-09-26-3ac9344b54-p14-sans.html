I’ll never know for sure if he was being generous or if I had shown him something he had not yet thought of in applying his own distinction. What stayed with me wasn’t the answer but the manner of it: a philosopher in his 70s, decades into a settled framework, still willing to test that framework against a case he hadn’t considered, in real time, out loud, in front of a student.
