Stacy McGaugh, an astronomer at Case Western Reserve University and a longtime proponent of modified-gravity theories, is more skeptical. “It’s not clear to me what the formation mechanism is,” he said. An ancient collision “is a plausible origin story, albeit an exceptional one. If that’s what’s happened, then the rest follows.” In other words: If these galaxies were created when their dark matter was stolen away, then it makes sense that dark matter is a physical substance.
