In praise of the dilettante

From our Germans, we glean a sense of where the dilettante goes wrong. But they can also help us make the corrective mental switch from seeing the dilettante as a wannabe practitioner to someone who chooses a more experimental, exploratory orientation toward a given practice.

The key to perceiving dilettantism as abundance instead of deficiency is to appreciate the sense of freedom it affords. It is, in the first place, a negative freedom, a freedom from the constraints that otherwise bind professional practitioners. More precisely, it is a freedom of mobility within a practice.
