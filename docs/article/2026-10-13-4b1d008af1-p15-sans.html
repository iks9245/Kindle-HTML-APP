Is ‘Attention’ the Essence of Intelligence?

A neural network that has learned enough about language to translate sentences from English into French can also complete sentences in either language. Predicting the next word of a translation is, in other words, a subtask of the more general task of “predicting the next word,” period. And that’s what large language models do. They’re trained on the contents of the entire internet, learning which words should attend to which other words in all sorts of contexts. They process and reprocess the whole dataset and try trillions of times to predict the next word in whatever part of the web they happen to be in at any point during training. The connections between neurons in these models are gradually tweaked in a way that produces patterns of attention between words that are ever more useful for predicting the next word in any context — or for producing an appropriate response to a prompt from a human.
