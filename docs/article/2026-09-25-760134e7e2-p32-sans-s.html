小中大切換為襯線

Where a land ethic blooms

Nowhere in the frameworks of sustainability have I come across love for our neighbours and the land as a guiding principle, yet love is at the heart of conservation, good farming and strong communities. We do not know what the future will bring, but resilience to coming shocks cannot be secured through better technology, greater efficiency or improved communications infrastructure alone. We need all of these advances, but conservation asks even more of us. Leopold’s words resonated with this call when he summarised his goals for an ecological education:

If the individual has a warm personal understanding of land, he will perceive of his own accord that it is something more than a breadbasket. He will see land as a community of which he is only a member, albeit now the dominant one. He will see the beauty, as well as the utility, of the whole, and know the two cannot be separated. We love (and make intelligent use of) what we have learned to understand.
