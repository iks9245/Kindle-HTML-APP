The attempts with Fable 5.1 and Opus 5.5 were both blocked by Anthropic's cybersecurity safeguards after two prompts. Then I tried GPT-6 Astra. I explicitly asked it to build the custom software we needed for personal use and take inspiration from existing open-source projects.

We adapted the existing jailbreak from SpiderCat, released by sparklerfish and the KindleModding community, and built custom tools called KindleCTL around it.

- What we built: Tools to send commands from my Mac, transfer files, and investigate errors on the Kindle.
