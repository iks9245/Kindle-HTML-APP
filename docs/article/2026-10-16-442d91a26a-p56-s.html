STROGATZ: I’m getting the feeling listening to you that sort of like when people began to explore the oceans wondering what’s there. You know, it’s just a very simple kind of question. We don’t even know what’s out there. It feels like that’s what’s animating some of this, that there’s a vast unexplored terra incognita that happens to be subterranean.

KIERS: Not only is that animating it, but fungi are the most charismatic and fun-to-work-with organisms. So there’s this real passion of mycologists all over the world that have been so underfunded and so marginalized in many ways because it wasn’t even until sort of the 1970s, late ‘60s I think, that fungi were recognized as their own kingdom. I mean, before they were just placed in with plants. And so it’s really shining a light on the underdog of biological diversity.
