There are very few models for what we are doing. Perhaps the most robust is the monastic tradition. Most orders of religious Sisters or Brothers see the proximity of living up close with each other as essential to spiritual growth. It is precisely in the discomfort of being witnessed, up close, at our worst, in being asked to compromise preferences, to share and serve others, they argue, that we really grow up.

Living with others has shown me things in myself that I would prefer to ignore
