Audio reveals exchanges between Spain train operators and drivers moments before deadly crash

- Published

Leaked audio recordings that were published in Spanish media have revealed conversations between train drivers and a control centre just minutes before a deadly crash on Wednesday morning, that has killed 69 people.

Officials are investigating the cause of the disaster, which occurred when a train from Madrid to Seville barrelled into a stationary train near Urda.
