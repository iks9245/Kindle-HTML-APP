I became a citizen of Japan.

Let me share about it. Some is my anecdotal experience, some my opinion, and some facts. Parsing which of these is what in each sentence is not going to happen.

EDIT: I have seen some online reaction to this, and appreciate your kind comments. If you are thinking about applying to naturalize, I encourage you to go ahead and consult at your local Legal Affairs Bureau. Act according to what they tell you there, not stuff you hear or read on the internet in places such as this random gringo’s blog. The list of required documents below is intimidating, but your case won’t be the same as anyone else’s. They treat every application individually, so the horse’s mouth is that of the Ministry of Justice, not me.
