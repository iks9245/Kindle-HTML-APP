小中大切換為襯線

Chasing la dolce vita

I’ve hunted for echoes of these idyllic days in Italian literature and art, and added them to the building blocks of my emigrant longing. Reading Italo Calvino’s novel The Baron in the Trees (1957), I picture the specific branches and trunks I climbed in childhood. I smell and feel the remembered coolness of stone floors and shaded high-ceilinged rooms when immersed in Lampedusa’s novel The Leopard (1958). An Andrea Mantegna painting, like The Agony in the Garden (1455-56), sends me right back to horizons dotted with hilltop villages and fortified towers of crumbling stone. I’m conscious of the pull of Italy whenever I watch its films – and the irrational reverence this triggers. Recently, I found Alice Rohrwacher’s film La chimera (2023) almost too poignant to watch as it teemed with the details of Italian life.
