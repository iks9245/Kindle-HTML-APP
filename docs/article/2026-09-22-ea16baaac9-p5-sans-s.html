For now, the White House media slot has remained vacant and instead the conservative TV outlet, Real America's Voice, was listed as "secondary" crew on Trump's trip to New York.

Several US outlets also announced that they would not participate in coverage of pooled events, with some saying they would not share still images from Trump events either.

Later on Monday, Trump met with New York Mayor Zohran Mamdani for an hour-long discussion. Afterwards, the two held a news conference, which was covered by New York media.

"I told the president that we're going to have all of the members of the press here on the lawn, and that is something that I believe in - is everyone, no matter the opinion or the critique, that we engage," Mamdani said.

Trump continued his criticism of the media, saying that journalists always say that they will boycott, but they "never boycott" him.
