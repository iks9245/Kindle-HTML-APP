小中大切換為襯線

Love with nowhere to go

The same happened to me: when the magical stranger disappeared, my loss was ambiguous – she still existed, we were never officially together, I had another partner – and my grief went into hiding. In a culture where the end of a relationship is ‘just a breakup’ and monogamy is the unspoken rule, I lacked the rituals to digest the grief and the hermeneutical resources to conceptualise and communicate what had happened to me. For ‘self-interpreting animals’ like us, in the words of the philosopher Charles Taylor, the result is that we end up in the dark, as strangers to ourselves.
