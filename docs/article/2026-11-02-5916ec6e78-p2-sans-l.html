The Ukrainian Central Intelligence Agency said there had been a failure in the app, and urged people to use other sources of information to clarify the situation on the threat of air strikes.

The alerts, in Ukrainian, started at approximately 22:47 (20:47 GMT), with the first reading: "Radiation threat. Radiation threat declared in Kyiv."

The next message, written in Russian, came at 22:48, which read: "Hi! There will be a nuclear alert across Ukraine. Don't worry, it's Oreshnik flying".
