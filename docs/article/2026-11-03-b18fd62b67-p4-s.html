So how did a nice philosopher like Price get mixed up in a business like this? Over Zoom, he explained that his interest dates to 2011, when he and other luminaries were hatching the idea for the Center for the Study of Existential Risk. This research group frets over nuclear winter, engineered bioweapons, massive volcanic eruptions, and artificial intelligence takeover — it rang that alarm bell long before it became fashionable. Price told me he saw parallels between doomsday scenarios and cold fusion. They may seem improbable, and studying them is a lonely or even fringe activity, yet the consequences are huge, so we should give more weight to them than might strictly seem warranted.
