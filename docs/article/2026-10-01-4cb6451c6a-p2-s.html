US Supreme Court allows execution of Christa Pike to go ahead

Supreme Court Justice Brett Kavanaugh, a conservative, granted Wednesday evening's application to vacate the stay of the execution. No explanation was provided in the order.

The top court's three liberal justices - Sonia Sotomayor, Elena Kagan and Ketanji Brown Jackson - dissented.

Sotomayor wrote that the decision cleared the way for Tennessee to execute Pike before her pending legal challenges to her conviction "can be properly considered".

"Tennessee's desire to expedite Pike's execution by a few days or even weeks cannot outweigh her 'fundamental interest in [her] own life," she said.

The latest Supreme Court ruling landed just before 18:00 local time. The state's execution order is due to expire at midnight.

If time runs out, the Tennessee Supreme Court would need to set a new execution date, the Associated Press news agency reports.
