When I started medical school in the 1990s, I found cancer fascinating and terrifying exactly because of the betrayal it represented. Most human disease represents failure of one body system or another. Whether due to trauma, infection, degradation of blood supply, mechanical wear-and-tear or other causes, one or more organs stop performing optimally. In such cases, our job in medicine is simple in principle: support the failing system, prevent further damage, and repair or replace missing function as much as possible.
