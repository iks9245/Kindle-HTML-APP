After toiling on this work for two years, Glass and Smith concluded that psychotherapy had a beneficial effect, and Glass presented the method at the San Francisco hotel where the education meeting was taking place. Debate continues to this day about whether psychotherapy works, when and for whom. (Eysenck called the work ‘an exercise in mega-silliness’.) But of the meta-analysis, the audience was thunderstruck. Here was a way to extract meaning from an apparent mess of different results.
