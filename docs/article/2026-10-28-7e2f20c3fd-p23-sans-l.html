Love with nowhere to go

Your grief tells you that you had the courage to love, that you allowed someone or something else to enter ‘the core of [your] being and find a home in [your] heart’. If you deny someone the right to grieve, you deny them access to what they loved and valued, which is to say that you deny them access to themselves. You don’t just rob them, then, of their love; you also rob them of self-knowledge.
