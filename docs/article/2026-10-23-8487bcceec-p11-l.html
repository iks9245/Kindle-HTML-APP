小中大切換為無襯線

Newborn twins fight for survival as conflict fuels Yemen's hunger crisis

Many parents - like Bajash - are trying to outrun both war, and poverty. He is now a shepherd without a flock. When he fled, he had to leave 10 sheep behind. They were his only livelihood.

As doctors monitored his twins, he cared for his three older children - all aged under five. They lingered on a bench in hospital reception without toys, bedding or baggage of any kind. But they could not sleep there.

So, they spent the night outside, on the street - one more family with war wounds that cannot be seen but will disfigure a generation.
