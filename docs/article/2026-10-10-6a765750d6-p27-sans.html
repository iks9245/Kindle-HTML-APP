Barbara Ward’s vision

To fully appreciate Ward’s contribution, it is important to acknowledge the limits of her vision. Her viewpoint was inherently Western-centric and elitist, and her earlier writings frequently explained imperial expansion as an expression of Europe’s civilising mission. She advanced a civilisational hierarchy that viewed Christianity as the ethical basis of human unity, undermining alternative creeds. Areas of the world that she dubbed ‘undeveloped’ were assigned only a passive role in her planetary community. When the ‘Global South’ sought to shape its own political and economic future through initiatives such as the New International Economic Order, she insisted that the West still had a moral responsibility to lead the transition to global justice and equality.
