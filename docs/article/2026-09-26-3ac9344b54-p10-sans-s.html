He did not give up everything. He still wanted to keep the public sphere as something to measure democracies against, even if the history behind it was messier than he’d first made it look. The criticism and his answer to it sit in the same book, printed one after the other. Habermas’s views were open to being challenged, and open for us all to witness his thought in process.
