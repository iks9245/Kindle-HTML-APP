Is AI the End of Math As We Know It?

AI models skip to the end. At the moment, their proofs are nearly impossible to read. They elide salient details and waste pages on irrelevant concepts. They fail to demonstrate how the new work builds on or connects to other results in the mathematical literature. Mathematicians know that the proofs are true — they’re verified by a system that checks their logic — but have no idea why. What’s missing is understanding, the currency that mathematicians trade in. If there is a journey, humans are not invited along for the ride.
