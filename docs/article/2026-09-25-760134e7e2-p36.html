小中大切換為無襯線

Where a land ethic blooms

A diverse rural landscape, shaped by goals of complexity and resilience, rather than maximum production and minimal accounting, will be invaluable to our communities in the coming years. The landscape of diverse farming systems that rely much less on fossil fuels for energy and fertility is much more likely to remain productive despite shocks such as intense rains, flooding, drought, rising fuel and fertiliser costs, declining soil structure and fertility, and limited access to credit and financial capital. Such shocks are predicted to become more frequent and more severe because of climate change, continued population growth, increasing demand for resources, and economic and social inequality.
