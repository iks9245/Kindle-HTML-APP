Micron (MU), Sandisk (SNDK), Seagate (STX), and Western Digital (WDC) — the memory and storage names that led the chip boom this year — all got clobbered, falling roughly 6% to 8%. Marvell (MRVL) fell even harder, a drop that came as it agreed to buy Lumentum (LITE) in a $115 billion deal. Nvidia (NVDA) held up better than most, but it's still down about 16% since Oct. 6.

Zoom out and the mess gets bigger. Chip and memory stocks have lost about $3.7 trillion in market value since Oct. 6, by Yahoo Finance calculations. Nvidia lost the most in dollars, but Intel's drop of nearly half is the steepest among the giants.
