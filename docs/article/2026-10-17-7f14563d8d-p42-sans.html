- Nonparametric memory stores that grow by appending memories outside the weights: kNN-LM, RETRO, Memorizing Transformers, and growing nonparametric networks.

- Finding and editing the memories inside trained models: Geva et al., Bietti et al., and ROME and MEMIT.

- Compressing short-term memory, or turning it into long-term memory: KVzip, gist tokens, Cartridges, and attention matching

- Continual learning methods like sparse memory finetuning

I’ve always been drawn to memory, maybe because I viscerally feel how limited my own is. Perhaps part of me hopes that by understanding how machines remember, I’ll learn something about myself too.
