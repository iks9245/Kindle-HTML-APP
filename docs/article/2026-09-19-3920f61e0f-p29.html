On the other hand, from outside the event horizon, we are unable to access the states of the black hole. We never have the opportunity to zoom in and retrace its history like we did with the room. Instead, all we see is the bigger picture, with the details shrouded behind the event horizon. The result of countless microscopic states clustered together to give a few numbers, like the temperature of the room, or the smell of perfume. Except, for the black hole, these are its mass and spin.
