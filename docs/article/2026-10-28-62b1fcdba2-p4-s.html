Set it up in three steps

Setup takes 20 minutes to about 2½ hours, depending on your computer and your internet speed. You only click for about 5 minutes; the rest is downloading.

- Download Survival LLM for Mac or Windows and unzip it. On Windows, right-click the zip and choose Extract All.

- Open it. On a Mac, double-click Survival LLM. On Windows, open the extracted folder and double-click Survival LLM.bat.

- Follow the setup. The app opens in its own window and downloads the AI engine (built into the Mac app), the AI model that fits your computer, and the library. Let it finish while you're online.

Mac: click Done, open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to "Survival LLM was blocked", click Open Anyway and confirm with your password. You only do this once.

Windows: click More info, then Run anyway.
