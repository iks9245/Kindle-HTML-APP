Cosmic amnesia

It seems counterintuitive that something so chaotically created can become so simple. Why do black holes seem to forget their violent histories? These strange objects, scattered throughout the Universe, are unlike anything else we know, yet their forgetfulness does not belong to them alone. Just like their ringing, the underlying physics also governs our world. The same ideas connect black holes and our own journey through time, washing away our histories and resulting in loss. The physics is called entropy. And its central lesson is that, when we zoom out, it is not just black holes that forget, but the world around us too.
