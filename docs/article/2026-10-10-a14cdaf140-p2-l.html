Today we’re introducing Microsoft-Decision-1, our new model for fast decision-scoring, available in Microsoft Foundry and through OpenRouter. This model is designed for routing, classification, prioritization, verification, and workflow control, making it easier to incorporate decision intelligence into existing applications, agents, and workflows in a secure, trusted environment. Microsoft-Decision-1 delivers top performance in latency and quality on structured decision tasks to outperform both LLMs and other decision models.
