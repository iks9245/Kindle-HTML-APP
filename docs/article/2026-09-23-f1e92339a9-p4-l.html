Narsarsuaq is a former civilian airport and World War Two base. It has deep-water access and a large runway which US military engineers were recently surveying, the US North Command confirmed to the BBC.

The Mestersvig outpost is remote, far less developed, and used by a Danish naval dog sled unit.

The "modalities and technical details" of the bases are yet to be confirmed, and according to the deal, must be mutually agreed by all parties,

This means the US security arrangements are not unfettered and Greenland and Denmark's approval is still needed.
