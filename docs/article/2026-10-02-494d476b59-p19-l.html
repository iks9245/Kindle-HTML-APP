Participating in independent learning communities, using open technologies based on ethical principles, and building a shared and inclusive digital infrastructure is an act of self-empowerment and emancipation.

Humans need to have the resources to learn, research and teach web publishing skills. It is humans who do all the essential knowledge and education work, and they need to be paid for it.

This sounds trite, but GenAI advocates do not acknowledge it. They let Big Tech get away with appropriating the work of web educators. We cannot make concessions here.
