How Voting Is Like a Klein Bottle

Before anyone reaches for a tinfoil hat, let’s be clear: Every ballot is counted correctly. The question is what should count as winning.

One solution is to ask voters to rank the candidates, then choose whoever would defeat every rival in a head-to-head contest. But consider the following scenario:

- One-third of voters rank Alice over Bob over Carol.

- One-third rank Bob over Carol over Alice.

- One-third rank Carol over Alice over Bob.

Now compare the candidates in pairs:

- Alice beats Bob, because the first and third groups prefer Alice to Bob.

- Bob beats Carol, because the first and second groups prefer Bob to Carol.

- Carol beats Alice, because the second and third groups prefer Carol to Alice.
