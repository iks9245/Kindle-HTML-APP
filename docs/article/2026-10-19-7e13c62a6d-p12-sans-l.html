The county is the community's own, from its registration or ZIP. Court records are counted only in the courts HOA Spy indexes and liens only in the recorder indexes it collects, so a county whose portal is not read shows few or none.

Florida against the nation

Florida associations bring more foreclosure suits against their members than those of any other state on HOA Spy. 10,296 of the 14,168 on file (73%); Texas is next with 2,349.
