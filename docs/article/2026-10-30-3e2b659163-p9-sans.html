Hundreds of personnel have been deployed to the disaster site to assist with recovery efforts, Spain's interior ministry said, with drones and helicopters being used to find victims and remove rubble.

A spokesperson for the Spanish Civil Guard told the BBC they had deployed more than 900 people from different specialist departments, and the priority now was identifying the deceased.

They said they did not know how long this would take, but urged those with missing family members to go to one of the local centres which have been set up to offer DNA samples.
