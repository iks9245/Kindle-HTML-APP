I have provisionally divided the feuilleton into 16 seasons, from childhood to a final season about my autobiographical work and my second set of children. I do not write and publish them strictly chronologically, but in the order that satisfies my curiosity and nostalgic cravings. (Though once I have started a season, I see it through chronologically in its entirety before moving on to the next.)

I have no master plan telling me in advance which parts of my life will matter
