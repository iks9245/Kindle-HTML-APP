This perspective may also help reconcile two positions within the mathematical community regarding AI. Some are comfortable with or even enthusiastic about using AI, but wish to develop appropriate processes and principles for its use. Others prefer not to use AI at all. Both positions have their merits. Egoless mathematics offers a way of reconciling them by introducing a higher consideration: both approaches can reflect the same underlying commitment to mathematical truth, regardless of how we choose to pursue it. Our aim here is not to settle particular questions of policy, but to develop a higher-level perspective that can guide our deliberations and ultimately our actions.
