And why some are radio loud, why some emit in the radio waves, why some don’t probably has a lot to do with the direction that you’re looking at them. It also has to do with the evolutionary moment in the lifespan of that galaxy. Some radio loud at some times and some not at other times. So we’re seeing the great variety, like the zoo of galaxies across cosmic time, and we’re fortunate that some of them really exhibit these tremendously illuminating radio structures that we can image with an Earth-sized telescope.
