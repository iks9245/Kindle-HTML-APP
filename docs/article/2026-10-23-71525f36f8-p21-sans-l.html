One of the phrases we use a lot in our household is ‘We are made for each other and we are made by each other.’ Marxists such as Silvia Federici call this ‘social reproduction’. Neurologically, this makes sense: human interaction shapes our brains from our very earliest moments. Every human comes into the world in search of a gaze.
