To see this connection, let’s forget about black holes for a moment and return to Earth. We need to understand what entropy is and how it connects to irreversibility. This link may seem distant at first. But I want to show you how a simple thought experiment takes us from the microscopic world, back to the sizes of black holes, then to the no-hair theorem, and eventually to what black holes forget.
