It was only upon retiring 10 years ago that I found myself free to resume my fascination with life-writing, now with the roles reversed: after decades spent writing the lives of others, I would become my own subject. The decision was sparked one afternoon while I was clearing out my office at the museum. I suddenly realised how much material I had accumulated without having given it a second thought while I lived my life ‘forwards’, as Kierkegaard once put it.
