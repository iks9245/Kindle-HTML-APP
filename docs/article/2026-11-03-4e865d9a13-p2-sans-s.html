Analysis: A Half-Sine Is Broadband, but Not Flat

Figure 2 shows that a finite half-sine is broadband but contains spectral zeros at f/fo = 3, 5, 7, …, with fo = 1/(2T) for pulse duration T. Its appearance alone cannot establish adequate excitation.

Analysis: Spectral Content Is Determined by the Measured Force History

Figure 3 normalizes each spectrum to its own DC value. Crest rippling adds content and can fill half-sine notches. Timing between separated contacts creates deterministic interference. For adequacy, inspect the unnormalized measured spectrum and response signal-to-noise ratio.

Recommended Practice: Evaluate Test Adequacy Against the Intended Objective

• Measure the complete applied force with adequate bandwidth, calibration, dynamic range, and time synchronization.

• Verify that the response measurements have adequate bandwidth and signal-to-noise ratio and are not clipped or saturated.
