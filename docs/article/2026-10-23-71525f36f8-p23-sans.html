One way of thinking about our unconventional living situation, then, is that it offers a way of choosing who we want to be formed by, as if consciously curating the powerful gaze of others. Our bet is that the real costs, in control, convenience and comfort, the exposure of being seen and known ‘with masks slipped from faces’, are worth it. I believe that it is a demanding but effective method of becoming the kind of people the world needs now.
