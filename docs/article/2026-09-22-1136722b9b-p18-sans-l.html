Even when the effects are more subtle, the relationship between genome and transposon seems to sometimes go deeper than that of a host adapting to an invading, self-interested genetic force. In many cases, it could be considered more of a coevolutionary arrangement, Feschotte said. Some products made by transposons may do the same jobs as native proteins, and over time, the host genome may become dependent on the transposon and its products, a situation he compared to an addiction.
