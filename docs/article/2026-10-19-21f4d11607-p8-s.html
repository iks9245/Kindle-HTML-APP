Another enhancement I created to demonstrate the capabilities of dynamic packages is a new form or frame conversion feature for Lazarus extensions. It converts any form or frame into an installable control that is automatically added to the component palette. All controls, events, methods, and properties are copied to the new control.

This makes it possible to visually design and lay out new controls or easily reuse existing forms and frames in other projects.

Note: If you attempt to build a package that is currently being used by an open form, the IDE will ask you to save or close the form before building the package.

These features and more are available for download now at the location linked below. The complete changes to FPC and Lazarus have been committed to GitLab in my forked branch named dynamic packages.
