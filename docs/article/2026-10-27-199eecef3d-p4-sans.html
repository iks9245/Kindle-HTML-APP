Lula wins fourth term in dramatic Brazil election comeback

The result came as a surprise after Flávio Bolsonaro won the first round on 4 October with 47% of votes ahead of Lula's 45%.

But Lula's camp gained momentum ahead of election day, focusing on boosting voter turnout after 33 million people did not vote in round one.

Sunday saw the largest drop in abstention between the two rounds of a presidential election since 1989.

Bolsonaro had been poised to give a victory speech and said the result should be annulled, calling it "inexplicable".

As his disconsolate supporters left the house in Brasília where their candidate had been watching the result, Lula took to the streets in São Paulo to celebrate.
