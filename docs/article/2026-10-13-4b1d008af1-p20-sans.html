Is ‘Attention’ the Essence of Intelligence?

Recent research persuades me that human thought is not purely linguistic (and most other animals’ intelligence is not linguistic at all): When we are thinking, more brain regions light up than just the specialized networks in the cerebral cortex that are associated with language. Language is how we communicate about the world, but our thoughts seem to draw on sensory impressions, physical and social experiences, memories, and reasoning abilities that exist at least partly outside the brain’s language networks. So language modeling seems to be merely a powerful component of human cognition, not its equivalent or source.
