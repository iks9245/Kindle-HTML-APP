I customized how long each photo stays on screen and the brightness level. If the Kindle goes to sleep, the slideshow picks up where it left off when it wakes.

For now, I use my Mac to change the photos and slideshow timing. The photos stay stored on the Kindle, so the slideshow works without Wi-Fi. In the future, we could add controls to change the slideshow timing on the Kindle and send photos directly from a phone or another device.

What I learned

Next time, I'd set up the feedback loop from the start so the agent could read logs, inspect screen captures, and test fixes on the device.

The Kindle now sits on our wooden shelf, where we use it every day as a photo frame.
