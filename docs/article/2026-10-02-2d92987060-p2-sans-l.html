Not because the speaker’s claim was implausible. On the contrary, as a description of much contemporary artificial intelligence, it seemed apt. AI systems are built around objectives, losses, rewards, predictions, preferences, benchmarks and feedback loops. They are trained to perform better relative to some standard of success. In one form or another, they optimise. That is a great part of their technical power.
