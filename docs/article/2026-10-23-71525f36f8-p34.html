This realism might be why, despite its very real benefits, this is not a full-throated advertisement for communal life. The costs are real, so you need a powerful story to hold you. A completely different script of what a good life looks like. I don’t think it necessarily has to be a religious one, but it does need to be powerful, and go beyond individual interest. Setting up my life for mutual care and reciprocity is the best thing I have ever done, but its chafing against the script I was born into still leaves blisters sometimes. On days when it asks more of me than I am receiving, it is the ‘why’ that makes me stay.
