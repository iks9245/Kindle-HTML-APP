Witnesses have described how festival-goers were shot, sexually abused and tortured by gunmen during the raid.

Forty-four people were kidnapped, of whom 25 were brought back from Gaza alive.

Ahuva Yavin Mayze, whose 21-year-old daughter Adi was shot and killed at Nova, told the BBC what happened at the site was "dystopic, unimaginable".

"Today is a difficult day, not only for me but for a large part of the nation," said Shlomo Sharf, a former Israeli footballer, in comments reported by Channel 12.

He paid tribute to his granddaughter, May Naim, 24, who was also killed at the festival.
