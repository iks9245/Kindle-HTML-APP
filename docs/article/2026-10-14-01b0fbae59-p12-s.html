Tap a chord pad, or press its letter on the keyboard. The default key is G major, so the middle row, A S D F G, holds G, C, D, Em and Am. Auto strum starts on, so the chord strums itself.

Turn Auto off, then swipe down across the strings for a downstroke and up for an upstroke. On a keyboard, H strums down and J strums up.

Open the Strum menu and pick a strum or picking pattern. You hear it once on your chord. With Auto and Loop on, it keeps playing while you hold a chord key.

Open Songs & recordings and pick a song. Press Play to hear it, or press the chord key that lights green to lead it yourself. The tempo button next to Play slows it down.

Turn on the Band switch next to Play. Its edit button opens Backing band, where you choose the drums, the bass line and the strings.
