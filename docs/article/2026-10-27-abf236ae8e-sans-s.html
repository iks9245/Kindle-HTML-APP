NASA’s Perseverance Views Dust at Neretva Vallis

Description

This 360-degree look at Neretva Vallis within Mars’ Jezero Crater was stitched together using 217 images acquired by the rover’s Mastcam-Z on May 20, 2024, the 1,155th Martian day, or sol, of the mission. While it is a wind-swept dusty vista today, billions of years ago it was home to a river channel that fed fresh water into Jezero Crater.

This is an enhanced-color version, which had its color bands processed to improve visual contrast and accentuate color differences,

Arizona State University leads the operations of the Mastcam-Z instrument, working in collaboration with Malin Space Science Systems in San Diego, on the design, fabrication, testing, and operation of the cameras, and in collaboration with the Niels Bohr Institute of the University of Copenhagen on the design, fabrication, and testing of the calibration targets.NASA’s Jet Propulsion Laboratory, which is managed for the agency by Caltech, built and manages operations of the Perseverance rover.

延伸問答

為什麼Jezero撞擊坑對毅力號的任務很重要？

Jezero撞擊坑是毅力號的著陸點，因為科學家認為它在數十億年前曾是一個湖泊，有河流注入，因此是尋找火星過去生命跡象和採集潛在岩石樣本的理想地點。

在Neretva Vallis發現過去的河道，對火星上存在生命的可能性有何意義？

發現過去的河道表明Neretva Vallis曾有淡水流經，這是生命存在的基本要素之一。這強化了火星過去可能適合微生物生命存活的假設，並鼓勵科學家進一步尋找生物標誌物。

Mastcam-Z儀器是什麼？它在毅力號任務中扮演什麼角色？

Mastcam-Z是毅力號探測車上的一對可變焦全景立體相機，能夠拍攝高解析度的彩色影像和影片。它主要用於探測車導航、科學目標選擇，並提供地質環境的詳細視圖，幫助科學家理解火星的地貌和歷史。
