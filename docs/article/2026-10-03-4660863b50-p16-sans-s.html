Nuke’s mind did the same when a stranger held his gaze too long. The look may have meant nothing, yet he imagined a motive and a threat, drawn from his life, as most of us do, rather than anything the stranger had done. Nuke is working with the same machinery as the rest of us. What that machinery was trained on is different, and so is the price of getting it wrong. For the driver, a misreading ends in a muttered curse or a blown horn; for Nuke, it could end a life. As Nuke admitted: ‘I know what comes with it. It’s either death or jail.’
