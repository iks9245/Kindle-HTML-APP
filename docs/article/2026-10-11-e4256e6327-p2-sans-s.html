Twelve killed in attack on airport in Saudi capital Riyadh

On Saturday, a projectile also fell near King Fahd International Airport in Dammam in eastern Saudi Arabia, the Saudi-led coalition said.

The attacks come a day after authorities said three Saudis including a pilot were killed in two attacks on the airport by the Iran-backed Houthis the day prior. Another three people were killed in Houthi attacks on two Saudi airports on 6 and 7 October.

In Saturday's statement, the Saudi civil aviation authority said some of the injured in were "in critical condition".

It said damage to the airport was being assessed.

An eyewitness who said they had been in the domestic terminal told the news agency that they had seen gate 401 hit and "injured [people] in front of my eyes", adding: "Everyone was running."

More than a dozen ambulances were seen rushing to the airport, according to news agency Reuters, citing an eyewitness.
