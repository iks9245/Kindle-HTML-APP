The original transformer paper made this connection too, but only in v2 and v3 on arXiv. There, Vaswani et al. describe the feedforward layer as “a kind of attention,” with the rows of its two weight matrices as the keys and values, and ReLU in place of softmax. They even tried replacing the feedforward layers with attention over learned key-value parameters, and got about the same translation quality: one version did slightly worse, another slightly better, though the first one trained about 30% slower. The passage is gone from every later version of the paper. We’ll get to how self-attention relates to all this below.
