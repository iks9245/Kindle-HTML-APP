The message seems to be that the price we pay for the freedoms of liberal individualism is loneliness. You can have personal autonomy, privacy and endless choice; you can outsource most of your needs to the market rather than navigate complex non-transactional relationships with other people that tie you into reciprocity; you can optimise your surroundings, appearance and experiences to your exact preferences, but what gets bundled in for good measure is a sense of existential weightlessness.
