Tennessee's attorney general had asked the Supreme Court to overrule the lower court. Pike was sentenced to death in 1996 for the murder of Colleen Slemmer.

She was 18 when she and her then-boyfriend, Tadaryl Shipp, beat, tortured and killed the 19-year-old, whom they met at a job training camp for troubled teens.

Supreme Court Justice Brett Kavanaugh, a conservative, granted Wednesday evening's application to vacate the stay of the execution. No explanation was provided in the order.
