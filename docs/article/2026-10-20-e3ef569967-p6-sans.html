StorageReview tested a 128 GB HP OmniBook Ultra 16 with the same 20-core N1X, running native Windows ARM64 binaries. They report 7-Zip ratings in GIPS, so I divided the MIPS ratings in my raw logs by 1,000 to match. Higher is better.

| 7-Zip workload | My ProArt, Linux (GIPS) | HP OmniBook, Windows (GIPS) | Difference |

|---|---|---|---|

| 1-thread compression | 7.221 | 7.31 | −1.2% |

| 1-thread decompression | 10.779 | 10.87 | −0.8% |

| 20-thread compression | 165.754 | 157.9 | +5.0% |
