How to be a (Roman) man

Cicero’s focus in his writings of these years was on a range of mainly ethical issues, particularly the nature of virtus – now taken in a sense much closer to the English term ‘virtue’ or ‘moral excellence’. A particular concern was the management of emotions in the pursuit of spiritual wellbeing. This was an issue of pressing personal significance to Cicero following his family bereavement but also of broader relevance to those who mourned the political changes in Rome.

It is about what it means to be a man and how manhood can be forged in – and by – challenging circumstances
