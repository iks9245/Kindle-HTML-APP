- Treat being wrong as data rather than disgrace. If you follow these sceptical rules of thumb, you will change your mind from time to time – or at least ought to. The question is whether you experience that as humiliation or as the method working.

None of this was merely theoretical for Cicero. It informed every major decision of his life, including the most contested one.
