Explanation: How many moons can you see? The best answer is five: four orbiting Jupiter and Luna orbiting Earth. This multi-moon picture was possible because Earth's Moon moved directly in front of Jupiter last week. The featured image is a series of exposures near alignment taken from Westchester, New York, USA. The sunlit crescent of Earth's Moon, on the lower left, is overexposed, while the Moon's night side, on the upper right, is faintly illuminated by Earthshine. Lined up nearly vertically above our Moon, listed from bottom to top, are Jupiter's bright Galilean satellites: Ganymede, Io, Callisto, and Europa. Callisto, Ganymede, and Io are larger than Earth's Moon, while Europa is only slightly smaller. NASA's robotic spacecraft Juno is currently orbiting Jupiter and has made close passes near three of these moons.

延伸問答

摘要中提到的「伽利略衛星」是什麼？

伽利略衛星是指木星的四顆最大衛星：木衛一（伊俄）、木衛二（歐羅巴）、木衛三（甘尼米德）和木衛四（卡利斯托）。它們在1610年由伽利略·伽利萊首次觀測到，是太陽系中最大、最容易用望遠鏡觀測到的衛星之一。

為什麼能同時看到地球的月球和木星的四顆衛星是罕見的？

這種現象之所以罕見，是因為需要地球的月球恰好在木星前方經過，形成一個特殊的視角。這要求地球、月球和木星之間有特定的對齊，才能從地球上的某個位置觀測到。

美國太空總署的朱諾號太空船飛掠了木星的哪幾顆衛星？它的主要任務是什麼？

朱諾號太空船曾近距離飛掠了木星的木衛一、木衛二和木衛三。它的主要任務是研究木星的起源和演化，包括其內部結構、大氣層、磁場以及極光。
