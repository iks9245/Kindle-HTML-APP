小中大切換為無襯線

Deskilling the body

It is only when we imagine that our ability to act has nothing to do with the body that we end up taking seriously the idea that we can live contentedly as machines. Far from upskilling us, a technology that shuts us off from embodied learning risks diminishing the intelligence it seeks to enhance.

The technologies with which we are increasingly interconnected lack the complex interiority required for real intelligence to arise. There’s no body, no independently formed interests, no being. There’s no metabolism or organisation or persistence.
