This may sound like asking us all to become saints. Insofar as it asks us to grow in virtue and relinquish our attachment to our own importance, that impression is not far from the truth. But virtue is not something to cultivate for the admiration of others. It is necessary for genuine joy, even in our work. The satisfaction of solving a difficult problem or winning a prize can be fleeting when it rests on our standing relative to others. The joy of understanding is different. Something previously obscure becomes clear, and we recognize a beauty that does not diminish when someone else discovers it first. We can share this joy without depriving anyone of it. Growing in virtue allows us to experience it more fully, free from the need to establish our own importance.
