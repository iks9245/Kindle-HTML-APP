European leaders have rallied around the Ukrainian president. Italy's Defence Minister Guido Crosetto told the BBC that the US restricting its intelligence-sharing with Kyiv would be a "complete mistake".

"Because it is a fundamental form of support. This means denying a country any chance of defending itself."

He added: "Peace will be achieved when Putin will be convinced that there is a stalemate... that he cannot advance anymore. But if he sees a weakness on the Ukrainian front, if the United States takes its resources away, then Putin will think he can win, and so he will continue the war."

Zelensky met Nato chief Mark Rutte as a two-day summit of European leaders began in Brussels on Thursday. The head of the military alliance said they had discussed "the importance of implementing an energy ceasefire as well as Nato allies' continuous support for Ukraine".

Zelensky told the FT that Ukrainian officials had been told by US counterparts that "if you will not stop [the strikes], we will block intelligence".
