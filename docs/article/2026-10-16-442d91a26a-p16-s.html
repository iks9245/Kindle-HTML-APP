But what you have to remember is that the diet that the plants give the fungi is full of fat, and fat doesn’t mix very well with water.

And so there are these things called condensates, which are these big lipid droplets inside the network. And what we think is happening is that there’s these beautiful molecular motors that are inside the network that actually drag the lipids, the fats, to the tip of the network so the fungus can use it to grow. And we think that’s what’s happening, is that when you pull these lipids on these… So a molecular motor, you can think of it as, a little railroad track that has, one of those cars on it that people move up and down and up and down to move forward. That’s what a, a molecular motor looks like.
