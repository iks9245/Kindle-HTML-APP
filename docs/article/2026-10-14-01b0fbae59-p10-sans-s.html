14 everyday strums from Steady downs and the pop staple D DU U DU to shuffle and 6/8, 4 styles (reggae, funk and country), 26 more rhythms drawn from real songs, and 12 picking patterns like Travis and classical p-i-m-a. Choose one and you hear it straight away.

Drums, bass and strings that follow your chords: 15 drum styles, including rock, funk, reggae, bossa nova and cajon, and 25 bass lines. The Band switch next to Play turns them on. Tempo goes from 10 to 220 BPM, with a metronome.

Paste a song from a chord site or as ChordPro text, pick a strum, and Guitoro plays it with the band. Write "x2" to repeat a line or a part. Lyrics are left out, and your songs stay in your browser.

Play single notes and melodies on the neck from the computer keyboard, with hammer-ons, pull-offs, bends and vibrato.
