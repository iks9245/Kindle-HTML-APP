But this is not to say that the PSR is universally accepted among philosophers. It is controversial because it seems to entail necessitarianism – the thesis that everything is necessary. You are currently reading an essay about the PSR and panpsychism, but you could have been doing something else. If the PSR is true, however, then there is a sufficient reason why you are currently reading this essay, which makes it necessary that you are currently reading this essay and hence impossible that you could be doing anything else. Since the PSR applies to everything, it seems to entail the necessity of everything, and many find this unpalatable because it threatens free will and the seemingly obvious fact that all manner of things could have been different in all sorts of ways.
