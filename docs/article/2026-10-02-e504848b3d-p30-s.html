And all I have to do is take some knots, so some knotted loops inside of the 3-sphere, and I’m going to do surgery on them. And this is confusing. It’s a really important operation in topology. I wanna think of them as being thick. It’s like I’m cutting out a bunch of bagels from space, so. And then I’m going to just re-glue, like, or replace exactly the thing that I deleted. So I cut out a bunch of these, like, thickened circles, these solid bagels, solid tori. And just reattach them.
