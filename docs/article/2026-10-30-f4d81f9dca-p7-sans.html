LEVIN: Yeah. I think after people got over the thrill that they actually existed out there and that they could indirectly be detected, there was a sense that they’re not major players in the universe. “Eh, so there’s some black holes out there.”

Quite to the contrary, I think we’ve come to understand that they sculpt our history and our past, that they have a very regulating and important role in determining the shape and size of galaxies and where there might be habitable regions even. And ultimately, we’re kinda doomed to fall into black holes eventually. So, they’re kind of our past and our future.
