The mill example is illustrative of scientific explanation in general. The boiling point of water is explained in terms of the hydrogen-bonding of H2O molecules, and the path of light around a massive celestial body is explained in terms of the curvature of spacetime described by Albert Einstein’s general theory of relativity. These cases take more work to understand, but the principle is the same as in the mill case.

If consciousness is a fundamental property of matter, then the problem of explaining consciousness does not arise
