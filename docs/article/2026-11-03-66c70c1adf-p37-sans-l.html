What’s most profound is his idea that to be *truly* happy, our quests for knowledge, or money, or success, simply cannot be our #1 pride and goal.

At work, I have contributions to give, and I battle between wanting to do them to prove how capable I am, and to serve my coworkers in making it a better place for them.
