And that is just the cost of the physical infrastructure. Once a data center is up and running, training AI models entails using tens of thousands of chips running in parallel that require massive amounts of energy to power, run hot, and need to be kept cool. Moreover, each time someone prompts a model to answer a question, create an image, or perform a task, additional computational power, energy, and cooling are needed to process the query and generate a response or complete the task.

Finally, there are external factors that are delaying data center construction and piling on additional costs. The fierce and still-rising backlash to AI in general and data centers in particular across the country has scuttled a number of data center projects and delayed many others. Every delay limits the computational capacity Anthropic is counting on to generate the revenue it will desperately need, and rising costs due to the Iran War and supply-chain challenges could drive Anthropic’s operational costs still higher.
