Other Site Improvements

It’s only been a short while since the last update, but I did squeeze in a couple of quality-of-life improvements. You can now take screenshots and record videos (with sound) of the emulated machine’s screen. This gives you a high-fidelity way to capture image content, since it’s a direct dump of what would have been shown on the screen.
