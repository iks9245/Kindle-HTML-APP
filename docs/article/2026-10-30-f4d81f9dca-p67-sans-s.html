STROGATZ: And so what is it about seeing an image emotionally, do you think? I mean, I realize this is getting into psychology rather than physics, but why is it so important for us to have that gorgeous picture of the ring with the blackness inside it?

LEVIN: I mean, it’s thrilling. We are observers of the sky. We look at the sky and it was our first instrument in many senses, and our first clock and our first recognition that, wow, there’s something beyond this Earth. And so I think just the idea that here we are looking at something — literally looking at it two million light years away — that is extending our cosmos even further out. I just think that is a human disposition to be thrilled by that.
