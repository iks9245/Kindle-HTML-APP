A life in episodes

An episode is a thematically coherent Facebook post of 300-500 words that takes a specific situation or experience as its point of departure. Working from a raw file of letters, diary entries and present-day reflections, I winnow the material and try to render what happened clearly enough for some meaning to emerge. Each episode must be able to stand alone, intelligible and engaging even for those who only occasionally follow my Facebook feed.

How many episodes a season holds emerges only in the writing, as readers’ comments spur new insights and new episodes. And above all, I have no master plan telling me in advance which parts of my life will matter.
