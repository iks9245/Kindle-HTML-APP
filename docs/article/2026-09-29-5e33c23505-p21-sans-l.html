- Small models don't reason. Expect fast, calibrated choices between the options you describe, not multi-step reasoning. At 0.8B–2B parameters this holds for every model, not just Jeff.

- Jeff-2B is a weaker game player than Jeff-0.8B. The untrained 2B already appears more risk-averse than the untrained 0.8B, and our training seems to have made that worse. This needs more investigation.
