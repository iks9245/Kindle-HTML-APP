Moscow would welcome Washington cutting off intelligence sharing to Ukraine and weapons sales to Europe that are destined for Kyiv.

It has already delighted to hear Trump declaring: "I think it's time for Ukraine to get a new president."

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky has called for a 60-day Russian ceasefire to allow for presidential elections in Ukraine.

Moscow's reaction?

"The Kremlin, the president, has said many times before, we don't want a ceasefire, we want a lasting peace, and we're willing to do whatever's possible for that," Peskov said.

"Whatever's possible" does not include a 60-day ceasefire, or a 30-day ceasefire, or anything close to that.

On Monday, the Kremlin proposed a one-day cessation of hostilities for Ukraine to hold elections.
