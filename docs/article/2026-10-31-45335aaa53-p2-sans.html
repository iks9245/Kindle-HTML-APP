Stewart, of course, was far more than Picard. He anchored the X-Men film franchise and was for decades a dynamo on stage. But there are reasons Captain Picard, commander of the Starship Enterprise, is the character he’s best known for. Over The Next Generation’s seven seasons, four movies, and a revival show —Star Trek: Picard — that ended just three years ago, he portrayed a model of what mature, responsible adulthood could look like for a generation of boys who were bookish, imaginitive, and maybe a little nerdy.

Two of the Globe’s dyed-in-the-wool Star Trek fans share what Captain Picard, and Patrick Stewart, meant to them.
