We estimated the total value of California’s land, summing it up parcel by parcel: about $8.14 trillion. We checked that figure two more ways — against federal housing-finance data and against time-trended sales — and all three methods land in the same range. It is, as far as we know, the first credible bottom-up estimate of what California’s land is actually worth.

That number is roughly eight times the billionaire wealth the state can still realistically tax. Los Angeles County’s land alone is worth more than the entire billionaire base the wealth tax is chasing. The Bay Area’s is close behind.
