Ward was fascinated by the planetary dimension of politics, which became central to her vision of development. The launch of the Soviet satellite Sputnik in 1957 inaugurated the Space Age, while Yuri Gagarin’s orbit of Earth in 1961 (and Valentina Tereshkova’s two years later) altered humanity’s perception of its place in the cosmos. As space exploration transformed public views of the limits of human life, and Planet Earth appeared as just one among many possible habitats for humanity, Ward argued that this new sense of unity would revolutionise human history. Her 1965 lectures at Columbia University, titled ‘Spaceship Earth’, outlined an idea of a fragile and united planet, anticipating the famous imagery generated in 1968 by ‘Earthrise’, William Anders’s iconic photo of Planet Earth from space.
