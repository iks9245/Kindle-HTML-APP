小中大切換為無襯線

Is everything conscious?

The hypothesis that fundamental matter is physical and non-conscious blocks the possibility of an intelligible explanation – in terms of the fundamental ‘bits’ of matter – of why creatures like us are conscious. If consciousness is absent at the fundamental level, there will always be an explanatory gap between the physical and the mental. What this means is that, for any arrangement of physical matter constitutive of a conscious being, it always seems possible that consciousness was absent; no arrangement of matter seems to be necessarily connected with, and hence able to explain, consciousness. But if consciousness is introduced at the fundamental level, this obstacle is removed. According to panpsychism, we no longer need to bridge the gap from non-conscious fundamental matter to consciousness at higher levels of organisation, since consciousness is present all the way down.
