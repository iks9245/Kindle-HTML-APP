This ancient truth remains valid, even as the scale of our mutual turnings stretches far beyond family and village and watershed to encompass national and global communities through economic and cultural exchanges and the shared cycles of carbon and nitrogen, dust and pollution. No matter the distance that separates us, the lives of urban and rural people still turn around one another. An ethic grounded in this truth must be expressed in mutual interest in and concern for one another.
