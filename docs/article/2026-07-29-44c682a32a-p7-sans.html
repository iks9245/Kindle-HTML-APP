Wildfires in south-west France flare up as region braces for new 40C heatwave

BBC World News ｜ 第 7 / 9 頁 ｜ 首頁 ｜ 存檔列表 ｜ 原文網頁

A 23-year-old man who was arrested on suspicion of starting a fire in the Var region. is now facing 15 years' imprisonment. Interior Minister Laurent Nuñez said that 184 people had been arrested since 6 July in connection with the fires.

Peggy Plouhinec, the general director of Château Picque Caillou - one of the few wine estates on the west side of Bordeaux - told the BBC that her business chose to close at the weekend because of the smoke, but that the situation had improved since then and that a bout of rain on Sunday night had helped.

While her vineyards were not at risk, she said tourists had "deserted" the region.
