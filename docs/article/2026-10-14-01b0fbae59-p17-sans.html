| Chords | Three rows of five: A to G for the main chords, Q to T for sevenths, Z to B for more chords and colors like sus4 and add9 |

|---|---|

| Strum | H down, J up, K down when pressed and up when released, N hard down, M slow down |

| Pick | Y arpeggio, U bass note, I O P the top three strings |

| Feel | L chuck, ; palm mute, Shift hard strum, Alt slow strum |

| Songs | The chord keys show the song's chords in order and the next one lights green. With My guitar on, Space plays and pauses the song |
