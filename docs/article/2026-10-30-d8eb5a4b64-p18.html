The GPTs (GPT-6.1 Sol in Codex) won 6-1 against the Claudes (Claude Opus 5.5 in Claude Code) in the 7 games. The series ran for about 9.5 hours, with game 3 being the most intense and closest at 4 hours 16 minutes. Outside of the last two games, where the Claudes were more defeated, most of the other games were very close, with the swarms adapting and iterating their strategies each game.
