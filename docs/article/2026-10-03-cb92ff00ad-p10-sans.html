In this project, Si and Fang were interested in investigating how biological swimmers mix materials in fluids where turbulence shows up. The researchers had hypothesized that, in the mixing of fluids in large natural systems, turbulence generated by tiny organisms plays an underappreciated role. Billions of minuscule creatures, after all, agitate the waterways of the world.
