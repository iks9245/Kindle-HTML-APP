After the Second World War, Ward was interested in the global challenges of economic growth and reconstruction. She travelled extensively in the United States, India, Europe and Africa, where in 1946 she prepared a study of demography and migration in British Togoland. In 1950, she married the Australian civil servant Robert Jackson, and three years later the couple relocated to the Gold Coast, where Jackson was appointed chairman of the preparatory commission of the Volta Dam, one of the continent’s major economic-development projects.
