My grandparents trusted that these rituals of the table (they saw them as basic codes of Italianness, connecting displaced Italians everywhere) would allow us to paper over the differences in our ethnically blended and peripatetic family. In this way, we’d be able to claim Italian identity for ourselves, despite its competition in our lives with Englishness and Frenchness. It would anchor us, or so they believed, and prevent us being bullied and singled out.

Revving their Vespas and wearing clothes I’d have avoided, they couldn’t make head nor tail of me
