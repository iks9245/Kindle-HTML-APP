Both knew every vote would count and did not want their side to miss out.

The president of the Supreme Electoral Court Kássio Nunes Marques – appointed by Jair Bolsonaro – confirmed Lula's victory and said that the voter extension did not change the outcome of the race.

The sheer gap between the two candidates' tallies - 4.4 million votes - makes the result more difficult to challenge.

The result came as a surprise after Flávio Bolsonaro won the first round on 4 October with 47% of votes ahead of Lula's 45%.
