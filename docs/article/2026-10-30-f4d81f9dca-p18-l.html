DOELEMAN: Portrait of a black hole, I love that. So I graduated high school pretty early. I was 15 years old. And went to college, and then after that I was pretty burned out, and I saw an advertisement that said, “Go to Antarctica and run all these experiments down there.” And I said, “That’s for me.” I interviewed for the position, and somehow they gave it to this 19-year-old kid. They needed some warm body down there to look after the cosmic ray experiments, the aerophotometry experiments, the electromagnetic experiments, and I got a real taste for doing experimental research in difficult circumstances. I just loved it. And then came back to MIT, where I met you in 1988.
