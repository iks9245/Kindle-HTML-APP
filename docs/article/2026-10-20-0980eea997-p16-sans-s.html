Horrible, unmanly, indispensable

As guns became more common, so did their appearance in the criminal records. By 1600, firearms had become the predominant weapon used to commit homicide in Bologna. That said, they still accounted for only a third of offences, and in interpreting the figures we should bear in mind the relative lethality of guns when compared with other weapons. In fact, across the broad range of gun crime, it was still relatively rare to come across cases where guns were directly used in planned homicide. More commonly, they were used to threaten: the ideal outcome for the highwayman who shouts: ‘Your money or your life!’ is to ride away with the money without firing a shot.
