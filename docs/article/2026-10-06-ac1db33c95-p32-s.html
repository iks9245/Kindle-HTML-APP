A life in episodes

The form itself works against tidying. Because I publish short, self-contained episodes in rapid succession, it is difficult to reshape what I have written into a narrative without readers noticing; any attempt to harmonise the whole after the fact runs up against episodes already published. The open feuilleton thus becomes a safeguard against the kind of retrospective and self-flattering narrativity that Strawson warns against.
