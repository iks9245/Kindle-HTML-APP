The physics of getting fired

Think of a power grid. Most of the time it hums along without problem. But during an unexpected surge, such as a heatwave when millions of air conditioners are switched on at once, the system can overload and shut down. To prevent this, engineers design grids with slack in the system and extra capacity to handle unexpected spikes. Without it, a minor disruption can cascade into a blackout.
