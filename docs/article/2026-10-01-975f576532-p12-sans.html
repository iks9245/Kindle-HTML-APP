Hardening systems: As frontier models grow increasingly capable, safely testing them requires secure environments that can keep up with the systems themselves. In line with our agent control roadmap, we are hardening our sandboxed environments by isolating and sealing them before high-risk training or evaluations begin. We’re committed to sharing these agent security best practices with our partners to improve security across the ecosystem.
