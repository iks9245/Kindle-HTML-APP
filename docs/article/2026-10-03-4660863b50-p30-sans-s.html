I have seen the same skills taught in a classroom, on a corner by an outreach worker, and in a boxing gym in the Chicago neighbourhood of North Lawndale, where the former gang member Derek Brown* has been running a programme called Boxing Out Negativity since 2009. He teaches boys to box and, alongside it, to sit still and breathe, to notice what their bodies are doing before a fight and after one, to name what they are feeling and to wait. The boys come to Derek’s gym to learn how to fight. They are also learning how to handle the moments before a fight starts, which is the harder lesson and the one they need more often in life.
