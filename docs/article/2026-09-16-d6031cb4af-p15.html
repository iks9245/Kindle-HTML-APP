小中大切換為無襯線

Chasing la dolce vita

My kids love YouTube and TikTok videos in which a nonna or nonno, from a well-ordered kitchen in Italy, reveals how to make the creamiest cacio e pepe or the tastiest tomato sauce. These elderly cooks appear to embody an authentic art to being Italian that cuts through the mimicry and stagecraft. The children laugh loudest when these elderly home chefs from Italy screw up their faces in disgust when asked to taste American shop-bought pizza or frozen lasagne. ‘Do you know what merda means in English?’ one asks.
