When stars die, they release some of their atmosphere, and so you can wind up with a red giant star with a molecular soup around it, and the light from the star pumps that molecular soup, and you can get effectively a laser the size of the star.

And around the edge of the star, around the limb of the star, you can get pinpoints of laser light in the radio, and these can be now observed with the Event Horizon Telescope too. So we may be able to see stars breathe in and out by using the Event Horizon Telescope with these molecular lines.
