Hi everyone,

I’m writing this because I recently found out about a silent and incredibly anti-consumer change Withings just implemented, and I think the whole community needs to be aware of it.

Starting from October 13th, 2026, any new Withings account is forced to pay for a Withings+ subscription ($99.99/year) just to sync their own data to Apple Health.

I reached out to Customer Support and their response was completely contradictory. They claimed that syncing with Apple Health is a "standard feature that does not require a subscription," but in the very next sentence, they confirmed that "any account created after October 13th will be asked to sign up for a Withings+ subscription in order to export data" justifying it with new "privacy measures" and an "AI connector".
