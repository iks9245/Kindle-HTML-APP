小中大切換為襯線

The bombarding of childhood

The lowest-scoring media are those in which the child’s attention is kept in motion by a manipulative rhythm that continuously restarts attention with the next tune, next bright image, next comic beat, next loop of familiarity or personalised nudge. Instead of allowing the child to settle into the story, such children’s shows repeatedly pull attention back; the same is true of many preschool game apps built around loops of reward and progression.

Evaluating children’s popular media taught me that pace is a revealing measure of quality but also a proxy for a deeper question about the child’s agency, their own sense of self.
