The ghetto on the lagoon

By the time the Ghetto was founded, Jews were already a familiar presence in the Venetian lagoon. As early as the 10th century, Jewish merchants from the eastern Mediterranean are known to have travelled on board Venetian ships; and at least a few spent some time in the city itself. They were, however, a transitory presence, staying only for as long as it took to unload a cargo or conclude their business, and then moving on to harbours new.
