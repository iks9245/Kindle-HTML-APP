In 2024, Condé Nast, The New Yorker’s parent company, signed a multi-year licensing deal with OpenAI allowing it to use much of the publisher’s content in its products. The terms of the deal aren’t public, but a spokesperson for The New Yorker told me Condé Nast has never granted an LLM developer permission to train models on its cartoons. Some ChatGPT creations reproduced The New Yorker’s logo alongside cartoons; that is also not allowed in any LLM deals, the spokesperson said.

OpenAI declined to answer my questions about how its models might have ingested signatures from The New Yorker’s archives during the training process.
