In praise of the dilettante

In a memorable scene from the Spike Jonze film Adaptation (2002), the journalist Susan Orlean (Meryl Streep), who is writing a book about the orchid thief John Laroche (Chris Cooper), is at once intrigued by his hunt for a rare hallucinogenic ‘ghost orchid’ and baffled to learn that it is only the latest in a lifetime of obsessions: turtles, prehistoric fossils, 19th-century Dutch mirrors, etc. How can he detach so abruptly from something in which he’s invested so much? He can only offer an anecdote. A profound and consuming love for tropical fish ended suddenly with the realisation that the passion had simply run its course. ‘Fuck fish,’ he tells her, disrupting her assumption that loving something means sticking with it forever.
