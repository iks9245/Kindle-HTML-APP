Small wonder, then, that in an age of tumult and transition, physics has found new admirers – especially among those who want to catalyse radical change, for better or worse. Musk speaks of first principles, Cummings of complexity. Their examples show how the tools of physics can shape thinking far beyond the laboratory. But in their hands, physics can feel more like a weapon than a framework, a way of bulldozing nuance in the name of clarity. For the rest of us, the challenge is not simply to borrow the garb and glamour of physics, but to carry forward its mindset – one that values humility as much as boldness, and curiosity as much as certainty.
