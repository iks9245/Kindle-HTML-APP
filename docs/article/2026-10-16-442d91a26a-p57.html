STROGATZ: My own world is math, especially, math applied to the real world, to things like biology and physics and social science. And so as I listen to you, it just sounds like such a case study in the part of evolutionary dynamics that people who study game theory like to think about.

KIERS: We do go to a lot of game theory conferences. We work with theoreticians at Oxford on many kinds of different questions that you can answer with game theory. And the collaboration works out quite well because then we can test some of these ideas very directly in the lab. So in some cases, we’re discovering something and then trying to use game theory to explain it, and in other cases, there’s game theory that’s existing, and we use manipulative experiments to test if it’s true.
