KIERS: Well, we use beautiful techniques, for example, confocal microscopy to look into these organisms. If you were to zoom in and actually look at the spores of these organisms, they contain hundreds of nuclei that shine like little bright stars inside a balloon, and they’re the most incredible structures that you’ve ever seen. And to think that these spores are traveling through the air and actually changing our weather patterns, right? I mean, more and more we’re starting to understand how spores, fungal spores in the air, are an important input to generate clouds over rainforests.

What is not charismatic about that? They’re so beautiful on a small microscopic level, and then up in the atmosphere they’re floating around forming clouds. I mean, there’s just nothing quite like it.
