Denim, Secrecy, and Vegetables

Language models learn to generate fluent sentences by “training” on vast reams of human-authored text. That means if you ask them what they “think,” their answers may just mimic the kinds of responses found in their training data. “They are trained to talk like humans, and humans talk about their inner life all the time,” said Shauli Ravfogel, a language model researcher at New York University.
