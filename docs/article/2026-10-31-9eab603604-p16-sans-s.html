In praise of the dilettante

However, from the outside, mobility may look like mere lack of commitment. No doubt this drives some of the allegations of superficiality commonly lodged against the dilettante. Pulling up anchors and moving on from an interest gets flagged as surface-skimming. I’m speculating here, but such criticism seems rooted in a work ethic that tells us that investment in certain interests ought to yield dividends: authority, credibility, permission to enter culturally guarded spaces, etc. Just as the neo-classicist wants to set the ‘true artist’ apart from the poseurs, the modern admonition to ‘pick a lane’, or (more aggressive) ‘stay in your lane’, stems from assumptions about the level and length of commitment required to claim an interest as one’s own.
