This project finds faint echoes in the self-improvement, self-development, self-optimisation we are all constantly hectored into – therapy (or psychedelics, or stoicism) for the mind and Hyrox (or reformer Pilates, or yoga) for the body (with those who are not working on themselves in some way being the exception). Interestingly, the unconventional way our household lives is rarely recommended to those seeking self-knowledge or self-development. The standard advice is an inward turn in order to facilitate a private excavation of an authentic self, along with strong personal boundaries that draw distinct lines between that self and others. Porousness tends to be framed as deleterious to Project Self. Most monastic orders encourage periods of solitude and silence, to be sure, but much of daily life is spent in an exposing proximity to others, the very idea of which would bring most of us out in hives.
