Most experts, though, cannot see how DF2, DF4, and DF9 could exist without dark matter being a physical thing, even if the trio’s genesis can be debated. “It’s hard to explain how you’d get something like this evolving by itself without something like dark matter,” Ghag said.

That makes this galactic trio bad news for MOND, or modified Newtonian dynamics, an alternative to dark matter proposed in 1983 by the physicist Mordehai Milgrom. “MOND says: What if there’s only visible matter, but its gravity works differently than we thought?” Whiteson explained.
