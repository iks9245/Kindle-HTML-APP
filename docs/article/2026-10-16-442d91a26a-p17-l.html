And so the fungi then take up these nutrients and move them through these vast networks and feed them to plant roots. And in return —obviously they don’t do this for free — the plants are feeding them a diet of sugar and lipids like fats. It’s like the most brilliant diet of sugar and fats that is provided by the plants to the fungi. And this partnership is very common. It’s almost ubiquitous, I would say, across terrestrial ecosystems. And because of this partnership and because plants are constantly feeding carbon down to these networks, we actually call it one of Earth’s circulatory systems.
