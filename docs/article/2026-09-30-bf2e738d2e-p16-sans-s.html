Before any series data, PREREGISTRATION.md is committed. It covers the item-selection procedure, the validation checks, the primary metric, the decision rule and the list of secondary metrics, so the git timestamp is public. A change is only called a change if the 99% interval excludes zero in two consecutive 10-day windows, and the effect is at least 3 points, and the control arm doesn't show the same move. Null results get published. So do improvements.

- PLAN.md is the original measurement plan: constraints, arms, threats to validity, and the v0 Max-plan scope in §0.

- docs/CALIBRATION.md is the day-1 record: calibration yield, classifier events, the design's cost and the validation results.

- docs/DESIGN.md holds the design generated from measured data: the panel, the rates and the predicted MDE.

- docs/VALIDATION.md is the positive control and the A/A check.

- docs/PILOT.md covers the pilots and the harness audit.
