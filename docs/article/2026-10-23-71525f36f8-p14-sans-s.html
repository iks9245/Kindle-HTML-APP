Living with others has shown me things in myself that I would prefer to ignore

My policy-wonk brain knew there were systems keeping us apart. Not just social scripts but monetary policies, mortgage terms, HMO (house in multiple occupation) legislation. Yet living communally has taught me that it is also what is inside us that makes it hard to be together. My romantic ideas about cultures worthy of the name, marked by interdependence and mutual care, clearly also require certain kinds of people – with emotional skills, developed character, an ability to make lasting commitments. None of which my individualistic formation had gifted me an abundance of.
