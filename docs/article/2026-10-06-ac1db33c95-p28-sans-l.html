The crucial difference between writing one’s autobiography in traditional book form and writing it as a feuilleton on social media is the possibility of entering into a dialogue with readers from the earliest stages. The profile currently has more than 1,000 followers from Sweden, Denmark, the UK and the US, and each post usually draws at least a handful of comments; occasionally as many as 200. Most responses come from a core group of around 100 regular participants; they come from every walk of life, and some of the sharpest remarks come from readers with no scholarly training at all. In recent years, however, this engagement has diminished, partly reflecting a general trend on social media, but perhaps also a waning interest in the project itself (another similarity with TV series).
