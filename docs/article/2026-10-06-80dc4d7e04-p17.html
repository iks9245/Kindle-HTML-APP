Last year, The Baltimore Sun fired Kevin Kallaugher, a Pulitzer finalist who had published editorial cartoons in the paper for more than 30 years. And earlier this year, the Sun began regularly publishing AI-generated cartoons in its opinions section. A quick check with OpenAI’s watermark identification tool shows the illustrations were created with OpenAI’s products.

“[We] condemn the use of AI for its theft from artists, its misuse of our planet’s resources, and its threat to original, creative thought,” Marc Murphy, the president of The Association of American Editorial Cartoonists, wrote in an open letter published last month. “The Sun is not alone among American newspapers taking this degrading way out.”
