Life on a hair trigger

The second pattern was a narrowing of perceived response options. When violence seemed possible, those we spoke with often saw only a narrow set of things they could do. Kobe, 24, described the months after his release from prison: ‘I was walking around like a ticking time bomb. Soon as somebody did anything, I’m whooping them like I’m they daddy.’ Similarly, when King learned that Lamont was dead, his thoughts narrowed to one response: revenge.

Such narrowing can be adaptive in dangerous situations, where hesitation can be costly. But when it is applied more generally, it can lead to serious problems. In these moments, people tend to bypass deliberate reflection and may feel that certain responses are inevitable, as King told us: ‘That’s how it be, and that’s how it’s going to always be. It’s a repeated cycle.’
