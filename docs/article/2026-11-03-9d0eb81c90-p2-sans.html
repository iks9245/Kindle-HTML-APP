But there are still more questions than answers as to how such a military force might work in practice. Western officials stress that any force sent to Ukraine would not take part in offensive operations or be involved in peacekeeping or ceasefire monitoring missions.

Nor would they be operating under the command of Nato or on the front line.

Officials say their presence would be to provide reassurance to Ukraine and to help rebuild its armed forces.

The US has been involved in discussions, but is not contributing to the mission.

Russia has already voiced opposition to foreign troops being stationed in Ukraine and, last year, President Vladimir Putin said they would be "legitimate targets".
