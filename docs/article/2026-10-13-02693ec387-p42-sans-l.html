Is everything conscious?

By giving up the all-encompassing demand for intelligibility and, instead, conceiving of philosophy as providing different perspectives on our condition, we can better understand what it is to make progress in philosophy. Widespread philosophical disagreement should not be taken as evidence that philosophy is degenerate compared with, say, the natural sciences. Rather, we should celebrate the fact that opposing views such as dualism, physicalism and panpsychism exist to provide us with richly diverse perspectives on life’s most tantalising mysteries.
