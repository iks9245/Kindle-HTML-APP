KIERS: I think one of the most basic ones, we just wanted to see if plants could discriminate between very cooperative and less cooperative fungal strains. And so, the experiments that we do often take place in a Petri plate. So they’re very lab-based, and we are the first to admit that what’s happening out in nature is obviously very different and influenced by so many other organisms.
