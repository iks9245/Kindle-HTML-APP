A life in episodes

But there are dissenting voices. The British philosopher Galen Strawson distinguishes between those who experience themselves diachronically, as a single self unfolding through time, and those who understand themselves episodically. Such people experience their lives as a sequence of fragments rather than as a coherent whole, and yet can be fully reflective and morally serious. Strawson’s chief witness is Michel de Montaigne – perhaps the greatest autobiographer in world literature – who moves freely between fragments, paradoxes and unfinished reflections in an open-ended exploration that never resolves into a story.
