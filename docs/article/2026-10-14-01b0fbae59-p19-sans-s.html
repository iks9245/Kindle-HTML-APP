A video shows a teacher's hand from one angle, at one speed, and the hand hides which strings it hits. Guitoro plays the song's part close to the original recording on a guitar you can see whole: each string lights up as it's hit, the pick shows every down and up stroke, and you set the speed. Then you play it yourself and the song waits for you.

51 songs and classical pieces, from Wonderwall, Hotel California, Let It Be and Perfect to Canon in D and Für Elise, each with its key, capo, tempo and strum or picking set up. See them all on the easy guitar songs page, or paste your own from a chord site.

Yes. Tap a chord pad and swipe the strings. In portrait, the enlarged neck sits below the strings with four frets per page. In landscape, the neck and strings sit side by side. Tap the frets to play single notes. Solo mode and My guitar need a computer.
