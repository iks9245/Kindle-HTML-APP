Deskilling the body

Long before the digital revolution, René Descartes described the body as a machine governed by physical laws, declaring that animals such as ‘worms and flies and caterpillars move mechanically’. But he treated rational thought as a separate and uniquely human power. The body might behave like a machine, but the thinking mind stood apart from it. Build something correctly, and its function follows.
