What a buyer, an owner or a legislator can do

- Before buying, read the association's record. Search it on HOA Spy: every lien, lis pendens and court case on file, each linked to the official record, and its corporate standing. A community that forecloses as a matter of routine, or whose corporation has lapsed, is a fact to price in before the inspection period ends.
