So how did OpenAI’s models learn those signatures? It’s possible they were scraped from the internet. Court documents released last month in the ongoing New York Times v. OpenAI lawsuit reveal the extent to which OpenAI knowingly circumvented publishers’ paywalls while developing ChatGPT. Unredacted Microsoft memos described ChatGPT as a “product that destroys its supply chain” and the data scraped to train its underlying LLMs as “the largest theft of labor in human history.”

In another memo, Jack Clark, then the policy director of OpenAI, wrote, “[O]ur work on AI and Creativity is going to increasingly lead to us creating systems that substitute for the labor of the people that define the ‘culture’ of society.” (Clark went on to co-found Anthropic.)

Many of the cartoonists I spoke to for this story echoed Clark’s sentiment.
