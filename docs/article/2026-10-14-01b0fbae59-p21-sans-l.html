Turn on the Band switch next to Play. Its edit button opens Backing band, where you choose the drums, the bass line and the strings.

Open Songs & recordings, choose Recordings, and press Record. Play, then use Stop next to the recording timer. Your takes stay in Recordings.

Keyboard

Your left hand holds the chords and your right hand strums, just like on a guitar. Keys follow their physical position on a US QWERTY keyboard, and every key on screen shows its letter.
