NASA to Conduct NCAA Flyover, Engage Fans at University of Alabama

Supported by NASA’s Marshall Space Flight Center in Huntsville, Alabama, the NASA Experience Zone will be open to the public from 10:30 a.m. to 2:30 p.m. CDT, with no game ticket required. The experience will give visitors an opportunity to meet NASA team members and learn more about the people and technologies advancing the agency’s missions in aeronautics and space exploration.

Attendees can engage with NASA team members and explore exhibits highlighting the agency’s Artemis campaign and efforts to return astronauts to the Moon, including:

- An inflatable SLS (Space Launch System) rocket.

- Displays featuring the Starship and Blue Moon human landing systems.

- Information about NASA internship opportunities.

- Photo opportunities featuring NASA exhibits and displays.
