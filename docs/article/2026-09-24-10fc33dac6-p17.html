At a conference in December 2023, Scholes had a conversation about what needed to happen to advance the field. He recalls returning to his hotel room and jotting down an idea: Maybe the “quantum” in quantum biology doesn’t come from quantum mechanics at all, but emerges out of wavelike ripples across huge, complex networks.

“I’m now convinced that you could get transformative effects, but they would come from classical systems mimicking what you can do with quantum systems,” Scholes said. “Does it matter if you can’t tell the difference?”

A World of Quantumlike States

Scholes wondered if the emergence of special synchronized states in complex networks, like flocks of birds or schools of fish, might produce quantumlike behavior.
