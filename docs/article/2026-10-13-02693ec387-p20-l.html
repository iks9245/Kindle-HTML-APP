Is everything conscious?

Unanswerable questions do not sit comfortably with our inquisitive human nature. If consciousness cannot be explained in principle, this would be a particularly egregious violation of the PSR. However, it is worth considering our options for explaining consciousness and, thus, upholding the PSR once we accept that reductive scientific explanation is not up to the task.
