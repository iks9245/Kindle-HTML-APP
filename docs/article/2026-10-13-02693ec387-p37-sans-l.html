But does this latter path offer a deeper sense of meaningful existence? I’m not so sure. In fact, uncertainty about ultimate meaning fuels the arts, which, at their most powerful, embrace and celebrate the unintelligibility and absurdity of life. On the other hand, it seems legitimate to criticise art when its meaning is too obvious, its message too on the nose. So, just as clear and unambiguous meaning is far from the supreme aesthetic goal, it’s not clear that rendering reality fully intelligible, providing the ‘meaning of life’, is a desirable or realistic aim for philosophy.
