小中大切換為襯線

Where a land ethic blooms

Forty iron rectangles, each one blunt as a piano key, rotate into a blurring fury that hides the eight-foot-long shaft at the heart of the flail chopper. I am perched atop several tons of iron, with 80 horsepower at my command. This part of farming is straightforward – even a mechanically challenged tree-hugger like me can manage to depress the clutch, shift into second, and ease the throttle forward. The tractor’s tall, deeply treaded tires churn, revolving into a smooth spin. Everything is spinning: fans, belts, alternator, the driveshaft, axles. I don’t understand the mechanism fully, yet the tractor and chopper and I are propelled forward across the knee-high whiskers of a coarsely bearded cornfield.
