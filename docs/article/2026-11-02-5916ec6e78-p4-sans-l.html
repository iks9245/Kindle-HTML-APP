In a separate post on Telegram, the Ukrainian Central Intelligence Agency said that due to the Air Alert application, the Radiation Hazard signal was played on loudspeaker devices in the Lviv region, and added there was no threat of radiation hazard.

If the alert app is not working, Ukraine's Centre for Countering Disinformation advises the public to turn on notifications on their smartphones, pay attention to sirens, and to only read official sources

Several governors of other regions also posted on Telegram that the app had apparently been hacked and there was no local threat.
