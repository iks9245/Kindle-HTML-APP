Swedish left-wing bloc narrowly ahead with election too close to call

It is not yet known how many votes will be counted on Wednesday, but back in 2022, more than 220,000 votes were included in the later count.

If the figures are similar this year, Wednesday's count could make the difference in the outcome, given that only about 28,000 votes currently separate the blocs.

Sweden has two major political blocs featuring four parties on the right and four on the left - meaning coalition and minority governments are relatively common.

On Sunday night, Andersson - the leader of the country's main opposition party - urged supporters to wait for the final result, but added that if the current results hold, "Sweden will have a new government."
