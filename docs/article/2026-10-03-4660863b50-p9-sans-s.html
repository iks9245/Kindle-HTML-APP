The first was misreading situations. A great deal of violence, the men told us, stems from misconstruing some aspect of an interpersonal interaction, such as the nature of the situation or the other person’s intent. Past experiences of violence had created threat-oriented frames that quickly registered ambiguous cues as dangerous. Nuke, 24, told us that even a stranger looking at him for too long could escalate into a confrontation: ‘Sometimes I’ll react. Like: “Man, what the fuck is you looking at?”’
