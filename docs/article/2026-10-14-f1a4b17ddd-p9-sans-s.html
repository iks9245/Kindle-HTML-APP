Deskilling the body

For the past six years, I have been researching the latest science on what life is and how it works. As best as we can tell, the intelligent actions of organisms derive from the imperatives of a living body that must regulate and preserve its own conditions for existence. When cybernetics was ascendant, the molecular structure of DNA had only recently been discovered, and the emerging view of genes as information encouraged an oversimplified picture of the body as running on a program that could be made more efficient. Yet such confidence has since waned among biologists. While genes carry some of the information that shapes our bodies, many changes in how a cell or a body functions arise from the system as a whole. Genes cannot be understood apart from the living bodies in which they act.
