小中大切換為無襯線

Paternity is poetical

While specific areas are activated at specific moments, it is key to see the brain as a network, and to trace patterns of connectivity between the regions. Both of these studies of creativity – of the guitarists, and of the poets – had a second step. The jazz guitarists were divided between experts and novices; and the poets, who were also compared by level of experience, were scanned twice, once while writing, then again while revising their poems. One characteristic of the experts is that they more effectively suspended the cognitive control mediated by the executive network; in the case of the revising poets, this was then re-engaged. The two processes are working together. David Rosen of Drexel University in Philadelphia, who led the study of the jazz guitarists, emphasises the importance of connectivity between the default and executive networks, which are both implicated in neuroscientific studies of creativity. He writes: ‘Increased connectivity across these networks may enable creative performance in a variety of domains, including music improvisation and creative writing.’
