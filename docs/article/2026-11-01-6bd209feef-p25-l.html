Red Hat's bulletin and AlmaLinux now publish nftables rules that drop incoming IPv4 TCP packets carrying IP options. Red Hat says its rule "reduces exposure to this vulnerability but cannot fully prevent it." The following is Atlas's interim recommendation, the same rule as AlmaLinux's, based on the CNA's scoring notes, which say the attacking peer supplies IPv4 Record Route or Timestamp options in its SYN.
