So I have like a really big circle, and I want to over time just have that radius of that circle go to zero without the curve breaking. And one issue is that let’s suppose that I could do that. One way that I would do that is I would find not just a circle, but a whole disc, so the whole like filled-in region, because that would give me instructions for how to actually shrink the circle. I would just say, “Oh, I’ll shrink that disc down to its center point,” and it’ll bring the circle down to the center point with it.
