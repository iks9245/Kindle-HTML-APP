How Do You Take a Picture of a Black Hole?

DOELEMAN: Yeah. I think the simplest way of saying it is that seeing is believing in a certain way. I mean, you can theorize something, but actually seeing it confers a reality on things that you don’t get otherwise. And there were some really important signatures, too. It wasn’t just seeing it. It was measuring it. Taking the measure of the thing not only gives you confidence in its reality, but you can test predictions.

So in 1915, when Einstein came with his theory of general relativity, which governs the space time around a black hole, and then the year after that Schwarzschild solved it in the trenches of World War I, a story that we know well, even after that, it was pretty clear that if you went to the conclusion of that solution, you would wind up seeing a ring of light around the black hole.
