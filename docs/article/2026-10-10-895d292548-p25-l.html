Allison Kudla

Researchers have now documented many cell-fate decisions that can be described in this way: as changes in gene activity that alter the shape of a landscape. The changes produce new attractors that correspond to stable cell types in the low-lying valleys; unstable gene-activity configurations constitute the high ground.
