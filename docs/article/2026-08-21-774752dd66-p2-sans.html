A vocabulary for the future: touch

In this instalment, the American theoretical physicist and feminist theorist Karen Barad muses on the nature of touch. Drawing partly on their essay ‘On Touching—The Inhuman That Therefore I Am’ (2012), Barad begins with a puzzle from classical physics: touch, strictly speaking, never happens, since what we feel as contact is only electromagnetic repulsion between atoms that never actually meet. Turning to quantum field theory, they reframe the question entirely. If matter has no stable, self-contained identity – if it is entangled with other matter from the very start – then touch is not a failed meeting between separate bodies, but something already constitutive of what matter is. Paired with visuals by the Barcelona-based digital artist Blanca Rego, the result is a mesmerising set of images that, alongside Barad’s words, turn something as ordinary as touch into a doorway into the deepest questions in physics and philosophy.

延伸問答

「未來詞彙」系列是什麼？

「未來詞彙」是巴塞隆納當代文化中心的一個系列，邀請知名思想家反思他們選擇的一個詞彙，並由電影製片人將其文字視覺化。該系列旨在透過重新審視熟悉語言，為更好的未來奠定基礎。

巴拉德對「觸摸」的重新定義對我們理解現實和互動有何深遠影響？

巴拉德的觀點挑戰了我們對物質獨立性的傳統認知，強調萬物從根本上就是相互糾纏和關聯的。這意味著我們與世界的每一次互動，包括看似微不足道的觸摸，都參與了物質本質的構成，而非僅是表面的接觸。

作為一位女性主義理論家，凱倫·巴拉德的女性主義理論如何與她對觸摸的物理學詮釋連結？

文章雖然沒有直接說明兩者連結，但巴拉德的女性主義理論通常強調主體間性和關係性，挑戰傳統的二元對立思維。她對觸摸的重新定義，即物質間的糾纏與構成性，與其挑戰獨立個體存在、強調關係網絡的女性主義觀點不謀而合。
