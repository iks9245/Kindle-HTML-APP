Cosmic amnesia

Though, perhaps forgetting is too simple. In our room, the past gets washed away. It may not be permanent, but thermodynamics makes it expensive to recover. And we do not live in this world of particles. We are never Laplace’s intelligence with a God’s-eye view (and why would we want to be?) Instead, we hear chimes, we notice the smell of perfume, we feel the warmth of a room, not the collisions of incomprehensibly many air molecules. We are given these transient experiences as the Universe drifts towards a vast, featureless expanse. This is the compromise nature offers us: something beautiful that must eventually be lost.
