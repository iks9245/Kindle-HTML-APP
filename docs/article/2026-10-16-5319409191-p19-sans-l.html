Hilbert never publicly conceded that Gödel’s result doomed his programme. He died in 1943; his colleagues had by then been murdered or exiled by the Nazis. His tombstone in Göttingen bears the words he had spoken into the Königsberg microphone: Wir müssen wissen. Wir werden wissen. We must know. We shall know.
