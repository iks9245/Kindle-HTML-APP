Which brings us back to King, in his room, with the clip full. Despite his initial instincts, he did not join his cousin to retaliate. He put the gun down and stayed put. ‘So I got high and I sat right there and just calmed myself down,’ he told us. ‘I shed a couple of tears for my boy and said: “Everything going to be all right.”’ It was the very mantra he had been practising in READI. King’s grief had not abated. What had changed was that revenge no longer felt inevitable. ‘Why I go try to waste my life for nothing?’ he asked. ‘That’s not me no more.’ In the space between feeling and action, King could see another response.
