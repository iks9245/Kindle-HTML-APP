If we can add more radio dishes, we’ll fill in the Earth-sized virtual lens much faster. The number of connections goes as N times N minus one, so there’s a quadratic factor in there. So you don’t just get one more telescope, you get all the connections to that telescope. And also, having more telescopes ensures that as the Earth turns, you always have a critical number to make the next frame in a movie.
