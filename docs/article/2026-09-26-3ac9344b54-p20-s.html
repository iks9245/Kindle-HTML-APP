A factual claim still has to get the world right, whether or not anyone’s around to check it. Moral claims never did: they answer only to discourse. And discourse is exactly what he’s just described coming apart. He doesn’t draw the conclusion for moral and political claims himself, but it follows from putting his two books next to each other: the very thing a claim about what we owe each other had to answer to is the thing he says is disappearing, in an age dominated by exchanges on social media.

People retreat into whichever group agrees with them and lose their grip on a shared standard of true and false
