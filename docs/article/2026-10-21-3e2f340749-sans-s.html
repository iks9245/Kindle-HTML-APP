Permit Pending is a city simulation in the spirit of the classics, with one difference: you are not a god. You are an elected

mayor. Every apartment block, bus lane and factory goes through hearings, reviews, objections, lawsuits and appeals. The economy

underneath is real. The neighbors are not always rational. Win the argument, or win the election. Ideally both.

How a good idea dies

A typical case from your inbox. The economic answer was obvious. The political path was not.

Month 1A tech boom pushes rents up. Young residents start to complain.

Month 3You rezone an empty lot for a 12-story apartment building.

Month 4Filed. Nearby homeowners organize over parking and "neighborhood character."

Month 7Hearing. You trade height for parking. The building is now 6 stories.
