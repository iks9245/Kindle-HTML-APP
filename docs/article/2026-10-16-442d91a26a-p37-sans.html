But this lab setup really allows us to have very precise control over conditions. And so we would do experiments, for example, imagine a plate, a circle that’s broken into three compartments, and one of those compartments has a root system. Then we can add different fungi to either side of the root compartment, and we can add more resources, let’s say, on one compartment than the other, and then actually track how much carbon goes to those different fungal compartments.

Those fungal compartments, sometimes we call it fungal arenas because that’s where all the action happens. And we did this, and we found that plants indeed were able to discriminate between effective and less effective strains, and consistently gave more carbon, more fats and sugars to the fungi that were giving more phosphorus in return.
