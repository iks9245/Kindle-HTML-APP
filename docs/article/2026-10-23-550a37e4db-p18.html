The BBC spoke to one of the doctors in the video, Dr Al-Haj Mohamed Ahmed. He told us he had no choice: "I was forced to be there.

"If I spoke out of line, they'd put a bullet through my head."

He was among several medical workers captured while trying to escape the city and sent back, along with Dr Mustafa Ahmed.

They distinguished themselves by bringing a cholera epidemic in a temporary prison at the paediatric hospital under control and were subsequently taken to Saudi Hospital.
