The new blade also differs from earlier designs in how it uses turbulence to create lift. As air flows over a rotorcraft blade, it forms a thin region called the “boundary layer.” The new airfoil intentionally disrupts the flow of that boundary layer at the front of the airfoil directly at the leading edge to create a disturbance that, counterintuitively, pulls the blade upward to help lift the rotorcraft more effectively than with earlier designs.

Another challenge for rotorcraft is “stall,” an aerodynamic condition in which performance drops sharply and flight becomes unstable following a change in the angle of airflow. Ingenuity’s blades experienced steep performance degradation at stall, but under the conditions studied, the roamx-0201 design showed significantly improved stall behavior. That means future helicopters could operate safely at a wider range of flight angles.

While the technology is not yet ready for Mars, tests are helping engineers understand and resolve discrepancies between computational predictions and real-world performance. Ongoing research across NASA aims to enable more efficient Mars helicopters that can fly farther, carry more payload, and unlock new science and discovery missions.

延伸問答

「機智號」（Ingenuity）火星直升機對NASA的火星飛行計畫有何重要意義？

「機智號」是NASA的首架火星直升機，歷史性地首次在地球以外的星球上實現了動力飛行。它成功證明了在火星稀薄大氣中進行航空探索的可行性，為後續更大型、更複雜的火星直升機任務奠定了技術和操作的基礎。

新的 roamx-0201 葉片設計將如何影響未來的火星探索任務，例如 SkyFall 之後的計畫？

roamx-0201 葉片設計旨在大幅提升火星直升機的性能，使其單次飛行距離能從「機智號」的0.7公里擴展至18.7公里，並能搭載更多科學儀器。這將使未來的火星直升機（如SkyFall之後的任務）能夠探索更廣闊的區域、抵達更多樣化的科學目標，從而解鎖新的科學發現。

相較於地球，火星的稀薄大氣對直升機飛行造成了哪些獨特挑戰，而 roamx-0201 葉片是如何克服這些挑戰的？

火星大氣極為稀薄，導致直升機難以產生足夠的升力，且火星音速較低，易使旋翼遭遇「阻力發散」。roamx-0201葉片透過其更薄、不對稱的創新形狀，以及故意擾亂邊界層以產生額外升力的設計，有效提升了升力。此外，它顯著改善了「失速」性能，讓直升機在更廣泛的飛行角度下也能保持穩定和安全，從而克服了這些嚴峻的挑戰。
