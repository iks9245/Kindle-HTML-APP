Sound guardians

For Abidin, a Balik elder who lives in East Kalimantan in Borneo, Indonesia, the sounds of nature aren’t merely a backdrop but a vital part of how he navigates the world. But as the rainforest around him has become more developed, the calls of creatures once heard everywhere have grown scarce. And that process is speeding up. To relieve Indonesia’s capital city of Jakarta, which is sinking due to overdevelopment and vulnerable to climate change, the government is building a new one, Nusantara, close to Abidin’s home. The noise of construction and city life is beginning to overpower the soundscape he relies on, and researchers suspect they may already be pushing wildlife away.
