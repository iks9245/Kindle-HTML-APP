Patrick Stewart died yesterday, and for people of a certain age, a little bit of the future left with him.

For a certain, less-than-new generation of fans of Stewart’s 1990s television work on Star Trek: The Next Generation, there’s a sorrow that the man behind the larger-than-life Captain Jean-Luc Picard would be lost to a very real, very heartbreaking condition, Alzheimer’s and Lewy Body Dementia, according to his family.
