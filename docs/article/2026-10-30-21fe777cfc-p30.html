So what would it take to embed this shift? My journey with physics began when I was a teenager, and that’s where I believe we need to start now. Too often, we present physics to young people as dry and technical. We teach them equations but not frameworks, facts without wonder. We rarely tell them that physics can help with the messiest parts of life, bound up as they are with uncertainty, resilience, decision-making.

In the UK, we make things worse by forcing students to choose just three or four subjects to study for the final two years of school. In practice, this often forces a split between being on the ‘science’ track or the ‘arts’ track. That false binary deprives us of exactly the kind of multidisciplinary thinkers we need: artists who understand systems, scientists who can tell stories, leaders who can hold both.
