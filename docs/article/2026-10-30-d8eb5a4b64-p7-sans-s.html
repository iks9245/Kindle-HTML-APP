The colony never really thrived. It trended toward compounding caution after the first night. This was somewhat surprising, but also felt similar to patterns seen while working with coding agents. For example, multistep agent reviews can often result in fixating on a specific step or role. Agents also tend to become overly cautious, especially when it comes to security and updating production services. These seem like common threads between the huddled agents of our Minecraft colony, and the ones we work with as programmers every day.
