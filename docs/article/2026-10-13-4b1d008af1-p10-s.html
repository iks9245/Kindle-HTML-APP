Is ‘Attention’ the Essence of Intelligence?

Crucially, no one had to tell a transformer that animals get tired and streets may be wide. The neural network discovers such associations during training, by learning from huge numbers of English sentences paired with their French translations. These associations get built into the weights. The network starts out with random weights that produce unhelpful queries, keys, and values, and thus poor translations. But each translation error sends a correction signal backward through the network that slightly alters the weights that produced it. Through trillions of these adjustments, the network eventually generates useful vectors that yield correct translations, even when it processes sentences it has never seen before.

Attention turned out to be the right mathematical operation for linking words and building context — the fundamentals of translation. And when you can translate blocks of text of unlimited size, it seems you can do anything.
