DOELEMAN: But Janna, you said something really interesting. ’Cause you said, “Well, what if the black hole could be charged?” And I couldn’t agree more. When we unlock the dynamics with these movies, we’re not just gonna see the unpolarized light. We’ll see polarized light, which will trace magnetic fields. So we’ll see not just the shape of the spacetime, but also the morphology, the shape, the configuration of the magnetic fields threading that spacetime. So that would help disentangle these battery models that you’ve come up with which are really cool.

The other thing I would say is that shame on us for just thinking about GR. I mean, there are other objects out there that are not black holes, right? And one of the cool things about the work we’re doing is that we’re beginning to make some of the first tests to rule out these exotic objects that can be black hole mimickers.
