We conclude with a thought experiment. Ramanujan is reported to have said, “An equation has no meaning for me unless it expresses a thought of God” (Robert Kanigel, The Man Who Knew Infinity, 1992). If he were alive today, witnessing the advances of AI in mathematics, would he be perturbed by what they might mean for his purpose as a mathematician?
