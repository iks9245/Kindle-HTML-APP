Is everything conscious?

A more prestigious or better-paid job may constitute a reason to get out of bed in the morning. But deep questions about value and meaning will remain untouched. The absurd is not so easily vanquished. Similarly, the promise of a philosophical system built on the PSR, such as Spinoza’s, may appeal to the extent that we want to ‘solve’ philosophy by providing an answer to any possible question. But we may equally take an explanation of everything as an explanation of nothing.

Instead of seeking perfect reasons for all our pursuits, perhaps we ought to just embrace what we do, whatever it may be, for what it is. As the French philosopher and writer Albert Camus reflected, solace for Sisyphus can only come from positively embracing his situation: ‘The struggle itself towards the heights is enough to fill a man’s heart. One must imagine Sisyphus happy.’
