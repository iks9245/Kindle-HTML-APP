LEVIN: So, Maggie, each time I ask you a question, you say something that I have to ask about. So first it was the homeschooling, and then it was me and my twin sister. Twin sister, an identical twin sister, and is she also a mathematician or an artist?

MILLER: No, we’re fraternal. I think we have a lot in common. We’re still sisters. She is not particularly interested in math or art in the traditional sense. She definitely has a lot of craft activities that she enjoys. My sister went to college and studied psychology, so I think she’s a little bit more interested in maybe social science.
