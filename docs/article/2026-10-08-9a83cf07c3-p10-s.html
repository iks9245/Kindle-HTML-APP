Now Chalmers was thinking about expanding this work. Wouldn’t it be useful, he thought, to synthesise clinical trials in every area of medicine and healthcare? Then doctors and patients would know, based on evidence, effective ways of treating diabetes, cancer, heart disease and many other conditions. Surprisingly, many medical decisions at that time were based on conventional wisdom or the unsubstantiated opinions of senior doctors rather than on evidence from research.
