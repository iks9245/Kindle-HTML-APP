Yes, on a computer. Pick a song and answer "Yes, my guitar", or switch to My guitar at the top of the page. The on-screen keys step aside, the speed goes down to 20%, and Space starts and stops the song. Tune up first with the online tuner.

Browsers only start sound after you touch the page, so tap a chord pad or a string first. Then check the device volume and the tab's sound. Bluetooth headphones can add a delay, and wired headphones or speakers fix it.
