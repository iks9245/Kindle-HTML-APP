GPTs appeared to be much more collaborative and willing to do grunt work. They were more willing to dedicate themselves towards the collective “goal” (winning).

Because GPT models seemed to be more hive-mind-like, they formed much stronger communication networks within the swarm. They communicated with more agents on average, much more frequently. Though a lot of the communication for GPTs was low value (alerts, info, what they did, etc.), Claudes were much more effective with their communication, using it in a way more similar to how humans might communicate in Slack.
