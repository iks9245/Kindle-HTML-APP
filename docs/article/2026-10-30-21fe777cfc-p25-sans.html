小中大切換為襯線

The physics of getting fired

We see the same principle in natural systems. Rivers branch into unequal tributaries. Lungs and blood vessels evolve into networks with a few large arteries feeding many smaller ones. That inequality of size isn’t corruption or greed, but a kind of efficiency. It allows flow. Yet when the flow is blocked, like when a river silts up or a vessel clogs, the system fails. The problem, in other words, is not inequality itself, but unfairness: the obstruction of flow, the stacking of rules, the blocking of opportunity.
