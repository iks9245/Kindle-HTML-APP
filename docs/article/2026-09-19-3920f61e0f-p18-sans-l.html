As early as the 1970s, this characteristic of black holes attracted the attention of physicists. It introduced an undeniable irreversibility into black hole evolution. The areas of black holes are always increasing, never getting smaller. Black holes were hinting at something: another link between our world and theirs. This link is a quantity called entropy.
