The fog of hunger

During the day, we swam, lay on the concrete, and talked about the coming year. My attention kept sliding away from the conversation. A thought would begin and dissolve into the same insistent loop: how long until dinner, how little I could manage when it arrived, whether anyone had noticed what my body was doing without my permission. I remember listening to a teammate tell a long story and realising I had not heard most of it. Something louder had taken the space the story needed.
