It's also a way to learn chords and songs that a chord chart or a video lesson can't show you. Each song plays its best-known part close to the original recording, stroke for stroke. Slow it down to 20% and watch it being played: each string lights up the moment it's hit, the pick swings down and up with the strum, and the neck shows which finger goes where.
