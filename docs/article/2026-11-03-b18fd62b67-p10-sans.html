Still wary of the dubious reputation of cold fusion, I reached out to physicists I trusted to learn more. One was Cătălina Curceanu of the Italian National Institute for Nuclear Physics near Rome. I had visited her three years before to see her experiments testing the foundations of quantum mechanics. She gently reminded me that she had told me about exotic atoms back then, too. I guess I was so focused on her other work that I failed to follow up. “It’s a whole world to discover,” she said. “Sometimes I call them ‘genetically modified atoms,’ but I stopped doing that because, you know.”
