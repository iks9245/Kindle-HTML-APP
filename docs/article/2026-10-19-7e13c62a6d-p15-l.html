- 737,459 lien and foreclosure filings against homeowners in Broward and Miami-Dade countiesThe recorder indexes of Broward and Miami-Dade counties alone list 460,412 claims of lien and 277,047 lis pendens (the filing that starts a foreclosure) by associations. At the peak, in 2009, associations there recorded 46,427 lis pendens — about 127 a day. One community, BEACHPLACE TOWERS CONDOMINIUM ASSOCIATION, INC., has 8,798 claims of lien on file. (Lien indexes are collected for Florida, Illinois and New York only, so this is a count for the covered counties, not a national ranking.)
