So in 2021, I founded an organization called SPUN, which stands for the Society for the Protection of Underground Networks. And it had an audition, a very, very audacious mission, and that was to map the biodiversity of these mycorrhizal networks across Earth and start advocating for their protection.

And so, we really wanted to transform how fungi were being used in climate change strategies and conservation agendas and really in restoration efforts. And so we started this organization that’s now funding close to 140 Underground Explorers all over the world, are studying the fungal communities in some of the most understudied remote ecosystems on Earth and actually working to make these invisible networks visible.
