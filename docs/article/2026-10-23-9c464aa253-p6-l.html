Trump says Musk has restored access to Ukraine's Starlink network

Ukraine first reported an interruption to US intelligence sharing on 14 October.

Musk said last week that the US government ultimately controls access to Starshield, the military-grade version of Starlink which Ukraine uses for some of its operation. He said the system was designed for the Pentagon, suggesting it may therefore be behind the restrictions.

The White House has repeatedly said it did not order access to be restricted.
