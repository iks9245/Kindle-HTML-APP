小中大切換為無襯線

Life on a hair trigger

Violence teaches the mind what to expect and how to answer. Because these are learned habits, they can be remade. A mind formed amid violence is not fixed by it. But few people revise their habits of mind on their own, sitting in traffic and deciding to see things differently. With proper training, people can learn to question their first reading of a situation, to see more than one response available to them, and to stop taking what they have done as proof of who they are. They learn, in other words, that their pasts need not define their present. The capacity is ordinary. What is not is the safety and ongoing support it takes to use it.
