HALLIWELL: Yes. I mean, some of my non-physics friends, or even physics friends, they say, “Well, how is you as a sort of, you know, hard-nosed theoretical physicist, how can you entertain all of these more elusive things that are spiritual or mythological or whatever?”

Well, here’s the thing. You see in physics, of course, we had two major revolutions at the beginning of the 20th century, one in relativity, one in quantum theory. In relativity, suddenly space and time are not even anything like what you think it is. In quantum theory, matter is not what you think it is. So physicists had to get used to adjusting enormously their worldviews.
