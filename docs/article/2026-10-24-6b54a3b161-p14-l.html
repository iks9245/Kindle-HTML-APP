You may think tribology is already a niche field, but we have specialisations within it. Mine was solid lubrication, and I worked on developing and testing materials for jet engines and automobiles. In some cases, the conditions of the end use were so aggressive that the steel I was running my material against would experience enough friction heating to completely change the colour of the steel. That’s how you know you’ve reached the extremes in tribology, but those extremes must be met and they obviously are. After all, we have space stations orbiting Earth and landers on Mars.
