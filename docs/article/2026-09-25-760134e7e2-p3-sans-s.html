小中大切換為襯線

Where a land ethic blooms

The wall of tall green corn that stood here this summer has been transformed by fall and harvest. Little is standing after the field was stripped by a seed-combing combine. The mechanical tornado flails so loudly behind me: wailing against the air, grinding off the root-buttressed corn-stalk stumps, sucking up spent leaves and stripped cobs. In my wake, dry corn litter passes through the chopper like water compelled into frothy turbulence, falling again into an even, silent surface that hides so much activity below.
