Where Does the Quantum World End and Ours Begin?

The original impetus for quantum cosmology is to do with the fact that the Big Bang model is based on quantum matter, but classical gravity. And quantum matter plus classical gravity, as you go backwards in time, as theorems due to Hawking, Penrose, and others showed, it must have a singularity initially, at least classically.

So a region of infinite gravity, infinite curvature. So that suggests that if it is still within the laws of physics, we need a quantum theory of gravity. And so perhaps the beginning of the universe is described by some quantum theory, which includes not just the matter modes, but also some modes of the gravitational field.
