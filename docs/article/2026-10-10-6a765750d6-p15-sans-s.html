She concluded that ‘we have become a single human community’ but warned that ‘Our physical unity has gone far ahead of our moral unity’.

Ward thought that only the Christian West could provide the world with the moral foundations of a planetary community

Technological changes, such as interconnectedness, shrinking space and industrialisation, were not enough to generate a meaningful political change. Ward argued that a planetary community could be achieved only by transforming the physical unity of Planet Earth into political, economic and potentially moral harmony. Yet identifying the appropriate moral foundations for planetary politics was a formidable challenge. She called for the emergence of a common planetary morality to ‘turn the mob into a community’.
