Construction of Building 30 housing the Mission Control Center began in June 1963 and finished in November 1964, with technicians installing the real-time computer in April 1964 and the first employees moving in two months later. The center initially housed two mission operations control rooms to support Gemini and Apollo missions and simulations. By January 1965, the center monitored the uncrewed Gemini 2 mission and the crewed Gemini 3 mission in a flight following mode.

In June 1965, Houston had full control of Gemini IV and has controlled each of NASA’s human space flight missions ever since. The control center received a major upgrade to its computer systems in the mid-1970s to support upcoming space shuttle flights. On April 4, 2011, the building was renamed the Christopher C. Kraft, Jr. Mission Control Center, in honor of the man who created the concepts still in use today in human spaceflight control. The control center continues to support the space station around the clock as well as Artemis missions taking humans back to the Moon.

延伸問答

詹森太空中心目前在國際太空站和阿提米斯計畫中扮演什麼具體角色？

詹森太空中心是國際太空站（ISS）運營的指揮中心，負責監控其日常運作。對於阿提米斯計畫，它負責月球基地計畫和獵戶座計畫，並在任務控制中心支持人類重返月球的任務。

詹森太空中心的任務控制中心在載人太空飛行歷史上具有什麼樣的標誌性意義？

詹森太空中心的任務控制中心自1965年雙子座四號任務以來，一直負責所有NASA載人太空飛行任務的全面控制，是人類太空探索的關鍵中樞。它見證並指揮了包括阿波羅登月、太空梭任務以及國際太空站運營在內的無數里程碑，並持續支援未來的阿提米斯計畫。
