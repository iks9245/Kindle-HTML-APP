Yet, whereas a bell sends sound waves through the air, a black hole sends ripples through spacetime itself, called gravitational waves. Some of these waves fall into the black hole, becoming trapped inside the event horizon. Others escape far away, travelling through space at the speed of light. Eventually, some reach Earth, and we detect them.

An entire planet like Earth might once have fallen in, and the black hole would show no trace
