This is sort of interesting. You can still get an interference pattern, but there’s a classical model for it actually, if the interference isn’t too big. The thing is, interference is created by the fact that the wave goes through two holes, and what come out is two waves. They’re not probabilities, they’re waves that can be all positive and negative, and they can constructively interfere, so they get bigger.
