KIERS: With mycorrhizal fungi, we see them as both like a physical scaffold that holds everything together, and that really drives home this idea of interdependence, right? So many organisms are built upon this scaffold of life. But there’s also this beautiful chemical interdependence, right? They’re moving carbon through the system, and fungi are collecting phosphorus and nitrogen, and there’s this beautiful sort of dependence and movement of resources. And it’s almost like this nested interaction of just so many organisms layered on. Even in the networks themselves, there can be organelles that used to be free-living bacteria inside the networks themselves.
