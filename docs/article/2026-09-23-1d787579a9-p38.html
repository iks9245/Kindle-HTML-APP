I did not set out to become the public-health scientist warning people about the fairy-tale mushroom, sounding alarmist or like a teetotaller. I am not opposed to research on psychoactive substances or to responsible use among adults. If anything, the therapeutic promise of psilocybin is part of why Amanita muscaria bothered me; psilocybin was creating a halo around other mushrooms, and manufacturers were exploiting it in ways that I saw as unethical and dangerous.
