'The city is alive': A brief peace in Kherson, but residents fear what comes next

Videos posted on social media showed people strolling down streets covered in anti-drone nets and gathering in the city's squares - ordinary activities that usually mean keeping one eye on the sky and one ear out for incoming drones.

"Ever since the ceasefire was announced, there's been total silence," said Olga, 42, speaking by phone from Kherson. "We are all, of course, overjoyed."

"I don't even know what to call it," she added, her smile coming through in her voice. "It's as if everything has finally come to an end."
