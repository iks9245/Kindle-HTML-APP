- How much information a layer can store: Nichani, Lee & Bietti, Barnfield et al. for linear stores, and Dugan et al., Garcia et al. for explicit fact-storing MLPs, and Zhong et al. which inspired the interference-to-signal ratio estimates here.

- Hopfield networks and memory stores with exponential capacity: Krotov & Hopfield on dense associative memory, Demircigil et al., Ramsauer et al., who connect it to attention, and the review by Krotov et al..

- Short-term memories that update during inference: fast weights, Mamba, Mamba-2, DeltaNet, Gated DeltaNet, TTT layers, Titans, MesaNet, and test-time regression.

- Huge but sparse parametric memory stores to supplement or replace our MLPs: product-key memories, Memory Layers at Scale, UltraMem, Mixture of A Million Experts, and Engram.
