A New Map of ‘Empty’ Space Challenges a Tranquil Picture of the Galaxy

Galaxies are remarkably inefficient at taking raw material — gas and dust — and turning it into stars. For decades, simulations have struggled to explain why. The new map suggests an answer: Star formation is suppressed by the constant cycle of heating and cooling that churns the ISM.
