Direct conflict regularly flared up. In 1891, in New Orleans in the US, 11 Italians were acquitted of the murder of the local police chief only to be seized by a mob of thousands and lynched. In France in 1893, locals turned on Italian immigrant workers in Aigues-Mortes, reportedly killing eight men, though the number is thought to be far higher. And in 1934, in Kalgoorlie, Australia, anti-Italian killings took place against a similar backdrop of resentment against foreigners allegedly taking jobs.
