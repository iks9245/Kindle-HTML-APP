Sarah said the plane had not been on the ground a minute before it was "surrounded by fire engines and police and a bomb squad". All the passengers were then escorted by police, leaving all their belongings.

"We were only later told that it was an actual threat, a bomb threat to the plane itself," she added.

Two flights heading to Warsaw were also affected, including one from London Heathrow.

The Heathrow flight was originally meant to land at the Polish capital's main airport, Chopin, but was diverted instead to the smaller Modlin airport, briefly forcing its closure.
