Reacting to the result, US President Donald Trump suggested the election had been stolen.

"I didn't take sides, but it looked like another rigged election," he told reporters on Monday, without providing evidence or more details. "It looked very bad to me."

Other world leaders from across the political spectrum have congratulated Lula. Mexico's left-wing President Claudia Sheinbaum said in a post on X: "The people of Brazil won."

Peru's right-wing President Keiko Fujimori praised Brazil's "democratic day". Similar messages were shared by the presidents of Chile and Ecuador.

Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney noted that their two countries had already "flourished under our partnership".
