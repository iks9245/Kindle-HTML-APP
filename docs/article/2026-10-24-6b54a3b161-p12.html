Temperature in particular is a fun variable to contend with, because friction itself leads to temperature changes; indeed, frictional heating can be extreme in some cases. Like with Roman chariots, greases can ignite at certain temperatures making it imperative to control friction heating in order to avoid accidentally reaching that temperature. And for applications like jet engines, which get very hot anyway, we have engineered solid materials with low friction to withstand the heat.
