Regarding water issues, then, Singapore has enjoyed great success. On the other hand, its recent aggressive efforts to ‘grow’ agriculture on the island are more questionable. Their principal manifestation thus far was the government’s now-jettisoned ‘30 by 30’ Plan, issued in 2019, the goal of which was to produce 30 per cent of the food Singapore’s population consumes, by 2030. In 2019, Singapore imported more than 90 per cent of its food, so this goal was ambitious. Perhaps more ambitious still was the additional goal of doing so while still using less than 1 per cent of Singapore’s land for food-producing activities.

Even after adding ‘eating out’, the total spent on food in Singapore is 20 per cent of household income
