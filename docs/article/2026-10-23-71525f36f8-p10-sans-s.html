For us, it took years of intentional conversations, initial enthusiasm from others that then petered out, bureaucratic wrangling, and research. We eventually found people who were equally committed (who were not, interestingly, friends already), and rented together to test it. We learned to navigate sex noises and bathroom smells (sound-proofing and background music helps a lot with the first). We had tense conversations about kitchen storage, our different expectations around spending and cleanliness. It surfaced stuff from our childhoods, which we then had to be honest about and confront. The process (much of which happened during COVID-19 lockdowns, which I’m sure accelerated it) was challenging and exposing, almost unbearably uncomfortable at times.

As I held him at 4 am, my son asked: ‘Why couldn’t we stay in our flat, just us?’
