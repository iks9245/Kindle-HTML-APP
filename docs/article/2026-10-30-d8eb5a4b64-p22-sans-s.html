Multi-Agent Mindustry comes with two environments. One is a best of N series like this run, with teams composed however you like from generic coding-agent harnesses and models, for example 5 Claudes + 5 GPTs against 5 Geminis + 5 Groks in a best of 5. The other, Endless Survival, has one team of up to 20 agents hold off endless enemy waves.

We intentionally do no harness-level customization or special memory work. Each agent gets its own Linux environment, a generic harness and the game’s API, writes scripts and takes notes as it pleases, and relies on the harness’s native compaction (which is quite good). Nobody tells it how to play, so it’s free to specialize, play and (optionally) try whatever it wants. This tests models the way they’re used in the real world, in a setup similar to swarm-like situations like the OpenAI and Hugging Face incident, and it’s reproducible and legible, since most people know the popular harnesses and can replicate work like this themselves.
