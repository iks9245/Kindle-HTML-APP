小中大切換為無襯線

The bombarding of childhood

When my research group compared children’s shows, apps and digital stories, we saw that pace can become a set of design choices. A story can move in a way that helps a child anticipate what comes next, or it can jump so quickly between scenes that cause and effect become blurred. A screen can guide the eye toward one important object, or fill the frame with competing movement.
