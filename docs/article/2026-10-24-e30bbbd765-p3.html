Shyshko said he witnessed and heard Hubbard being beaten by prison guards and electrocuted with a stun gun.

"They beat him on the legs, on the back, on the arms," he said. "Of course, they didn't give him food, they didn't give him medical care. They kept him in the cold.

"He was always treated more harshly, because he was an American. They claimed he was a mercenary, and said 'let's beat him'."

Shyshko, a captured Ukrainian soldier, alleges Hubbard was subjected to sexual humiliation and abuse for the entertainment of the prison staff.

Accounts from the two former Ukrainian prisoners corroborate statements made by Hubbard's family that he was tortured in prison.
