DOELEMAN: Yeah. Black holes simply put are when you confine enough matter into a small enough volume that the force of gravity runs away and the material undergoes catastrophic gravitational collapse into a point of infinite density. And you’d think, “Okay, that’s pretty tiny,” but what happens is that around that is a membrane called the event horizon, and that’s where the gravitational force is so strong that light can’t escape. So that really defines the region of space that we can observe ourselves.
