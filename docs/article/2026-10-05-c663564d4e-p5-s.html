Yemen's government announces all-out war to reclaim land from Houthis

A war erupted in 2014, when the Houthis seized the capital city from the Saudi-backed government, before going on to take control of swathes of the country's north.

Riyadh intervened in the war the following year, leading an international coalition.

After several years of relative calm, the war reignited in July amid the widening conflict in the Middle East.

Last month, a lightning offensive enabled the Houthis to seize the country's entire Red Sea coast, stopping Saudi Arabia from using it as an alternative oil export route to the Strait of Hormuz.

The strategy benefited the Houthis' main sponsor, Iran. But it has also forced Saudi Arabia back into direct military confrontation with the Houthis.
