Let me give you a nice pictorial analogy. There’s a lovely book by, uh, Yakir Aharonov on quantum paradoxes, and on the cover it’s got a picture of a man skiing through a forest in the snow.

And, and that, that is obviously impossible for a skier, but that’s exactly what happens in quantum physics. The electron looks back and notices the tracks in the interferometer where it really has gone through both sides.
