Who, besides myself, could be interested in the answers? Unlike Jerne, I was not a well-known figure – but that was, paradoxically, a virtue. For me, the examined life mattered as a souci de soi, an act of self-care. I thought of the writing exercise as a present-day, healthy ageing counterpart to the late-medieval and early modern ars moriendi – the art of dying well.

I spent three years reading and taking notes, year by year, page by page

And for others, I thought, my autobiography might be of interest as an experiment and an example: I wanted my readers to focus on questions of genre without being distracted by the historical (in)significance of the subject.
