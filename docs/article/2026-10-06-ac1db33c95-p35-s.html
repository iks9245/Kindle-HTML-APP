A life in episodes

The genuinely unsettling discovery was not the buried memory itself, but what I had done with it. I had, in fact, already written an episode connecting that humiliation, explicitly, to the seminar compulsion – and when I sat down to write about the compulsion the second time, I had forgotten the connection completely and even pruned the clue out of my own quotation. I posted the recovery the next day as what it plainly was: a specimen of forgetting, almost in a Freudian sense – that one can write about a painful thing in a detached, factual way and still keep mislaying it, precisely because it is too uncomfortable to hold. The form had caught me, in public and across two days, in the act of reading my own life badly – and only the second reading, prompted by the readers and by going back to the source, set it right.
