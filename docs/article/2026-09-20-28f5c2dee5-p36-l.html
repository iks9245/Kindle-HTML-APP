Tin: full-text search for Postgres

To operate within Postgres, a text search system that assigns sequential identifiers must, at some point, convert those identifiers back into a ctid in order for Postgres to work with it. Both ParadeDB and pg_textsearch maintain a separate data structure just to perform this mapping. If a text search matches 10 million rows, ParadeDB and pg_textsearch have to look up 10 million identifiers in their ctid mappings. TIN avoids that work completely.
