I put this together into a bootable USB and extended upstream Archinstall 4.5 to install the hardware support. The repo has the build scripts and patches, including the steps to dump your own ACPI tables and extract the speaker firmware from ASUS's driver package locally. Mine runs firmware H7607BA.300.

The build inputs are hash pinned. The hardware packages I put together over the weekend are signed with my Arch Linux Package Maintainer key, and the ACPI overrides come from the laptop itself.
