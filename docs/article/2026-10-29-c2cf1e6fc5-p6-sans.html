Even in the carriages farthest away from the impact on the Renfe train "everything was completely destroyed - the seats were detached, and most of the people who were travelling facing the same direction as the train were thrown into the air", according to Salvador Arriza.

"It was something brutal," he said, describing how some people "were trapped between the seats".

Once out, he saw how bad the situation was.

"What caught my attention is that in one of the coaches there was a piece of train track that went through the roof of the coach, and I think that might have helped to stop or reduce the inertia of the train."
