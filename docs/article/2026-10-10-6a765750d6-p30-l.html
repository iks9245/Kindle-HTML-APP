The first step towards devising a strategy for Planet Earth is for the nations to accept a collective responsibility for discovering more – much more – about the natural system and how it is affected by man’s activities and vice-versa.

Financial investment, international cooperation, and scientific expertise could help ‘creep up on the infinitely sensitive issues of divisive economic and political sovereignty’ because ‘No nations, on their own, can offset the risk of deepening disorder.’
