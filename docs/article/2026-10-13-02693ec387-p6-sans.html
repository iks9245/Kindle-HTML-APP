If you come home from work to find a bouquet of flowers on your doorstep, there will be a reason for this. Maybe it’s a birthday present, a congratulations on a new job, a thank you for some good deed. If your car dashboard shows a warning light, you’d be wise to assume that there is some reason for this, such as a problem with the engine, fluids or tyres. The Chicxulub crater, 200 km wide and 1 km deep, in the Yucatán peninsula in Mexico, cries out for scientific explanation, which is precisely what the hypothesis of an extinction-level asteroid impact millions of years ago provides.
