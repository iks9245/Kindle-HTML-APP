A life in episodes

The deepest corrections altered the account of the person I was. In the season about my years as a PhD student, I describe an event relating to a former partner, Lone. We had lived together in Copenhagen in the mid-1970s, but the relationship was troubled, as I remember it, because of her mood swings, which I attributed to her diabetes. I left her for someone else and then stopped thinking about her almost entirely. Years afterward, she suddenly wrote to me, saying she had been evicted from her flat and asked for help. I did not answer. On my 40th birthday, she sent me an envelope lined with red silk paper, empty inside. I did not answer that either. It was only when I began the memoir decades later that I searched for her in the Danish public registry and discovered she had died in her early 40s, alone as far as I could tell. I then remembered I had once promised her I would be there when she died and plant daisies on her grave, and felt guilty about not having fulfilled my pledge.
