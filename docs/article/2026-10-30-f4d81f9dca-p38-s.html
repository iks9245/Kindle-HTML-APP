DOELEMAN: We finally did come with an image of Sag A*, but only after we had overcome the algorithmic problems of how to deal with something that’s changing while you’re looking at it. So we have two of these sources, and we’ve imaged both of them with the Event Horizon Telescope.

And when we laid a ruler across the spacetime of the event horizon, we did verify that for their mass, they’re exactly the size that Einstein predicted, to within the uncertainty of our measurements, which is the best evidence that we have for the existence of supermassive black holes in the centers of galaxies. And also, yay for Einstein. He keeps passing all these tests that we throw at him.
