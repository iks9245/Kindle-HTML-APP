In praise of the dilettante

The further sense of the dilettante’s freedom involves mobility between different practices – a freedom from the barriers dividing them. The dilettante maintains a certain detachment, or intentional separation, between who one is and what one does, to live the life of a cultural itinerant. Painting, gardening, MIG welding, beekeeping, language learning, comic-book collecting, dancing, swimming, printmaking, powerlifting, pickleballing, sourdough baking, accordion playing, brisket barbecuing – all exciting destinations to travel and explore without seeking citizenship or even permanent residence in any one. The dilettante is a wayfaring cosmopolitan whose sense of freedom is found in never being stuck in one place.
