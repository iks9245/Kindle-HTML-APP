Now, that almost wasn’t that surprising, but then we did the reverse experiment where we had two root systems that were connected by a single fungal network, and the root systems gave less carbon or more carbon. And so what you have there is this sort of reciprocal exploitation in a sense, where both partners are very good at discriminating and choosing the correct partner.
