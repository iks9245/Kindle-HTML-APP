Play

No guitar needed. Guitoro is built to be played, not just clicked, on whatever you have in front of you.

Your left hand holds a chord on the letter keys and your right hand strums with H and J, the way you'd play a real guitar. 15 chords sit under your fingers, plus keys for arpeggios, single strings, chucks and palm mutes.
