For more information on ways to use incremental compilation in your own projects, or to learn more about how this feature works under the hood, consider checking out this blog post by a Zig core team member.

Future releases will continue to focus on improving this feature, including introducing a new

Mach-O linker and self-hosted aarch64 Backend with good support for incremental

compilation; adding support for using incremental compilation without --watch; and

fixing any remaining bugs.

The self-hosted SPIR-V backend is now multi-threaded like the other backends.
