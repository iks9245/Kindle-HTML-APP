To avoid such pitfalls, researchers want to devise tests of introspection that can’t be gamed using outside information. Ideally, a language model will only pass these tests if it’s actually aware of its current internal state.

Lindsey set out to devise such a test. He’d been a graduate student in computational neuroscience, using neural networks as tools to model brain processes, when the world began to take notice of language models. “It started to feel like there’s just a lot of value in understanding them for their own sake,” he said. In 2024, after receiving his doctorate, he left academia to study the inner workings of language models at Anthropic.
