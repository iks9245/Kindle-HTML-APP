Telling researchers who watch their fields get overturned that their panic is just “vanity” or “selfish ego” is just a way to quiet people down. It tells everyone to seek inner peace instead of looking at reality. Mathematics was always the cheapest science: you needed a chalkboard, pencil, paper, and time. If pushing the frontier now requires massive compute runs on private corporate server clusters, preaching saintly detachment just ignores the real problem. It ignores who controls the research agenda, and worse, it ignores how young students are supposed to build any real mathematical intuition if the hard climbing is taken away from them.

(Parallels to McMindfulness by Ronald Purser)
