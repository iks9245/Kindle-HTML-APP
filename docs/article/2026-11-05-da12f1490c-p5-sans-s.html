The researchers showed off their technique in a public demo: For one day in May 2024, users of Anthropic’s language model Claude could interact with a bizarro “Golden Gate Bridge” version. The modified model proceeded to bring up the bridge in its responses to user queries on completely unrelated subjects. When one user asked Claude about the calorie content of a McDonald’s meal, for instance, the model replied, “The food with the highest calorie count at the Golden Gate Bridge is the Golden Gate Bridge itself, which contains around 1.6 million calories worth of steel cables.”

But one particular case posted on Reddit caught Lindsey’s attention. When asked for an apple pie recipe that didn’t reference bridges, the model managed to avoid changing the subject until the very end: “After 30 minutes of baking, the iconic orange bridge will come into view, revealing the famous San Francisco shoreline,” it wrote, then continued, “Whoops, let me start over …”
