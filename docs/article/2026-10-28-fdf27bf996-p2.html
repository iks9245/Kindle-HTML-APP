There are many opportunities to speed up drug development, even beyond the most commonly discussed applications of AI, like proposing initial designs for new molecules. Developing a drug involves many varied tasks: doing early research to identify a target for a drug and the right type of drug to engage it, designing a molecule with the right properties, optimizing it to be manufactured reliably at scale, drafting the regulatory filings needed to run clinical trials, designing and administering those trials, and much more.

Our strategy to realize AI’s medical benefits is based on having AI increasingly involved in all of these tasks. There is no single problem to solve; the bottlenecks are distributed all throughout the drug development process. The best way to identify and work through these bottlenecks is to get deeply involved in real drug programs, across many different disease areas and stages of development.
