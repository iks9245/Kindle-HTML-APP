STROGATZ: Uh-huh. You guys in your conversation mention that the moon just happens to occlude the sun. It just happens that the moon is the right size, given its distance and given the sun’s distance and given the sun’s size, that it can block the sun, but not totally. Lets you see the corona, okay.

Do you think that this might have something to do with the human belief in miracles and gods? That because early people might have observed solar eclipses, that it’s such a bizarre coincidence and so, so what? So scary that the sun goes black at some point. Like maybe that gave us a sense of wonder as a species, and if the astronomical coincidence hadn’t been there, maybe we wouldn’t have ever started asking why?
