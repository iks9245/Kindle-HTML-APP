Police used tear gas to disperse crowds after protesters hurled bottles and flares. No injuries or arrests were reported.

Rail drivers went ahead with a planned strike on Thursday, with the Spanish Union of Railway Drivers (Semaf) saying the strike action was being held "in defence of railway safety".

The leader of Spain's main opposition People's Party Alberto Núñez Feijóo said: "Spain has the right to know what is happening with its infrastructure."

At the crash site, emergency crews worked through the night under powerful floodlights to cut open metal carriages in the search for victims, with many of those killed in the crash still to be identified.
