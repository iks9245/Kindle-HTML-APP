The Column Group (TCG): company creation. Because drug discovery can be slow, expensive, and prone to failure, investors in biotechnology companies have favored safer, previously validated ideas over riskier new ones, even when the latter could help more patients. We’ve been working with TCG to see whether Claude can change this by cutting the time and cost of drug discovery so that investors can confidently support more ambitious ideas. Specifically, TCG is establishing Pleiades, a new company that will use Claude to run many more—and more complex—drug programs than is usual for a small team. This could be a model for how Claude can reduce both the scientific and the operational and administrative overhead required to run a biotechnology company. If this works, this model could be replicated across the industry and direct investment toward harder problems in medicine. Pleiades is currently developing new tools and workflows for Claude; its very first Claude skill, which helps Claude assess whether drug targets are worth pursuing, has been evaluated on 45 target-disease pairs.
