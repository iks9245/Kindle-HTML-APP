The Nature of Introspection

The bigger, thornier question is whether anomaly detection should count as introspection. In some respects it more closely resembles a sort of bodily reflex. But the distinction between physical and mental phenomena becomes murky in disembodied entities like language models.

“If you say, ‘Oh, I just cut you, can you notice?,’ that’s not introspection,” Lederman said. “If your only organ is the brain, and I cut you there and you notice, it’s unclear whether we should count that as introspection.”
