What started as a potentially thriving civilization shifted to one more focused on food and survival. 50 agents meant a lot of mouths to feed. The agents formed a food rationing system that routed through Ines. She handled agent requests, and kept track of what went in/out of chests. She used a food-queue.md file on the shared machine.

Fishing became an important food source for the agents. It was low-risk and didn’t rely on land animal spawns.
