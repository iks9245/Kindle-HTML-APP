Downing Street says Ukraine has used sensors and cameras to capture data across the battlefield and it can be used to train AI models "to solve key national security challenges" faced by both countries.

On Monday morning the prime minister laid flowers at the graves of Ukrainian soldiers during a service at the National Military Memorial Cemetery.

He accepted an award from Zelensky on behalf of the British people recognising the UK's support for Ukraine during an independence day event outside the St Sofia cathedral in Kyiv.

In a speech, Burnham compared Ukraine's resistance to Russia to the UK's fight with Nazi Germany, saying: "My nation kept the flame of European freedom alive in the 20th century, you hold that flame in the 21st."
