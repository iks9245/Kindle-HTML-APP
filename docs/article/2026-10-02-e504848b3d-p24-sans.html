So in dimension three, we have a lot of intuition for what should be true, because we live in a space that seems to be three-dimensional. That doesn’t necessarily help with super-abstract problems, but at least simple behavior, you can probably guess what will happen just based on your lived experience.

But dimension four is different and hard to conceptualize. I have a lot of papers where the format of the paper is, “Here’s something that’s true in dimension three, and here’s an example of how it’s not true in dimension four.” I think that’s a fun thing to explore.
