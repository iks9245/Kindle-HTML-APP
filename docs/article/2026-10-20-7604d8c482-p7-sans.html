In the central city of Limoges, the ornate 19th Century city hall was gutted by a fire started by rioters on Saturday, its clock tower and roof collapsing as the flames took hold.

The US Embassy in France has issued an alert advising its citizens to avoid areas affected by the recent demonstrations.

The warning says to "exercise caution" if they find themselves close to large gatherings. China and Germany have issued similar warnings.

Some politicians have accused the left-wing La France Insoumise party of playing a role in the violence, with Lecornu alleging in an earlier news conference that some groups "want to create chaos in our country up until the presidential elections".
