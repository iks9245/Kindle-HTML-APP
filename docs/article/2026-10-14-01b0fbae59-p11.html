Features

The most complete virtual guitar online: four instruments, every key, real chord shapes, songs, a backing band and recording, all free in one place.

Steel-string acoustic, classic guitar, electric and four-string bass, each built from recorded samples. Pick a recording for the acoustic (Warm, Bright or Mellow) and the classic (Natural or Concert), and presets for the electric, from Clean to Crunch, Classic Rock, Metal, Lead and Ambient.

Pick any of the 24 major and minor keys and the chord keys fill with the chords that fit it: the main chords, sevenths, and sus, add9 or sus2 colors. Edit puts any chord you type, like F#m7 or Dsus2, on a key.
