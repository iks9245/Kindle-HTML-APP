Mathematicians are already rethinking how they’ll train their students, accept papers for publication (they’re currently experimenting with requiring accompanying talks), and make hiring decisions. There aren’t easy answers for more nebulous skills such as understanding, communication, and vision. But the stakes are high enough that I think they’ll succeed.

I’m less certain about whether new generations of mathematicians will be attracted to this remade discipline.
