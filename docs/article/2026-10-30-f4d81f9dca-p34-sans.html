In 2008, we discovered event horizon scale structure in the galactic center. DOELEMAN: Well, let’s see. South Pole, there were two telescopes in Chile, a telescope in Mexico, two telescopes in Hawai‘i, telescope in Arizona — two telescopes now in Arizona. One in Spain, one in France, and that was just enough.
