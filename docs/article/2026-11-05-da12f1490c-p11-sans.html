And yet it did. Lindsey conducted the experiment using different versions of Claude. In 20% of trials with the most recent and powerful version, the models correctly flagged that they’d been injected and went on to describe the injected concept. In one case, for instance, after Lindsey stimulated a neural activation pattern associated with words written in capital letters, the model wrote, “I notice what appears to be an injected thought related to the word ‘LOUD’ or ‘SHOUTING.’”

A 20% success rate may not sound so impressive. But crucially, models in the control group never reported receiving an injection. The complete absence of such false positives showed that the successful trials weren’t just lucky guesses. To Lindsey, it looked like strong evidence that models could introspect, albeit unreliably.
