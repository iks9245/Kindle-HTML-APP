'I've never been to Afghanistan': Six million deportees forced to start over under the Taliban

"I don't leave this house," a former Afghan security guard, first deported from Iran and then Pakistan, nervously tells us when we meet him far from the border in what he hopes is a safe house.

"We are all living in fear including my wife and children who are also afraid to go outside."

He dismisses the Taliban government's amnesty, announced days after their return to power which declared that former government officials, security personnel and civil servants would not face retaliation and were encouraged to return to work.

"I have no trust in their amnesty because many of my colleagues have been killed." He says his case had been registered with the UN but was deported before he could be transferred to a safer country.
