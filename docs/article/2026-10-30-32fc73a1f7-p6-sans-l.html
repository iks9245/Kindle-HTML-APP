'The city is alive': A brief peace in Kherson, but residents fear what comes next

Though Kherson residents are clear-eyed about what lies ahead, for a brief period this week the city caught its breath.

No violations have been reported on either side since Tuesday. There have been other ceasefires in the past, but these were generally not respected, Kherson resident Dima said.

"This is the first time the shelling has stopped, people don't quite believe it," he told the BBC. "They are going to the shops, drinking coffee, walking around... The city is alive."
