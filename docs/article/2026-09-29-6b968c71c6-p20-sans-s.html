Of the group, Robert Lowell is perhaps the purest example of a father poet – because of his enormous skill, and the drama of his life, and because the change in his own style is so stark. At first, Lowell wrote like this:

And like this:

And colorless as seaweed or the floats

At anchor off New Bedford. We were shut

In gardens where our brassy sailor coats …
