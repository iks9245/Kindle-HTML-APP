READI offered men a chance to discipline their inner worlds. The 18-month intervention paired a part-time job with cognitive behavioural sessions, held three to five mornings a week, that participants called ‘CAD’ (Control-Alt-Delete, the keystroke that reboots a computer). The lessons taught participants how to slow down to reconsider an automatic reaction and widen the range of responses in a tense moment. They learned that they did not have to be ruled by their first feeling or thought. In doing so, they began to loosen the identity that violence had formed
