And they actually make a beautiful supply chain because what you’re imagining —and I want you to really close your eyes and picture this — is that the plant is pouring carbon down into this root system, and the carbon, which now we can label with a fluorescent label, so we can actually watch it travel through this open-pipe system all the way down to the tips where the fungi use the carbon to keep building their network. Now, as they’re building that network, they’re collecting the phosphorus and the nitrogen in the water that they’re moving in an opposite direction back to the root system. Now, one of the most fascinating things about this symbiosis is that this is an open-pipe system, yet it has flows moving in two directions at the same time.

So it’s like a river going north and south at the same time. So when you say picture it, it’s actually one of the most beautiful things I’ve ever seen. And the biophysicists when they saw it for the first time, they said, “ This just can’t be true. How do they do it?”
