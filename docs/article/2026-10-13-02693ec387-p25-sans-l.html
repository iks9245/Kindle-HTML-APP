Is everything conscious?

There is another route to panpsychism via the PSR. The Norwegian philosopher Hedda Hassel Mørch argues that fundamental consciousness is required to properly explain the basic motions of matter, documented by fundamental physics. Science tells us plenty about how matter behaves and what different subatomic particles such as protons, electrons and neutrons do. But it does not tell us what matter is. This is evident from the way in which science characterises matter. The electron, for example, is characterised in terms of its properties: mass, charge and quantum spin. And these properties, in turn, are understood in terms of how they dispose the electron to behave. What is absent from this scientific description of the world is an account of what physical properties are like in themselves, and hence why they have the power to dispose matter to behave in certain ways.
