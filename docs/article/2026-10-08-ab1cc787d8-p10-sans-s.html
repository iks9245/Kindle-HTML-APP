We approached several members of his Likud party for an interview, without success. We also requested that the party respond to their critics, and are waiting for their response.

Netanyahu's denial of responsibility has now become an election issue, as he prepares to run for re-election later this month.

A video game called Sleepy Bibi, released by the team of his main opponent, Gadi Eisenkot, asks players to help the prime minister avoid responsibility by dodging warnings of an attack and "making it to bed safely".

Netanyahu has styled himself for decades as Israel's "Mr Security", and despite presiding over the worst security failure in Israel's history, is running on the same security platform again.

For the past three years, he focused on waging new wars against Israel's enemies - Hamas, Hezbollah and Iran.

Polls suggest a close race, and that the 7 October attacks are central to many voters' decisions.
