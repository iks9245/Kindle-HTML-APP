Scholes wondered if the emergence of special synchronized states in complex networks, like flocks of birds or schools of fish, might produce quantumlike behavior.

Strip away the physics from a quantum state, and the mathematical object you’re left with is a vector — an ordered list of numbers that acts like coordinates for a location in a mathematical space of possibilities. Quantum states are represented in a particular kind of vector space called a Hilbert space.
