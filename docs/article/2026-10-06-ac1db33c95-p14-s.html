A life in episodes

What is new in my project is the move onto social media. For me, this was no great leap: a decade before I began the memoir, I had begun blogging, and later Tweeting, extensively about the museum’s transformation. So, when I embarked on my autobiography, writing it on social media felt entirely natural.

I chose Facebook – it lends itself to longer texts, and it was where many of my old friends and colleagues were already. I posted several times a week: striking memories, interesting documents I had read, engaging conversations with old schoolmates, and, not least, spontaneous reflections on genre. I wrote in Swedish, my mother tongue, in my adopted Danish, and in English, as the mood took me. The posts formed a florilegium: a gathering of flowers in the original sense of the word. Gradually, I accumulated friends and followers, who began responding to the life I was putting before them. A dialogue was quietly getting under way.
