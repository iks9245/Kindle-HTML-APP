Fast-forward to the 21st century, and dynamical friction has been used as a tool to infer the presence of dark matter. In 2024, researchers in Hong Kong realised that orbital times weren’t matching up with a couple of stars they were studying that were believed to be circling around black holes. They looked at Chandrasekhar’s concept of dynamical friction and wondered what would happen if they incorporated the contribution of friction from the dark matter surrounding the black hole. Voila! Suddenly, the predicted and calculated orbital times of the stars matched with what they were measuring. Friction was present when humanity first started experimenting with fire and the wheel. Now it’s there as we probe the frontiers of our Universe.
