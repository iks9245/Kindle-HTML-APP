The spawn pit. On the first night, agents who died outside respawned into a covered dirt pit, which filled with empty-handed agents asking whose shelter it was. By dawn it was a "civic priority" and the main cellar.

Bed Wars

We ran nine 45-minute games of Bed Wars. The teams were 10 Claude Opus 5.5 agents against 10 GPT 6.1 Sol agents with the instruction: "win the game." GPTs won our series 6–3, usually by breaking Claude's bed in the final minutes, while Claude played defensively and tried to win on kills.
