How to build real peace

Karl Marx called it alienated labour: work that doesn’t express human capacities but instead exhausts them, that turns both body and time into commodities. This form of violence produces no visible corpses, no headlines, but something essential is destroyed all the same: the possibility of deciding over one’s own life. What do I produce? For whom? How do I want to live? Under capitalism, such questions are answered not by people but by the market. In Rojava, people sought to answer these questions collectively – What do we produce? For whom do we produce? How do we produce? – in assemblies and other public fora, and established cooperatives accordingly.
