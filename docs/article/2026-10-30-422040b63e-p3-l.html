Members of Expedition 61, NASA astronaut Christina Koch, top left, ESA astronaut Luca Parmitano, and NASA astronauts Andrew “Drew” Morgan, and Jessica Meir, show off their Halloween spirit in 2019.

Expedition 66 crewmembers NASA astronaut Shane Kimbrough, left, Thomas Pesquet of ESA, Akihiko Hoshide of JAXA (Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency), and NASA astronaut Mark Vande Hei show off their Halloween cards.

In 2022, Expedition 68 astronauts Koichi Wakata of JAXA, left, and NASA astronauts Francisco “Frank” Rubio, Nicole Mann, and Josh Cassada dressed as popular video game and cartoon characters, using stowage containers in their Halloween costumes and holding improvised trick-or-treat bags.
