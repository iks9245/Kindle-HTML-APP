Reason is more than a tool

For Kant, reason is sovereign because it is the power by which we become answerable to standards that no private appetite, market incentive, algorithmic recommendation or political authority may dictate. It is the power of not being owned by the given, the power of being able to question, to criticise, to think up something new. But that power, just like the ‘human values’ invoked in alignment talk, waxes and wanes and can be realised only through our courageous efforts, through our own belief in it. It is precisely this belief that our dependence on AI threatens to undermine at every turn.
