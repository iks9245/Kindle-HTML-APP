We followed Bajash as he rushed through the corridors, trying to get the all-important admissions slip. He found an ally, a forceful supervising nurse, Aisha Ragab, who had run from the war herself, back in 2018.

"I feel for them," she said, "because I have already lived through the situation myself."

Two beds could not be found, but Aisha was undeterred.

"I will settle this by having the twins share a bed... so that we can save them. By God, I will convince them [the doctors]."

Within an hour the twins were lying on a single bed, their tiny bodies taking up little space.

Lina was feeding them sips of fortified milk from a tiny bottle, but Raad could not keep it down. He was cradled in her arms - his eyes wide and his cheeks sunken. Asked if she thought her twins would recover, Lina replied quietly, "God willing."
