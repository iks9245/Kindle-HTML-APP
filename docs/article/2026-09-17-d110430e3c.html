The meaning of fake marble

Humans have prized marble for its colours, patterns, texture and heft for thousands of years. In this video, Mary McGillivray, a digital creator and PhD student at the University of Melbourne, explores how, for as long as we’ve prized it, we’ve been inventing ways to fake it. McGillivray traverses Europe to investigate how, from ancient Rome to modern home-improvement stores, we’ve developed techniques to cast, plaster and paint imitations of the metamorphic rock. And, as McGillivray argues, this process has been about more than finding a cheap alternative to a luxury material. Rather, the millennia-long history of our fascination with marble, and of our attempts to recreate it, has much to say about how humanity is inspired by, and competes with, the natural world.
