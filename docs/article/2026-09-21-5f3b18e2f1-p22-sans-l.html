- Paid security. Tidelift's whole model was paying maintainers to keep their packages secure and selling the assurance to companies, which is a good idea, and in December 2024 it got acquired by Sonar, which is what happens to good ideas that can't find enough buyers on their own. OpenSSF's Alpha-Omega gives out five or six million dollars a year, mostly to fund security staff inside foundations, which is sensible but small.
