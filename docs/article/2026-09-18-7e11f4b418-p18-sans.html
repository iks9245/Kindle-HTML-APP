The dominance of data permeates software down to individual user interface (UI) components: a five-star rating system, a text string, a dropdown, a hashtag. Experiences are collapsed into bits. Actions like ending a relationship or talking to your parents are now charged with social subtext based on what software you might use to perform them. Software’s ability to observe, model and respond to the user is turned toward creating or augmenting rather than facilitating the user’s intent.
