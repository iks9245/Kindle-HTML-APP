Ukraine says bodycam video shows deadly Russian double-tap bombing

- Published

A bodycam video has been released by Ukraine police which they say shows the moments between two powerful Russian bomb strikes that killed at least 40 people at an aid centre and a shelter.

The footage, from a camera on one of the killed police officers, shows residents of the southern village of Kamyshuvakha going to an underground shelter after the first attack on Monday. Soon after a blast is heard and flames are seen as the shelter is hit.

President Volodymyr Zelensky said more than 30 people may still be under the rubble, as a search operation continued for the third day.

Nine gliding bombs, some weighing 3,000kg, hit the village as people had gathered to get humanitarian aid, Ukraine said.
