Both US President Donald Trump and UAE officials have indicated to the media that Iran may be responsible for the incident, without definitively blaming Tehran.

Trump told reporters on Thursday that Iran would be "hit very hard" if it was deemed to have been involved in the attack.

Pilots from countries without diplomatic relations with Israel were usually not allowed to fly there, Netanyahu said when asked about how the suspected Omani pilot came to be on the plane.

He added: "Obviously there was a loophole here and we're taking care of that among other things... with the full co-operation, I have to say, of the Emirati government, so we're both interested in making sure this doesn't happen again."

Oman has not formally established official ties with Israel, unlike the UAE.

The prime minister was also asked earlier on the same network whether there was an indication if Iran was involved, he replied: "It's too early to say".
