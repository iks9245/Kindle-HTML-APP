The US government has not commented.

While both companies are owned by Musk's SpaceX, Starlink is a civilian service that uses satellites in low Earth orbit to provide internet access, particularly in remote areas, while Starshield is designed for government and military use.

The restrictions on Tuesday night came as Russia launched deadly attacks against Ukraine that killed several people in Kyiv, officials said, and hit communications and energy facilities used by Ukraine's military.

Zelensky said it was "one of the largest attacks" from Russia, and while "a significant part of the drones were shot down" there were "also hits".

At least 600,000 people across Ukraine were left without electricity, and residents said they received no warning of incoming drones before they hit.
