This is where I part with Wallace. He was right that we can choose how to read a situation, but he underestimated what such a choice requires. For the men we interviewed, seeing differently was not just a private act of will performed alone in traffic. It was something they practised repeatedly with others, under conditions of safety, enough time and skilled support.

‘I didn’t know that if you yelled at me, I was gonna break down. But now I [see]: it’s not you, it’s a past trigger’
