Influential models of drug addiction describe a progressive shift from prefrontal control to the striatum, a deep part of the brain involved in actions that become automatic over time. As such, addiction is a change from thoughtful self-command to more habit-driven control. Nowadays, addictions might not come only in the form of obvious candidates like alcohol and nicotine, but also smartphones and chatbots. The bodily gifts that allow us to learn can train us into patterns that diminish our freedom to act otherwise.
