1:15 p.m.: International Space Station briefing for Crew-12 return and SpaceX Commercial Resupply Services-35 launch with the following participants:

- Bill Spetch, deputy manager of Commercial, NASA’s Low Earth Orbit Program

- Dr. Liz Warren, deputy chief scientist, NASA’s Low Earth Orbit Program

- Lee Echerd, senior mission manager, Customer Operations and Integration, SpaceX

- Andreas Mogensen, leader, Human Exploration Group, ESA

To participate virtually in the teleconference, media must contact the NASA Johnson newsroom for call details by 12 p.m., Oct. 8, at: jsccommu@mail.nasa.gov or 281-483-5111. To ask questions, media must dial in no later than 10 minutes before the start of the call. The agency’s media credentialing policy is available online.
