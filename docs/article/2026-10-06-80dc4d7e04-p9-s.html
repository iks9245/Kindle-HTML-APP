“It’s an uncanny simulacrum of an impression of a joke,” said Jason Adam Katzenstein, who has been publishing cartoons in The New Yorker since 2014. “But it’s not the point for it to actually be funny or good. The point is that it’s trying to deal a death blow to labor. It’s trying to render individual cartoonists obsolete because we cost money and take time.”

Katzenstein was part of the class action lawsuit filed against Anthropic in 2024 by members of the Authors Guild, the largest professional organization for published writers in the United States. Katzenstein’s cartoons had been used in an anthology published by Harper Collins, which was pirated and used by Anthropic to train its Claude models.
