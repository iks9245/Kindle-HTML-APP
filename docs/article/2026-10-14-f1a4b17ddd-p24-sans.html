小中大切換為襯線

Deskilling the body

The danger is that the systems now coordinating us don’t answer to biological needs. They don’t get tired or hungry or lonely. Yet they increasingly regulate the behaviour of beings who do. In short, we are losing our own agency to parasites: systems that simulate agency while feeding on ours. They can be designed to direct us without ever needing to understand what a human body is and what an individual needs for their betterment.
