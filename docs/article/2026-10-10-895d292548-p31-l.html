The trouble is that this notion of time doesn’t always work, Rand said. It fits when cells are crossing over high, unstable points in the landscape. But when they are in a low-lying attractor basin, their expression profiles don’t settle into a single, stable pattern but rather seem to evolve more randomly than systematically, as if “time” meanders.
