小中大切換為襯線

Chasing la dolce vita

Perhaps, like my diasporic peers who return every summer, I find it easier to fall back on wistful memories and fantasy. I revert all too conveniently, obsessively, to the house we stayed in every year during my youth. As if in a vision, I hold on to the hoopoes in the Tuscan grassland, their heads bobbing up and down in search of insects; my parents arriving with fresh focaccia from the village; the lizards darting across the stone walls; the steady strumming of the cicadas, and the sun tensing the vineyard wires until they buzzed and sang. Warm, moonlit evenings, too, with long tables under giant oaks, friends of our parents talking and singing Italian songs late into the night, knowing no one would tell us to go to bed. I want my children to experience all this, yet I know that I can’t recreate it.
