By late 2024, the US Food and Drug Administration (FDA) had also reached its own conclusion about Amanita muscaria in food, offering a perspective that confirmed my own. In a scientific memorandum, FDA toxicologists concluded that Amanita muscaria and three of its active constituents, including muscimol, do not meet the criteria for safe use in food in the US.
