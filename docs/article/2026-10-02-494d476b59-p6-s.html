The death of web development education

Rachel Andrew is a renowned technical writer and editor of many books and articles on web topics. In a series of blog posts, she reflects on the impact of GenAI on technical publishing. She reports that GenAI has broken the relationship between subject matter experts – the authors of the original draft – and editors who proof-read, fact-check and polish the article.

Editors have to apply extra scrutiny because AI tools might have introduced subtle inaccuracies without the original authors being aware of it. AI can be very confidently wrong, and if the text seems clear, it’s possible to miss that it’s clearly nonsense.

Authors want to save some time, but create additional work for the editing team.

Andrew sees LLMs as a useful tool but emphasizes that its field of application is very limited and its output needs to be reviewed by people who deeply care about the end result.
