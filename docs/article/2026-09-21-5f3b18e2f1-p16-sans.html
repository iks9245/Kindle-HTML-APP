- Corporate generosity. Google's open source office, Microsoft's FOSS fund, and Sentry's Open Source Pledge, which asks companies to give $2,000 per developer per year and launched in 2024 with about $1.3 million committed. I like the Pledge. It has the same flaw as everything else on this list, which is that it's charity, and charity does not scale to trillions. Dan Lorenc, who spent years at Google and OpenSSF trying to give money to maintainers, said they had more money than they could give away and it didn't fix anything. I think that's one failed attempt rather than a law of nature, but it does go on the list of failed attempts.
