And again, this is just one observation. but it was really beautiful to see the intricacies. It’s almost like looking into the inner lives of these organisms. I mean, how beautiful that they’re transparent, first of all, that we can even shine light through them and see what they’re doing. It’s really an invitation in many ways to understand them on their own level, on their own way. Sometimes I describe it as being like a primatologist. There’s so much observation involved because you’re just watching these behaviors in real time. And unlike bacteria, which you know multiply and you have to wait to see what happens, when you have a network growing on one of these Petri plates under the microscope, you can interact with it in real time. You can get down very close to the Petri plate, and they’re so exquisitely sensitive to the outside environment that just your breath changes the direction of the flows.
