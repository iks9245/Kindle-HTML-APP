The area around the hospital that was hit was "very crowded because it was the only working hospital in the city providing surgical services", he says.

On that day medical staff operated on 16 injured patients, he added.

The attacks culminated in an RSF raid on 8 June. Hospital staff were taken by surprise - many of them escaped over the back wall as the RSF fighters entered the building, says Dr Mustafa Ahmed, a pharmaceutical specialist.

But the director general of the hospital, surgeon Izzeldin Ahmed Dawaud Asow, encountered a group of the paramilitaries outside his office.

They did not know he was a doctor, he said, but denounced him with ethnic slurs.
