Our community (and other lasting ones) hold together by turning attention beyond ourselves

Why, then, are experiments like ours so rare? Why do I, a public advocate, a recipient of the very real benefits of communal living, still feel conflicted about arguing more strongly for it to become the norm? It is partly that such a life is, as Murdoch says, painful. Decentering my preferences, letting go of my self-aggrandising illusions hurts. These are growing pains, every one worth it – but still. Inviting others into something this rigorous, and costly, is not in fashion.
