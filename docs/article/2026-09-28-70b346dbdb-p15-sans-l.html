小中大切換為襯線

Inside Yemen's front-line city as Houthis battle for control

Fatma Mohammed Saleh was lying on an empty rice sack outside another family's tent. She has a disability and cannot walk, and told me she had nowhere of her own to sleep: "I have nothing… no shelter, no mattress, no family. Nothing."

Fatma said she had been with her two sons and their children when the fighting reached their village. But she was slowing them down so told them to take the children and run, she said.

She spent the next two days crawling on her hands and knees, she said.
