Deskilling the body

Ordinarily, as we walk, optic flow – the shifting images on our retinas as we move through our surroundings – enables our bodies to sense our movement relative to the world. Receptors in our muscles, tendons and joints detect the movement of our limbs. Signals from the inner ear add information about location and orientation. Together with landmarks and memories of past journeys, these signals help us work out where we are and how to reach our destination. It’s a vast, coordinated sensorium that takes us across the landscapes of our world.
