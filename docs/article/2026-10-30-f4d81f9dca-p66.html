DOELEMAN: Yeah, yeah. Now the cool thing is that imagine there was an astronomer on the other side of the galaxy. They would see a ring too, because they would see all the light from where we are lensed around in their direction. So any civilization, and we can talk about different civilizations in the Milky Way if you want to, but anyone in the Milky Way galaxy would be seeing the same thing we are.

So if anyone’s curious about testing Einstein’s theories or whatever theories have been concocted by another civilization, they would be curious about this same object that we’re looking at.
