The vigilance that keeps someone alive at one point can generate danger at another

Among the men we spoke with, these shortcuts took their shape from a world that taught a brutal lesson early: people are dangerous, and hesitation can get you hurt. The lesson came from somewhere. The sociologist Elijah Anderson in 1994 called it the ‘code of the streets’. Where safety is not a given, a reputation for violence becomes the protection people can supply themselves. The frame comes first: this is a threat. The script that follows, I have to fight back, is learned from experience and reinforced by others.
