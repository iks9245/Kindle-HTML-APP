The safety profile, accessibility, and potential for synergy with existing treatments make circadian interventions an attractive addition to the ADHD treatment. As the field advances toward precision medicine approaches, circadian phenotyping may prove essential for treatment selection and optimization. Further research is needed to fully elucidate the bidirectional relationships between circadian disruption and ADHD symptoms, identify biomarkers for treatment selection, and establish optimal long-term management strategies.
