I take that argument seriously. Western medicine does not own all wisdom, and therapeutic approval pathways are limited. Many plant medicines will struggle through conventional drug approval because they cannot be easily patented, because no company has an incentive to fund the studies, or because they belong in a ceremonial setting that is not transferable to a randomised trial. If the only knowledge we recognise is knowledge that can be monetised through drug development, we will miss a great deal.
