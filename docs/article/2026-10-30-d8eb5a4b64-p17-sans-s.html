Because GPT models seemed to be more hive-mind-like, they formed much stronger communication networks within the swarm. They communicated with more agents on average, much more frequently. The language of GPTs within swarms seemed to get compressed and more specialized as time went on, forming their own nicknames for events and things that the entire hive abided by.
