Editor’s Note: This article was originally published on Oct. 15, 2026, and has been updated to correct the location of the flights.

A NASA Dynamic Aviation A200 aircraft will conduct low-flying research flights over farmland near Amarillo, Texas, from Thursday, Oct. 22, through Monday, Nov. 9. These flights will measure gaseous and particulate emissions from animal feeding operations.
