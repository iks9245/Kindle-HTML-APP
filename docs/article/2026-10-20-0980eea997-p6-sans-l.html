The backdrop to these developments was the decades of war in which France and Spain contended for hegemony on the Italian Peninsula. In the 1480s, while pitching his services to the duke of Milan, Leonardo da Vinci, highlighting his potential as a military engineer, wrote:

I have also plans of mortars most convenient and easy to carry with which to hurl small stones in the manner almost of a storm; and with the smoke of this cause great terror to the enemy and great loss and confusion.
