Reason is more than a tool

What troubled me was the ease with which a definition appropriate to machines sounded like a definition of intelligence as such. That slide from engineering model to human anthropology is easy to miss. We begin by saying: this is how machines work. Then, almost without noticing, we say: this is what intelligence is. And then, more dangerously still: perhaps this is what we are.

An old argument about human beings had entered the room without announcing itself. It sounded, instead, like a definition. The AI revolution has brought one of the Enlightenment’s deepest quarrels back to life – and made one side of it seem like plain common sense. In my view, the wrong side. Hence the alarm bell.
