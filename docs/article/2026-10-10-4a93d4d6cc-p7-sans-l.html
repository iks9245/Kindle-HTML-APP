Netanyahu welcomed the sanctions, saying the ICC was "not a court" but a political body that threatened law-abiding democracies and "the very idea of national sovereignty".

The ICC said that the sanctions were aimed at disrupting its work and intimidating those who co-operate with it - but that it "will not be deterred".

It called the measures "an assault on the rule of law" and said no one should be punished for serving the cause of justice, adding: "History will judge this moment."
