Other people

Whether you call the present a polycrisis or just a gigantic roiling clusterfuck, I became more convinced that the medicine for most of our ills is to actively and counterculturally move towards other people. To resist the powerful forces driving us apart. There are lots of ways of doing this, of course. But letting go of the idea that our homes must be private retreats from other people seemed a decent place to start. I believe that our homes can be places to relearn the habits of reciprocity, sharing and mutual care that for most of history (and, in many parts of the non-Western world, still) are the defining characteristics of a culture worthy of the name. A culture we are urgently going to need.
