The bombarding of childhood

What makes a show slow or fast for a given child? It depends. Children differ by age, temperament, language, sensory sensitivity and circumstance; thus, without testing their responses, it is impossible to provide a firm recommendation: a show that suits one four-year-old may overwhelm another. We therefore give ratings only to provide a map for adult judgment. The ratings are illustrations, inviting parents to check whether their child is being given enough time to complete an act of attention.
