- Reason in code, decide with Jeff. It's a classifier, not a planner. State what each option leads to ("this move gets you hit by a car"); asked to forecast ("a car arrives in 2 turns"), it does no better than random.

- Wording matters enormously. Describe options consistently: giving Frogger's goal option the same words as every other forward option took one episode from 15 crossings to 23.

- Use short option keys and descriptive text: {"1": "Engagement letter"} , not long IDs, which cost time and add

nothing.

- Ask independent questions together in one request.
