To give you a sense of the problem, if you have a black hole and it’s spinning not at all, you get one size of the ring related to its mass. If it’s spinning maximally, the fastest it can ever spin, you see the same ring, but it’s just about 4% smaller, okay? So spin is not measured well by looking at the shadow of the black hole that we’ve already seen.
