Paste the chords of a song from a chord site and hear how it goes before you sit down to play it.

Type a chord progression under My songs, loop it with the backing band, and record the idea before it's gone.

Show a class a chord shape or a strum on a big screen, slowed down, with every string and every pick stroke visible.

Singers, keyboard players and songwriters can hear how guitar chords are voiced and see how a progression lies on the fretboard, without learning to hold a guitar first.

Features

The most complete virtual guitar online: four instruments, every key, real chord shapes, songs, a backing band and recording, all free in one place.
