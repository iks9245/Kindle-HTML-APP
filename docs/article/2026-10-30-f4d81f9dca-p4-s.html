STROGATZ: I’m really looking forward to hearing it. I know you’ve always had a special affection for black holes. I’ve had a chance to interview you in some earlier version of this.

LEVIN: That’s right.

STROGATZ: And you blew my mind over and over about black holes.

LEVIN: Yeah, they require some defense. They’ve got a bad reputation, not entirely earned.

STROGATZ: What’s the bad reputation? Because they like to eat things?

LEVIN: Yeah. You know, black holes, kind of this monster truck language around black holes all the time. You know, they’re like… But actually, black holes are remarkably elegant, clean, simple systems. They leave you alone if you leave them alone. They’re actually quite small for their heft. That’s kind of their whole gestalt, that they’re actually tiny for their heft.
