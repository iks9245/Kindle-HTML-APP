The important thing is you also have to compute correlations between different spins, like are two spins the same? And then from those you can actually try and assess are these correlations, are these measurements consistent with a classical model? And by a classical model, what you really mean is an underlying system in which the thing you measure has a definite value, so the spin is either plus or minus, or the position is definitely this or definitely not that.
