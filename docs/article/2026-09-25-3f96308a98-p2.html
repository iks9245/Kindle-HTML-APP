But in his overnight ruling, Judge Timothy Kelly cast doubt on the claim about national security concerns.

The chaotic standoff limited some TV coverage as Trump welcomed Chinese President Xi Jinping to the White House ahead of a state dinner later.

Judge Kelly ruled the ban on the outlets should be temporarily lifted because it "likely violated their constitutional due process rights".

CNN, Politico and MS Now all reported being denied entry to the White House on Thursday morning, prompting their request for an emergency hearing.

In response, the director of White House Operations said in a court filing that the press passes and confiscated badges had been restored by 09:45.
