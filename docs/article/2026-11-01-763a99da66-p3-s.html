The oldest evacuee was 94 years old, while the youngest was one, according to Dmytro Lubinets, Ukraine's human rights ombudsman.

Zelensky said in a post on X that "all of them will receive the assistance they need. It is important that government agencies and all the regional and local authorities involved work effectively and help our people adapt."

Many people bear the scars: the convoy includes as many ambulances as coaches. The impact of years caught between the frontlines is written on the weary faces of many of Oleshky's residents, some hobbling on crutches, others needing wheelchairs.

They have a lot to come to terms with, not least having left the place they call home.

A humanitarian ceasefire took effect across the frontline region of Kherson on Tuesday, providing the first few days of peace in the area in four years, following Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine in February 2022. Before the war, Oleshky was home to 24,000 people.
