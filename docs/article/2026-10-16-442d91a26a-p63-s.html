And he studies that there are two genomes simultaneously inside a single network. So it’s, imagine our human body, but instead of having one genome, it has two genomes inside the same, what we would think of as an individual. It just doesn’t make any sense, and so he’s starting to understand, is there a cooperation between these two genomes? Who gets to colonize what parts of the network? Is an individual even a network, or is an individual just a genome, tiny little nuclei floating inside this tube? It just, it’s really hard to understand.

And then we don’t even know, do these genomes, do they recombine? For, for many years, the conventional idea is that these are asexual organisms, yet we see hints that maybe there’s a different kind of sex going on.
