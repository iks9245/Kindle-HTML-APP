Ashley told me that biological samples from Andrew were eventually sent for specialised testing. Whether those results can establish causation is a legal and forensic question, and I do not want to claim more than the evidence can support. But her account shows the public-health problem in human terms. Once a consumer product enters the marketplace without reliable safety evidence, accurate labelling and adequate testing capacity, the uncertainty does not end at the point of sale. It follows the consumer, the family, the medical examiner, the investigator, and anyone trying to reconstruct what happened after an adverse event leaves questions in its wake.
