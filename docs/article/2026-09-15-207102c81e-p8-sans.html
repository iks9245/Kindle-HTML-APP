小中大切換為襯線

The bombarding of childhood

That logic is, for the most part, the logic of the attention economy. It overwhelms and overshouts, making it easier for the screen to steer the viewer. The attention economy has introduced a host of small design features for shortening time. The Like button is faster than writing an appreciative comment. A progress bar tells us how many minutes remain in an article before we have decided whether to read to the end. Increasingly, AI appears beside long documents with an offer that sounds helpful but carries a philosophy: this text is long; would you like a quick summary?

The child is swept along by urgency rather than invited to dwell on responsibility or care for the other
