- They put liens on homes that then cannot be sold or refinanced Before any suit there is a claim of lien, a one-page document in the county's official records against the home. A lien does not have to be foreclosed to do damage: a title company will not close a sale over it, a lender will not refinance behind it, and clearing it means paying whatever the association's lawyer says is owed. In the recorder indexes HOA Spy collects (Broward County Official Records index, Miami-Dade County Official Records index), 460,412 claims of lien have been recorded by associations since 1978, and 277,047 lis pendens — 60% of the liens were followed by a foreclosure filing. 5,702 communities recorded a lien in the last five years; 1,047 liens on file were contested. 2025 saw 7,916 claims of lien, up 8% on 2020 (7,352); the peak was 2008 with 29,518. claims of lienlis pendens · 2026 is partial The communities with the most liens on record: BEACHPLACE TOWERS CONDOMINIUM ASSOCIATION, INC.
