DOELEMAN: I know. It’s in the most unimaginative, unoriginal way possible. So we’ve taken still images of black holes, and with any great discovery, they just raise the next set of questions. So the next natural thing to ask is, can we make a movie of the black hole and see not just light bending around, but the motion of matter as it orbits the black hole?

Because this will be the key to other even more sensitive tests of Einstein’s theory. But it will also unlock the astrophysics, the processes that allows these supermassive black holes to launch those jets that I was telling you about. So there’s every reason for us to keep going, and the key is adding more radio dishes around the globe.
