But that’s not the entire story. Nearly half of your genome is a wild drama: mobile, repetitive, disruptive, even viral. This half is the result of genetic material that can clip itself out of the DNA sequence, float off, and re-root somewhere else. These sequences can multiply and expand, inflating the genome from within. They can hop into the middle of another sequence and break it. They can also be fertile soil for new adaptations to grow.
