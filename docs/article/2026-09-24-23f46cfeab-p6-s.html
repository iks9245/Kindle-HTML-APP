He also reiterated that Iran would not give up its right to have a civilian nuclear programme and that it would not seek to develop nuclear weapons.

"We say it clearly: no nuclear weapons and no limitations on peaceful nuclear technology."

Trump argued in his speech that the war had prevented Iran from obtaining a nuclear bomb and demanded that its leaders "relinquish their nuclear ambitions".

On Tuesday, Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi and US envoy Steve Witkoff held indirect talks brokered by Qatari mediators on the sidelines of the UN General Assembly in New York.

Witkoff said he hoped the discussions – the first since June – would "prove constructive and promising", adding that the mediators would continue their work.

But Iran's foreign ministry spokesman Esmail Baqai said the talks were "not anything new" and that Araghchi had maintained Iran's conditions, including the end of the US blockade and the release of frozen Iranian assets.

延伸問答

伊朗與美國及以色列之間「近七個月的戰爭」是如何開始的？

這場衝突始於今年二月，美國和以色列對已故最高領袖哈梅內伊的住所發動聯合襲擊，導致其身亡。隨後，伊朗發動導彈和無人機襲擊報復以色列及美國在中東的基地，並關閉了霍爾木茲海峽，攻擊商船。

霍爾木茲海峽為何在這次衝突中如此重要，伊朗對其有何具體要求？

霍爾木茲海峽是全球五分之一石油和天然氣運輸的關鍵通道，其封鎖對全球經濟影響巨大。伊朗堅持其自由使用該海峽的權利，並希望對過往船隻徵收費用，這與美國拒絕伊朗控制該水道的立場相衝突。

儘管伊朗表示願意進行外交談判，但目前達成協議的主要障礙是什麼？

達成協議的主要障礙在於雙方的條件差異。伊朗要求美國結束對其港口和船隻的封鎖，並釋放被凍結的資產；而美國則要求伊朗放棄其核野心，並限制其核計畫。
