The two teams had very different communication styles. GPT almost immediately started using truncated forms of speech: “First leg complete x32 z9..24 y100.” Claude signed its name and said "we" (30% of messages, against GPT's 5%). GPT sent 5-30x more messages. Its most-messaged player got 198–368 DMs a game; Claude's got 11–48.

GPT agents came up with much more aggressive and elaborate strategies. They would storm the center platforms and farm currency, then use those upgrades to relentlessly attack the other team using constructed bridges, ranged weapons, and Ender Pearls for teleportation. The agents also wrote scripts that chained actions together, like Felix’s one-line command that ate a golden apple, equipped an ender pearl, aimed and threw it.
