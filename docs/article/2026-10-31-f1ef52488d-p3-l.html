A New Map of ‘Empty’ Space Challenges a Tranquil Picture of the Galaxy

Over the past few years, astrophysicists have transformed our understanding of the interstellar medium. Using newly developed maps stretching thousands of light-years across our galaxy, they’ve discovered that it’s not the quiet, steady backdrop that researchers thought. Instead, much of the ISM appears to be unstable — a finding that directly contradicts the leading theoretical models taught in textbooks. The surprise finding could help resolve a long-unanswered question about how quickly stars form.
