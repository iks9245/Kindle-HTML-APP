HALLIWELL: Versus superposition, but it goes even beyond that. Sometimes with a superposition, you can still find an underlying model which has definite states. It does come down to this notion of contextuality. Another way you can explain away indefinite states by saying when you make a bunch of measurements, if they depend on each other in ways that you hadn’t noticed, that’s what an indefinite state might look like.

Except that in quantum physics, we do these experiments in such a way that there isn’t a dependent on context. So two distant particles in an entangled state, you arrange it so they can’t possibly signal. It still looks like they’re talking to each other. I mean, that’s really the true mystery.
