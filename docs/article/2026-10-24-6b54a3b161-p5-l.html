Tribologists like me study this force in all its forms. Tribology is the science of interacting surfaces in relative motion, concerned with the wear of materials, friction and lubrication. Basically, tribology is the specialisation of things rubbing against each other. You may raise an eyebrow at a field of science focused on this, but when you pause to consider how much motion is going on inside us, around us and throughout the Universe, you can appreciate that tribology is vast. More than anyone, tribologists know the problems friction can cause. But they also know the achievements that have followed as we’ve come to understand resistance better.
