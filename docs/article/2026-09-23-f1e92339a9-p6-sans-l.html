Critically, no territory is ceded to the US, and it recognises both Denmark's "sovereignty and territorial integrity" and Greenland's right to self-determination.

On one hand, that's a significant climb down from Trump's earlier calls to acquire the Arctic island.

But it does deliver a strengthened US military presence and expand on the previous agreement. Therefore to some American eyes, that will be perceived as a historic win.

The new deal must still be approved by both Denmark's and Greenland's parliaments.
