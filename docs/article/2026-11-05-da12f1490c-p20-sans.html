In a spring 2026 paper, Ravfogel and his NYU colleagues Shashwat Singh and Tal Linzen raised an even broader philosophical concern with how language model researchers seek evidence of introspection. Intuitively, we think of introspection as a model’s ability to sense its internal states. But everything that happens inside a language model (after the very first step of reading an input) boils down to processing internal states. “If we say that this is introspection, the term ‘introspection’ becomes kind of meaningless,” Ravfogel said.
