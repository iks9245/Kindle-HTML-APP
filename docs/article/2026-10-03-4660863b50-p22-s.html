Life on a hair trigger

The first thing the men learned was to question their reading of a situation. In one CAD session, the group was given a scenario: somebody walks past and calls you a name. What do you do? For TY, 20, who had spent his adolescence surviving, the old answer would have arrived without thought. Instead, he worked through it. ‘I ain’t got no right to cause hurt to another person [if] this person ain’t do nothing to me,’ he said. ‘He probably having a bad day. He probably just got into it with his wife. You don’t know what’s wrong.’ Such exercises helped participants practise reframing a situation more charitably.
