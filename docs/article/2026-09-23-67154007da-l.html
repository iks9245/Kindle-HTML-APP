Trump threatens to 'annihilate' Iran in UN speech as officials meet on sidelines

- Published

President Donald Trump has told the United Nations General Assembly he faces a "big decision" on whether to strike Iran again or pursue a peace deal.

A single member of Iran's delegation watched on during the president's speech on Tuesday in New York, in which he said he could "annihilate" the Islamic Republic if the conflict went unresolved.
