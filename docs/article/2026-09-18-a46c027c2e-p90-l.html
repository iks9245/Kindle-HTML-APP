When I’ve given popular talks, I have the sense that the general public interested in physics, feel discomfort at the lack of understanding. That they want the physicist to deliver something mythologically meaningful to them in terms of the Big Bang, and they’re dissatisfied. For me, I found a sort of other perspective in a more, let’s say, spiritual side, where I’m just happier to sit with the mystery of multiple perspectives.
