Marseille braces for more unrest after protests give way to 'apocalyptic' disorder

18-year-old student Maryame only arrived in the southern city a few weeks ago, albeit from the countryside not too far away.

"It was very scary because it's my first time in a big city, and also my first time demonstrating," she said.

Maryame said violence engulfed the student demonstration her friends had joined as looters began attacking shops.

She said the solidarity of the earlier march was "beautiful" and the subsequent disorder "terrifying", but went on to say that she was conflicted as to whether even more violence would make the government sit up and listen.

"It is a very complicated question if violence is legitimate," she began.

"But the thing is, France, our history is really based on revolution, and sometimes we really have to make a really big impact.

"Sometimes it goes too far."
