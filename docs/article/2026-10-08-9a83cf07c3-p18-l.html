Most people who have seen a Western doctor have unknowingly benefited from systematic reviews

But Chalmers knew that synthesising all the evidence on effective treatments in medicine was such a gargantuan task that it would take more people power than this. So, in 1993, he and a group of like-minded folk started the Cochrane Collaboration (now known as just Cochrane), dedicated to producing high-quality systematic reviews of evidence on the effectiveness of health treatments.
