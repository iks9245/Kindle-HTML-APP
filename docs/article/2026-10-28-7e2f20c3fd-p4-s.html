小中大切換為無襯線

Love with nowhere to go

There was the woman whose calmness, clarity and silence embodied the qualities I have come to like so much about the Nordics since moving to Norway. Like a magical stranger, she entered my life unexpectedly, but at the same time her arrival felt so natural, her presence so obvious, that it was as if she had always been there. After our first meeting, which lasted almost 24 hours, I wrote in my journal: Ze is gekomen om te blijven. ‘She had come to stay.’ And she did, for nine intense months. She tried to teach me to ski. We listened to the music of Nils Frahm, which reminded her of melting snow. And she made me see that the deepest form of intimacy happens in the space between the words.
