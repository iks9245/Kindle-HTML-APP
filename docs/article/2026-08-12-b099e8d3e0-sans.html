This article is for students grades 5-8.

The Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope is NASA’s newest astrophysics observatory. The telescope will scan large sections of space. Roman will help astronomers answer questions about dark energy, dark matter, exoplanets, and more.

Words to Know

Astrophysics: a branch of space science that applies physics and mathematics to study the universe.
