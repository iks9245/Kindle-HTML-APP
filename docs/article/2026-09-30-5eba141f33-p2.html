The proposals - which have been presented in the form of two decrees - come into effect immediately, but must be approved by Congress within a month, although the government hopes to hold the vote as soon as this Friday.

Pedro Sánchez's leftist coalition government lacks a majority in parliament and has struggled to get right-leaning parties to approve previous decrees.

In April, a decree seeking to roll over rental contracts was blocked by right-leaning parties.

García, of the Sumar alliance, said other measures agreed upon on Tuesday included the rolling over of contracts until 2028; stricter regulation of short-term and room rentals; and banning the purchase of housing by investment firms.
