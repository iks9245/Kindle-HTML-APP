小中大切換為無襯線

Where a land ethic blooms

Is it fair to call any of this waste? The spark of life in the stinking barn pack is not yet exhausted, not yet lost to entropy. Roots burrow deep in search of more phosphorous, nitrogen and water to carry on the busy work of building ion transporters, enzymes, lignin and proteins – from root to stem to leaf to seed. Through spring, summer and fall, the straw, plant fibres and dead microbes that re-enter the field will take shifts in resurrection. Such is the life of the soil.
