At times, the trap snaps shut. I sense I’m merely living out the choices of the dead, unable to escape their restlessness. Visits to Italy only temporarily assuage the feelings of unsettledness, while leaving again stirs it up. I’m losing something I’ve never owned, just as I’ve been defined by a homeland I don’t inhabit, a past I cannot alter.
