Comet NEOWISE Rising Over the Adriatic Sea

Explanation: This sight was worth getting out of bed early. Just over four years ago, Comet C/2020 F3 (NEOWISE) rose before dawn to the delight of northern sky enthusiasts awake that early. Up before sunrise on July 8th, the featured photographer was able to capture in dramatic fashion one of the few comets visible to the unaided eye this century, an inner-Solar System intruder that has become known as the Great Comet of 2020. The resulting video detailed Comet NEOWISE from Italy rising over the Adriatic Sea. The featured time-lapse video combines over 240 images taken over 30 minutes. The comet was seen rising through a foreground of bright and undulating noctilucent clouds, and before a background of distant stars. Comet NEOWISE remained unexpectedly bright for over a month, with its ion and dust tails found to emanate from a nucleus spanning about five kilometers across.

延伸問答

彗星NEOWISE為何被稱為「2020年大彗星」？

彗星NEOWISE因其在2020年7月出乎意料的亮度，成為北半球肉眼可見的壯觀天體，並持續數週，這在本世紀實屬罕見。它的高亮度和長時間的可見性使其在當時的夜空中脫穎而出，因此獲得了「大彗星」的稱號。

摘要中提到的「夜光雲」是什麼，與觀測彗星NEOWISE有何關係？

夜光雲是一種在極高層大氣中形成的冰晶雲，通常在夏季的黃昏或黎明時分可見，當太陽在地平線下時，它們仍能被陽光照亮而發光。在拍攝彗星NEOWISE的影像中，夜光雲為彗星升起時提供了引人注目的前景，增添了畫面的戲劇性和美感。

彗星NEOWISE何時會再次拜訪內太陽系，或者它有什麼樣的軌道？

彗星NEOWISE是一顆長週期彗星，根據計算其軌道週期約為6800年。這意味著它在內太陽系可見後將需要數千年才能再次返回，因此對於現代人類來說，2020年的觀測是一生中僅有一次的機會。
