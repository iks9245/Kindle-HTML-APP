But yes, I think what it teaches us is just that these organisms are incredibly sensitive to their environment, and they have evolved all kinds of strategies to maximize this resource exchange. There’s even plants that don’t photosynthesize at all. This is incredible.

There’s types of orchids that make no photosynthetic body. They still bloom above ground and make a flower, but they actually tap into the mycorrhizal network, and they steal the carbon. So it’s like a reverse. So they’re cheating the fungi and pulling the carbon out so that they don’t have to waste time photosynthesizing.
