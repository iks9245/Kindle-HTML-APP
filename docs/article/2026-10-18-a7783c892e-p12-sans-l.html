I made another run at the process in 2018. Again it did not take.

I spent a hell of a long time living in Japan without ever applying for permanent residency. PR never felt like the way for me, because I always assumed I would be applying for naturalization at any moment. Every time I renewed my spouse visa, the people processing the documentation would remark on how unusual it was.
