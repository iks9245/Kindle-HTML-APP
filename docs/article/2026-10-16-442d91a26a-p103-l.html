And then we don’t even know, do these genomes, do they recombine? For, for many years, the conventional idea is that these are asexual organisms, yet we see hints that maybe there’s a different kind of sex going on.

So lots of open questions about how these fungi just reproduce. We know they make spores. We know these spores go into the air. We know that the spores germinate and make what you would think is a new individual. But because there’s hundreds of nuclei inside a spore, and they’re not all the same, it just doesn’t make much sense using our conventional understanding of genetics.
