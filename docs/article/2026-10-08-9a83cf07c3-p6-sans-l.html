One of the biggest events in the history of evidence synthesis occurred in a hotel in San Francisco in 1976. There, at the annual meeting of the American Educational Research Association, the statistician Gene Glass revealed an ingenious technique that showed how to integrate findings from a large volume of individual studies. This technique would become so important to science that it has its own biography.
