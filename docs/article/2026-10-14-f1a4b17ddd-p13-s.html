Deskilling the body

The physical organisation of an organism is inherently evaluative. It is from this embodied evaluation that agency emerges. Yet the same unique qualities that make life-forms so causally special and intelligent can undermine us when repeatedly trained by harmful environments. Instead, we can acquire patterns or lose pathways that progressively weaken our intrinsic intelligence, which we need to flourish. For example, one study found that only a single week’s worth of leg immobilisation can reduce thigh-muscle volume by about 5 per cent. A separate study found that, by 15 days of non-use, the firing behaviour of the cells controlling the leg muscle begins to diminish.
