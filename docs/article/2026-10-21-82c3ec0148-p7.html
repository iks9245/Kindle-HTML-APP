France's Interior Minister Laurent Nuñez - whose resignation in the wake of the riots was rejected by President Macron - travelled to Marseille on Monday evening.

He came to the City Hall, which was briefly occupied by protestors on Saturday, and met Marseille's Socialist Mayor Benoît Payan.

Payan said he wanted answers from the government as to how the chaos was allowed to happen.
