Other people

Living with others has shown me things in myself that I would prefer to ignore. How much of me is still a child, wanting my way, quick to cry ‘it’s not fair’. I have been learning all this as the world around us unravels. The contrast is disconcerting. If it really is the end of the world, at least as we have known it, then what kind of people will be needed? What would it take to be braver, more resilient and flexible, more joyful, creative and free? A person who could, at bloody least, wait patiently to go in the bathroom, or let someone else use a favourite mug. Someone able to sacrifice sleep to care for another’s child, and have the hard conversation about a failure to mop the kitchen floor, rather than avoiding it. I want to learn these habits. I have a hunch that much more than this might be asked of me. Of all of us.
