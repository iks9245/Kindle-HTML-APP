Where a land ethic blooms

Among the trade-offs that come with taking personal responsibility for investment in infrastructure and community is that much of the pay-off will come in the long term rather than in the next quarter or next few years. Building a long-term outlook and sustaining collective commitment will require cultivating the skills needed to navigate disagreements between individuals and factions, to face the uncertainty of alternative models of community development, to weather economic hard times, and to engage in both practical and joyful scepticism of the intense propaganda that flows from the anonymous economy to its victims. This demands vision, sincerity and the arts of neighbourliness and community engagement.
