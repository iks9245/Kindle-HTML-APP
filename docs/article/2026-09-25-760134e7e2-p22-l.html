Where a land ethic blooms

The land ethic remains relevant in part because Leopold recognised his effort as a synthesis, as an articulation of the best of current understanding of people, land and the connections between them, rather than as the final word.

We still have far to go in the attempt to encode a land ethic sufficiently strong to temper our appetite for Earth’s resources and focus our work towards restoring land health. A land ethic, good land use, and an equitable and civil society arise from countless actions and conversations, constantly unfolding, as the nation’s citizens go about their daily lives.
