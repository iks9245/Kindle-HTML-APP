小中大切換為無襯線

Deskilling the body

The expansive thoughts and sensations that come as I step outside are enabled by the physical changes that accompany my movement. My heart rate has increased, sweeping nutrients and oxygen through my tissues and into my brain. My muscles begin contracting, clearing sugars and fats from my bloodstream, immediately working to counteract some of the effects of sitting for so long. Each step is a bodily integration of balance, senses and muscular force, allowing me to predict what will happen next, and the more I do this, the more I can calibrate my actions in the environment. The thoughts that come from this physical enactment are hopeful and generous, and often unexpected. Yet this shouldn’t surprise. When we move in the world, we draw on physical capabilities that are relatively underused when we remain indoors and seated. From balance to sensing what is happening inside us, our bodies are continuously perceiving and, crucially, remembering in the flesh. Our thoughts can emerge as much from bodily intelligence and our awareness of the wider world as from abstract symbols on a screen.
