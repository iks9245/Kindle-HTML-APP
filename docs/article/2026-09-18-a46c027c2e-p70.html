So I think at the end of the day, I believe in mystery. Sitting with the mystery and the uncertainty, and that, that is the most comfortable position. In fact, one of my meditation teachers said, I think, quoting from thousands of years, that a lot of these things are about the infinite one way or another, the beginning of the universe.

And instead of worrying about “What it is”, you can ask, “Well, who am I in relation to that?” That’s the simple question, I think, which is there in a lot of spiritual traditions. So that’s where I am with physics. You know, how do I feel in relation to all this uncertainty and immensity?
