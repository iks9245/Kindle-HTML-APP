Computational complexity has many open questions and conjectures, whose proofs or refutations have so far eluded the best efforts of scientists. We hope that humans with AI can provide new understanding in this field, and shed light on the inherent capabilities and limits of efficient computation.

The physics collaboration will understand and explain a result that gives a mathematically rigorous existence proof of certain interacting quantum field theories containing gauge fields and fermions in four dimensions, including Quantum Chromodynamics (QCD) and a unified completion of the Standard Model of particle physics. Our work builds on Balaban’s approach(opens in a new window) in constructive quantum field theory. As a subproblem, this work also includes a proof of the existence of pure Yang–Mills theory, which is part of the Quantum Yang–Mills Theory Clay Mathematics Institute Millennium Prize problem(opens in a new window).
