Newborn twins fight for survival as conflict fuels Yemen's hunger crisis

Malnutrition in Yemen is a crisis within a crisis - another frontline in a devastating civil war. Hunger did not arrive with conflict, but it's spreading with the latest bloodshed.

The United Nations says around 800,000 people can no longer receive lifesaving aid and more than 250,000 have been displaced, after an informal ceasefire collapsed in the summer.

Since then the Houthis have made a lighting advance along the Red Sea coast, and they have now completely cut off Yemen's third largest city, Taiz.

The UN says civilians there are "at grave risk" and lacking food due to soaring prices.

The internationally-recognised government is fighting back against the Houthis - or trying to - with the help of Saudi air strikes.

Caught in the middle of all this are fathers like Bajash, fighting to get help for their children. We saw another mother with a malnourished baby being turned away.
