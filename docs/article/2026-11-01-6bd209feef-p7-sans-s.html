- Ubuntu: the tracker lists the linux package as "Vulnerable" on every release. On 26.10, 26.04, 24.04, and 22.04 it adds "work in progress" in 7.3.0-10.10, 7.0.0-43.43, 6.8.0-149.149, and 5.15.0-203.213; 20.04, 18.04, and 16.04 show only "Vulnerable." Many cloud and hardware-enablement kernels are listed the same way, and no Ubuntu Security Notice has been published.

- Red Hat: rated Critical, CVSS 3.1 9.8, since October 30. RHEL 8, 9, and 10 and OpenShift Container Platform 4 and 5 are listed as affected, with no fix; RHEL 6 and 7 aren't affected (CVE page). Its bulletin, RHSB-2026-016, says "Red Hat is expediting the release of fixes," gives an nftables mitigation (compared below), and says any product that relies on the RHEL kernel, including RHEL CoreOS, "is also potentially impacted."
