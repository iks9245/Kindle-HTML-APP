International governance could, Ward argued, provide the best means for overcoming environmental degradation and economic inequality. Yet, at the conference, she discovered that not all members of the UN wanted to limit national sovereignty. Her plans to expand international cooperation to the non-violent settlement of disputes, progressive farming, urban planning, pollution control and global economic redistribution remained on paper. While largely unrealised, the declaration that the conference’s delegates produced remained an important signpost on the road towards global environmental governance.

She later downplayed her earlier emphasis on Christianity as the moral foundation for a planetary community
