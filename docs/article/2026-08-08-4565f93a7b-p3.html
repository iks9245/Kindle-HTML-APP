“The facility helps develop and test technologies needed for long-duration lunar exploration,” said Mike Salinas, Propulsion and Power Division branch deputy chief. “Engineers are advancing techniques to extract resources from lunar regolith, which can be turned into oxygen for astronauts and liquid oxygen for rocket propellant.”

The spirit of exploration extends beyond the facility’s walls. Its exterior features a large-scale mural depicting astronauts exploring the lunar surface beneath a view of the cosmos. Completed in 2024 by artist Sebastian Boileau, the artwork celebrates the innovation, ingenuity, and discovery happening inside the building every day.

Now, anyone can step inside the facility from anywhere. Explore NASA’s new 3D virtual tour of the Lunar Development and Test Facility to see where engineers are helping prepare the technologies that support this Golden Age of exploration and innovation.

延伸問答

月球塵埃與地球沙子的主要區別是什麼？為什麼月球塵埃對太空任務構成如此大的挑戰？

月球塵埃與地球沙子不同，它尖銳、具磨蝕性且容易附著在各種物體上。這種獨特的性質可能損壞太空裝備和太空衣，並對太空人的健康構成風險，因此對月球任務來說是個巨大挑戰。

月球開發和測試設施除了應對月塵，還在月球探索的哪些方面發揮作用？

除了減緩月塵影響，該設施還致力於開發和測試長期月球探索所需技術。例如，工程師正在研究如何從月球風化層中提取資源，這些資源可以轉化為太空人所需的氧氣和火箭推進劑所需的液氧。

該設施如何模擬月球環境來測試硬體？

該設施利用真空室，並在其中填充月球風化層模擬物，以重現嚴酷的月球條件。它採用閉環氮氣系統來維持這種模擬環境，使工程師能夠評估太空衣和太空船部件在實際任務中的表現。
