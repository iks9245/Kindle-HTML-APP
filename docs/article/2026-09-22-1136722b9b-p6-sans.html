The transposons McClintock discovered are DNA transposons, so called because they travel as a DNA molecule. Transposons in this class jump by means of “cut and paste.” Enzymes called transposases bind to the ends of the DNA transposon and splice it free. The liberated bundle then touches down somewhere else in the genome, where native DNA repair processes paste it in.
