Our thoughts emerge as much from bodily intelligence and awareness of the world as from symbols on a screen

This is something I feel increasingly keenly. Sometimes, when I know I’ve spent yet another day indoors at my desk, I get myself outside and walk the road uphill to where I can look at the view across the dale. It is often hard to release my mind. It feels as if, from concentrating all day and neglecting the rest of my body, I have become a clenched fist somewhere inside my skull. Yet once the light and the breeze and the sounds of birds reach me, the grip eases, a sensation of pleasure passes over me, and ideas begin to free up too.
