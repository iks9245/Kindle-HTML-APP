Around that time, I reached out to my advisor for help asking Habermas for a foreword to a collection I was editing on Habermas and democracy. He was known for guarding his time closely – very few people had his email address, and he accepted only a handful of the hundreds of such requests he received each year. A couple of months later, an email came from an address I didn’t recognise: he had accepted, and written to say so himself. Amid the logistics, he added one condition: ‘You should send me at least a substantive part of the book before I can and would sit down and settle a few ideas.’ At 92, he wanted the material in hand before he’d commit to anything.
