However, the US has provided a six month grace period before some of these latest sanctions kick in, meaning member states can keep paying for its operations - a move Washington says is to allow countries time to negotiate a "solution to address" US concerns.

UN Secretary General Antonio Guterres called the US decision "a serious blow to our collective pursuit of international criminal justice, of which the ICC is a key pillar".

The UK joined Canada, Denmark, Germany, France, Italy, Japan and the Netherlands in reaffirming their "continued and unwavering support" for the ICC and said they "regret and strongly disagree with" the sanctions.
