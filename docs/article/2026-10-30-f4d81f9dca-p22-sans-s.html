LEVIN: So let’s distinguish death states of stars, which are the kinds of objects that were creating signals like Cygnus X-1. Two star systems born and died together. One becomes a black hole, the other starts to descend. It gets cannibalized. It creates these X-rays, these jets. These supermassive black holes, at the time we were calling them quasi-stellar objects or quasars, right? It was not understood where these supermassive black holes came from or if they were so prevalent, if they had permeated the entire universe. I mean, it was a surprise, wasn’t it, that those quasars were commonplace, that they probably exist in every single galaxy.
