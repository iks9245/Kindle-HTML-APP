Terrel reached the same realisation by naming the trauma he had long carried. ‘I never got through my first trauma,’ he said of his sister’s death; in the years since, he had lost a brother, a cousin, a nephew and friend after friend. ‘That shit just fucked me up to this day. I’m suffering with PTSD bad.’ Understanding his behaviour as a response to those losses, rather than as proof of who he was, made change imaginable. As Terrel said: ‘If you don’t deal with it… you can bring that baggage into your adult life.’ Naming the ways in which his past spilled into the present through his PTSD allowed Terrel to challenge his own narrative about himself and begin to heal.
