Rogers welcomed Stevens' endorsement and said it showed "courage" during brief remarks with reporters.

President Donald Trump echoed that sentiment during a campaign rally in Iowa, calling her "very brave".

"I don't know Haley. I've never met Haley. But Haley said what few people were willing to say," Trump said. "I'd love her to join the Republican Party actually."

Many of Stevens' staff reportedly resigned immediately after her announcement, while the chair of the Democratic Party in Michigan said her decision "shows that she has abandoned everything she claims to care about".

"We reject the attempts by Stevens and bad-faith actors to divide our party and undermine our momentum," Curtis Hertel said in a statement.

Top Senate Democrat Chuck Schumer called Stevens' endorsement of Rogers "a despicable betrayal".

In downtown Detroit, several Democrats the BBC spoke to said they were disappointed by Stevens' endorsement of Rogers.
