Bloodied, dazed and angry - how passengers' rail journey turned to disaster in Spain

Rafael Barcelo was in one of the worst-affected carriages. A bruise on his forehead makes him one of the lucky survivors. He was having a coffee at the time of the crash and "felt a huge impact".

"We were all thrown into the air," he told Reuters news agency at the site of the crash.

"I am reasonably OK, my friend is quite worse."

"I was travelling in coach seven, which is the one with more victims and the one most destroyed. I at least counted eight victims in that coach," he said, visibly shocked.
