If AI takes our ability to contribute, what’s left for our children?

Neil Postman offers a few suggestions for positive purposes for education that I found heartening, not mainly for their content, but for the fact that it’s possible to make up new purposes for education and advocate for them. My favorite suggestions of Postman’s are:

- Spaceship Earth – we’re all in this together; let’s take care of each other and our planet

- The American Experiment – let’s revive our sense of wonder, curiosity, and responsibility about what America is and can be
