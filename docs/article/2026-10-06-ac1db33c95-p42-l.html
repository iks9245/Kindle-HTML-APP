This is a common conception of autobiography as a genre. The French autobiography scholar Philippe Lejeune holds that it is a retrospective prose narrative; within identity research, life stories are regarded as a fundamental form of self-understanding – the view being that we all live in and through stories about ourselves, and that a good life means shaping a unified life story. Narrativity is seen as a moral ideal: the good, examined life is, by definition, a narrated life.

Each episode must be readable on its own; the reader need not know everything that has preceded it
