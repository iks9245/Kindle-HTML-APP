A spokesperson for the justice department said in a statement that it disagreed with the decision and "will appeal in due course".

In his ruling, Judge Furman said Comey's dismissal violated the US Constitution as well as her right to a fair legal process.

He said the US attorney general can fire federal prosecutors but only "in a particular manner and only for certain reasons".

Furman noted that in Comey's role, "she was assigned some of the country's highest profile cases and consistently received the highest accolades from supervisors and peers alike".
