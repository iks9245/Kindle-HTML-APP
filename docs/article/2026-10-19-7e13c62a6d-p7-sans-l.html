- They raise the cost of staying: dues, special assessments and the condo-safety bill Since the Surfside collapse, Senate Bill 4-D (2022) and the laws that followed it require older condominium buildings to pass a milestone structural inspection (section 553.899, Florida Statutes) and to fund structural reserves on the strength of a structural integrity reserve study (section 718.112(2)(g), Florida Statutes), reserves the owners can no longer vote to waive. The repairs and the reserves are paid by the owners, through higher dues and special assessments, and an owner who cannot pay is back at step one above. The cost is visible in the dues. Redfin reported in August 2024 that median monthly HOA fees in the three months to July 2024, against a year earlier, rose Tampa 17.2% to $614 a month, Orlando 16.7% to $467 a month, Fort Lauderdale 16.2% to $611 a month, against 5.7% across the metros it tracked (Redfin). Buyers price that in: Mortgage Professional America reported in January 2026, citing housing analyst Nick Gerli, that condo values in Florida fell 9.9% over the preceding twelve months (source). A home that costs more each month to keep and less to sell is the crisis in one sentence. No public index records what an association charges, so HOA Spy cannot count the assessments. What it can count is what happens when they go unpaid — the liens and foreclosure suits above.
