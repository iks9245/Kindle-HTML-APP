MILLER: Yeah. I like to tell students there are three really important classes that students take, which are real analysis, algebraic structures, and topology. Real analysis is essentially a continuation of calculus, and most students who are taking that class probably have a lot of intuition from their experience in calculus, and probably their intuition is correct, and they’re learning how to formalize it. And when you learn about algebraic structures, you probably don’t have very much intuition at all because it’s very abstract.
