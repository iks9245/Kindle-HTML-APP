- They litigate, and every owner pays for it Every suit an association files or defends is paid for out of the dues — and the cost of a suit an owner wins is still passed back to the owners. 48,999 court records name a Florida association, from 13,601 communities. The association is the plaintiff in 29,947 of them and the defendant in 14,113; when it is sued, the most common suit is Appellate (4,380). 92 communities have fifty or more cases each — litigation as a matter of routine. The ten busiest communities account for 2% of all the records. Among the 24,664 closed cases with both dates on the docket, half took more than 223 days.
