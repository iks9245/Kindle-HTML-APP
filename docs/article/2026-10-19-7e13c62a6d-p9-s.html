Florida against the nation

Florida associations bring more foreclosure suits against their members than those of any other state on HOA Spy. 10,296 of the 14,168 on file (73%); Texas is next with 2,349.

- One in three HOA lawsuits in the country is a Florida caseFlorida holds 48,999 of the 147,465 court records naming a homeowner or condominium association on HOA Spy (33%). Texas is next with 41,271, and no other state reaches 15,879.

- Florida associations foreclose on their own members more than every other state combined12,006 Florida court records are foreclosure cases — the association foreclosing its lien on a home, or named in a lender's foreclosure — 55% of the 21,898 nationwide. Texas is next with 5,084.
