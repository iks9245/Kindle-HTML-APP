"This, my fourth term, will be the best of any president in the history of this country," the former metal worker told supporters. "It will be the one in which I listen most to the Brazilian people."

After pulling off this victory, Lula faces big hurdles: a divided country, an opponent that won't recognise the result, and a hostile Congress packed full of Bolsonaro allies.

Markets reacted negatively to the unexpected outcome. The Sao Paolo stock exchange opened 15% down, sparking a brief suspension before recovering slightly. Interest rate futures rose as investors scrutinised Lula's fiscal plans.
