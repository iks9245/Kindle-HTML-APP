How Do You Take a Picture of a Black Hole?

LEVIN: Did you think at that moment, “Well, this is what I’m gonna do for the rest of my life”? Or is it that the memory comes back and we reconstruct the story, you know?

DOELEMAN: That’s a great question. I think it mostly, it just comes back. Life isn’t linear like that. But I will say the idea that there would be these huge objects that were moving and they could align perfectly and allow you to see what would normally be hidden, like the corona of the sun, was startling to me. I mean, it didn’t have to be that way. Like, the angular diameter of the moon doesn’t have to be the same as the sun so they totally block each other out. But these coincidences sometimes give you a special view into nature.
