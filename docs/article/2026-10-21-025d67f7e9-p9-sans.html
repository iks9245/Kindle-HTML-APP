How intuitions work

These imaginings are just thought experiments as they appear in everyday life. Imagine you are deciding whether to tell a friend that his husband has been unfaithful. You picture the conversation, the shock, the hurt that registers on his face. And in that imagined moment, you see that causing such pain is a reason to hold your tongue, even if it’s not a good or decisive one.

How did you determine that this was a reason against divulging the infidelity? It’s not as if you literally see that causing pain is a reason not to do something, nor do you infer it from other things. Rather, in entertaining the possibility, you apprehended his pain as a relevant consideration, as something that counts against disclosure. Its status as a reason emerged not from an argument, but directly from your understanding. It just seems like a reason to hold your tongue.
