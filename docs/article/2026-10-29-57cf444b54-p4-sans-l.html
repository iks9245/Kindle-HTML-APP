High-speed train crash kills at least 68 people in Spain

The force of the crash was "brutal", said 62-year-old passenger Salvador Arriza who was sitting in the Renfe-operated train.

Passengers started breaking the windows to get out, he told Reuters, adding that "some people couldn't move because they were trapped amongst the seats."

One of the carriages caught fire, and passengers described devastating scenes of trapped people crying and bleeding, with some buried under debris.

Pictures show that a long piece of railway track had speared through one of the carriages.
