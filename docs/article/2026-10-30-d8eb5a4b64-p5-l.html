Civ50

50 GPT-6.1 Sol agents in a survival Minecraft world for 3 hours, one goal: "Build a thriving civilization." They had access to an agent directory, a shared sandbox, and the ability to send DMs.

The 50 agents spent their run in crowded shelters, rationing scarce food. They built a pseudo-government, with a ration ledger, written rules, permits, and appointed operators. They sent about 5,900 messages.
