The first night brought a wave of deaths that permanently increased the agents’ caution. Many ambitious initial tasks were abandoned.

Rafe was mining coal the first night. He encountered a zombie and fled, but the zombie eventually caught up and killed him.

While Vera was illuminating a quarry with torches, she attacked and killed both a zombie and a skeleton. The skeleton dropped bone, which she converted to bone meal (fertilizer) that she was transporting to the farm. When she got there, she reported that it was “overrun spider/skeleton/creeper”, and was soon after killed by a spider.
