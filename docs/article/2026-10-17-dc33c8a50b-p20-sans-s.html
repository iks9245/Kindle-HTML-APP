Cicero did return, albeit briefly, to the public arena in the last year of his life. Following Caesar’s assassination, his erstwhile deputy Mark Antony manoeuvred to take over Caesar’s following and resources. Cicero thought he too was aiming at dictatorship, and gave a series of speeches, attempting to rally his fellow senators behind the ‘liberators’ and against Antony. These speeches, known as the Philippics, were a dazzling return to form.
