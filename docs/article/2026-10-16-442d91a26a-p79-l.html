I think that’s what’s interesting, is that we have an ability to watch the carbon come up to what we call a decision point, where there’s a fork in the road, and it can either go left or it can go right, and we can watch hundreds and thousands of these decision points and try to, again, use math to understand how the carbon is being moved across the network in a very efficient and effective way. One of the most beautiful videos that we captured live was that there was this sort of blob of carbon coming up to a junction, and it moved all the way to the left. And then it was about halfway up into the next decision point, and it backed down to the decision point and then split almost perfectly in half and went both to the left and the right.
