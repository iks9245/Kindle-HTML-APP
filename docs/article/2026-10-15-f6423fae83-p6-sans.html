Triplet of ‘Ghostly’ Galaxies Hints at What Dark Matter Is — and Isn’t

“You wouldn’t get galaxies without dark matter,” said Chamkaur Ghag, a physicist at University College London. “Is there a way of evolving the galaxies without dark matter in the first place? Our understanding says no.”

So when DF2 was discovered in 2018, astronomers didn’t quite know what to make of it. The galaxy is roughly the same size as the Milky Way, but with 200 times fewer stars, it’s considerably dimmer. Suspicious, scientists used the Keck Observatory in Hawai‘i to track the motions of those stars to estimate its total mass. They discovered that, even for such a star-deficient galaxy, it was lightweight: It seemed to contain, at most, 400 times less dark matter than they would expect, making DF2 the first dark matter–deficient galaxy ever spotted.
