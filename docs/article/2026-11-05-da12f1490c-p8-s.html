A 20% success rate may not sound so impressive. But crucially, models in the control group never reported receiving an injection. The complete absence of such false positives showed that the successful trials weren’t just lucky guesses. To Lindsey, it looked like strong evidence that models could introspect, albeit unreliably.

Or was there another way to explain the results?

Thought Experiments

Other researchers soon began to investigate that question. Lindsey’s findings “seemed super compelling,” said Kyle Mahowald, a computational linguist at the University of Texas, Austin. But Mahowald was also well aware that striking behavior in language models often turned out to have a simpler explanation. “If that’s somehow less real than it seems, that’s super interesting to understand,” he said.
