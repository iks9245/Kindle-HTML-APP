Barbara Ward’s vision

Ward accused Western societies of the ‘under-development’ of colonial societies but still considered the West superior to the rest of the world. She assumed that postcolonial countries wanted to follow Western industrialisation models (or in her terms, ‘to modernise’) but could not do so alone without Western guidance. Her proposals for the development of postcolonial societies expressed a paternalistic and hierarchical worldview. Moreover, by promoting a new world order based on ‘social justice, humane concern and Christian compassion’, Ward suggested that equality did not depend on material conditions alone but also on a spiritual transformation. In her political vision, Catholic universalism provided the foundational moral values for the realisation of a just political and economic order on a planetary scale.
