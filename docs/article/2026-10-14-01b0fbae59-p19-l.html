Getting started

From silence to a song you lead yourself. The first two steps take a few seconds.

Pick Acoustic, Classic, Electric or Bass at the top of the page. On a phone, tap the guitar picture. You can switch at any time.

Tap a chord pad, or press its letter on the keyboard. The default key is G major, so the middle row, A S D F G, holds G, C, D, Em and Am. Auto strum starts on, so the chord strums itself.
