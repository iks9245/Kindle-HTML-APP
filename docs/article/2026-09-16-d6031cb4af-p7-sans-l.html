小中大切換為襯線

Chasing la dolce vita

A cousin recently emailed me photographs of my great-grandfather Marco, the first to set the family’s migrations in motion. I was surprised to see that he was as tall as the family legend of the eagle would have it, towering over others, but also that he was standing in front of what was the Italian Hospital in Queen Square, Bloomsbury, the place he’d apparently helped to run. This was the very spot in London I’d sat on many occasions when, as a student at King’s College, I’d search for quiet garden squares in which to read and revise for exams. I’d always believed I was drawn to the Italian Hospital because of its name. But I remember how, more than once, I’d had to get up and leave, overwhelmed by an inexplicable sadness. In another photograph, Marco was posing with my great-grandmother Delfina, also from Emilia-Romagna, on a patch of grass in Hyde Park. I recognised it immediately as the hidden slope leading down to the Serpentine lake where I often go.
