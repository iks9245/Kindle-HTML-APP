At the heart of this circling around identity lies a thorny and painful question. Why do so many Italians continue to leave, and stay away? Between 2023 and 2024, 270,000 Italians emigrated, a figure considerably higher than in the previous two years. And behind the statistics lurks another grave concern: Italy’s population has been declining and is set to lose about 7 million people of working age by 2050. In 2025, there were only 355,000 births – the lowest number since the unification of Italy. In this context, the question of impossible returns is all the more troubling.
