For decades, Sarnak has argued that mathematicians should approach the Riemann hypothesis by considering the zeta function as just one member of a broader family of functions, and then proving statements about that family. “But it was not clear exactly how to do so, or which family to consider,” said Michael Rubinstein of the University of Waterloo.

As it turns out, Sarnak had the right intuition. But the family that OpenAI’s model identified had previously been overlooked. The Rutgers University mathematician Henryk Iwaniec narrowly missed it in 2002, when he identified a number of closely related families in an effort to prove the Riemann hypothesis.
