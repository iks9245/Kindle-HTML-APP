Life on a hair trigger

Of course, the milieu that trained these frames and scripts has not entirely disappeared for most. Recall that young men in these neighbourhoods faced a greater risk of being killed than a soldier deployed to Iraq or Afghanistan. A soldier, though, eventually returns home; these men have no such separation. What they must learn is more complex: how to hold on to vigilance without letting it dictate their reactions in the wrong place or at the wrong moment. The problem is not the impulse to fight back itself, but being unable to distinguish situations that call for it from those that don’t.
