At work, I have contributions to give, and I battle between wanting to do them to prove how capable I am, and to serve my coworkers in making it a better place for them. It’s an interesting paradox: you can’t really accomplish anything without being ambitious and cocky (I can do this), but the joy is lost when the achievement becomes your #1 goal.

Maybe winning isn’t everything, or the only thing, Vince Lombardi.

It’s like in the film Walter Mitty, when the character played by Sean Penn declines to take a photo of a faraway snow leopard (or some other big cat) — and instead simply relishes the moment.

And I may be completely off base, because I am not a mathematician whose livelihood is on the rocks.

But there’s something profound to be found here.

— Jake Toronto
