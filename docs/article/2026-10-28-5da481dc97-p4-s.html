Relations between the US and Ukraine and its European allies have soured since US President Donald Trump agreed to lift some sanctions from Russia, allowing Moscow to export oil to the America.

As the Trump administration's position on Russia has softened, several European leaders have said Europe should be trying to re-establish direct contacts with the Kremlin.

Stubb and Støre are the first European leaders to contact Putin since French President Emmanuel Macron's conversation with him in July 2025.
