He is now under pressure to respond to the students' demands. What has been offered, including a website for students to express their grievances and an afternoon of internal discussion in schools on Friday, has made little impression so far.

There seems little chance of the protests coming to an end just yet. France's biggest union, the CGT, has said that further protests involving school and university students are being planned for Thursday.

There is widespread concern over allegations of "disproportionate force" by police, and France's IGPN police inspectorate has been investigating a number of cases.

One police officer who fired a tear-gas grenade at a high school student at Saint-Ouen-l'Aumône, north-west of Paris, last week has now been charged by prosecutors with "violence... resulting in mutilation or permanent disability".
