小中大切換為無襯線

Where a land ethic blooms

We spent an hour flagging the buffer, and the heat of the day was lapsing as we reached the property line. To get back to the truck, we followed a strip of weedy foxtail grasses that waved yellow seed heads in a rolling sea of hay. We were moving due west, as the foxtail marked precisely where the soil had been disturbed when David’s brother bulldozed the old fenceline a few years earlier. Halfway back to the truck, I was surprised when David stopped at a short, west-facing slope, where the field dropped about 10 feet over the course of 30-40 feet.
