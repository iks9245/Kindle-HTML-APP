It also feels like a developer-focused and under-construction system: many of the included apps are technology demos (though who doesn’t love a Utah teapot), while a contemporary Be homepage links directly to the developer release notes and discusses CodeWarrior releases and access to the bug tracker. The unfinished nature extends to third-party software too – I stumbled across some BeOS software on this Amiga magazine CD, and most of it had a “version 0.1” feel, either literally or figuratively. To be fair, I did find some more fleshed-out software, for example, Gobe Productive from the former ClarisWorks team. This and other software is on Infinite HD if you’d like to try it out (preferably in later OS versions).
