He announced that France would call a meeting of the G7 to discuss energy and raise the question of gas stocks with the European Commission.

"In the coming weeks, we will have a G7 meeting devoted to these energy issues, both to strengthen cooperation and to avoid unnecessary tensions between the G7 countries and our main partners," Macron said.

"It will also be an opportunity to consider options for a possible release or unlocking of strategic reserves."

In a domestic context, he called on candidates in next year's elections not to exploit public unease by blaming the government, but to acknowledge that fuel price inflation was the result of uncontrollable international circumstances.

The hard-right opposition National Rally candidate Marine le Pen is currently leading the polls for the April-May vote to replace Macron.
