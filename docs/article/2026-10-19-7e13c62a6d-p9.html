- Some have stopped functioning as corporations at all An association is a corporation, and a corporation that is dissolved, delinquent or not in good standing with the state cannot reliably insure the property, keep its reserves, sign a contract or answer a lender's questionnaire — which is enough to stall every sale in the community. Of the 104,659 Florida associations checked against the state's corporate registry, 3,753 have no active corporation on file and 2,908 are delinquent in their registration — 6% in all. Corporate status as the registry records it: active (51,981).
