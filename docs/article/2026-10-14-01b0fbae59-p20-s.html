Yes, as a guide next to your guitar. It shows you what to play and how it should sound: the chord shapes and fingers, which strings each stroke hits, which way to strum, and whole songs at any speed. Watch it slowly, copy it on your guitar, then play along. It can't hear you or check your hand, so listen to yourself and compare with Guitoro.
