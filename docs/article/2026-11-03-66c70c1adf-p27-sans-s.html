First, the whole argument assumes a strict Platonic view as an obvious fact. Hitting an unexpected counterexample doesn’t prove that theorems are ancient fossils sitting in some cosmic realm waiting for us. It just means formal axiomatic systems have rigid logical rules. If you are a formalist, an AI paper isn’t some “revealed thought of God” at all – it’s just an algorithm grinding through human-made syntax and axioms. Taking Platonism (implicitely) for granted is problematic (though I like it!).

Second, using this Platonic stance to demand “egolessness” feels like a convenient distraction. It turns a massive disruption of mathematical profession into a personal moral lecture.
