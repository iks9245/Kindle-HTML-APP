How did that go over, this idea of trying to build a coalition out of all of these different observatories? And, it’s a really international collaboration. I mean, hundreds of people.

DOELEMAN: I’ll, I’ll put it to you this way. In my group, we spent a lot of time working on instrumentation that would allow a telescope to record information that could then be compared to recordings made at other telescopes. This is how interferometry works on a global scale.

But this was not what these telescopes were doing, so we had to convince these telescopes that we could bring our instrumentation, bolt it in, in some cases manhandle some of their very, very sensitive equipment, operate the telescope in a way that it had not been designed to be operated in, and then take all these disks with data and disappear, and then maybe tell them months later what happened.
