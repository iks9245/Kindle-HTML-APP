DOELEMAN: This is what I live for. I live for putting steel on mountaintops. uh, and Kilimanjaro by far is the best site in the entire African continent to make these kinds of measurements just because of its altitude. Now that being said, it’s a very sensitive site, so we started over three years ago working with The Open University of Tanzania and other institutes in Tanzania to develop the idea. We’re still quite a ways off from doing everything.

The most important thing is to get the technical possibilities in line, and equally important, if not more important, is to make sure that everyone who’s affected at all levels, economic, social, educational, cultural, are all approving it. And what I tell the team is, “No is an acceptable answer, full stop.” But if we can, then I think it would be a tremendous asset for the country, for the community, for education, and of course for astronomy. So by the early 2030s, I think we’ll have real movies of an event horizon.
