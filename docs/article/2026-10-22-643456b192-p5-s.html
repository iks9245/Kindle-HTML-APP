According to a report in the UK's Financial Times newspaper, external, EU regulators are examining possible foreign online interference and disinformation campaigns around the French riots and similar recent outbursts of unrest in EU states.

It quotes unnamed security officials as saying that pro-Russian and far-right social media accounts linked to previous disinformation campaigns have spread violent videos in an attempt to sow more chaos.

Iran and Algeria are also suspected of being behind some of the messaging, the paper adds.
