That, I think, is where physics can still make its greatest contribution. Not by giving us ready-made answers or comforting illusions of inevitability, but by training us to question ourselves and live with the resulting uncertainty. Public discourse is saturated with physics, yet we need a deeper grounding in what it really teaches us about how to ask better questions, to test our own blind spots, and to admit what we don’t know. That need feels more urgent as new technologies only increase the temptation to reach for shortcuts.
