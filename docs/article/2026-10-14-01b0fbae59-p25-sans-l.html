Put your fingers where the dots are, strum, and compare your sound with Guitoro's. A buzzing or dead string usually means a finger is too far from the fret or touching the next string. Many barre chords have an Easy version for while your hand gets stronger.

Open Songs & recordings and pick a song, or paste one under My songs. Press each chord key as it lights green: the song waits for you at every change. Start slow, then raise the speed a little at a time.
