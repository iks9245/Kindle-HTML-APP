First of all, from my personal perspective, thank you to everyone who has reached out in person or online to express support and encouragement. That’s the most important thing. I’m confident that many of the reverted features will come back before too long.

PostgreSQL 19 will still be a great release. REPACK CONCURRENTLY, logical replication of sequences (itself a previously reverted feature), and planner hinting with pg_plan_advice, just to pick a few headline items, will make this release very attractive. And it will be very robust, after all the additional scrutiny.
