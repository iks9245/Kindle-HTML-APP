You can try it today in Safari 27.3 beta. To enable, first make sure your system appearance is set to dark. Then browse to a website with a bright background. Safari will offer a preview of Adaptive Dark Mode. Tap ‘Try’.

For websites that signal support of dark mode with in HTML or color-scheme: light dark in CSS, Adaptive Dark Mode does not change a thing. For the rest of the web, it quickly analyzes the page, looking at colors and estimating whether it’s predominantly light or dark. If the page generally has a dark background with light text, nothing is changed. When a page has a light appearance, Adaptive Dark Mode adapts the page to be dark.
