What came back was not a review of the interviews that make up the book one by one. He says so himself in the opening lines: it isn’t his task to comment on the conversations directly. Instead, he used the occasion to answer the standing objections to his view of deliberative democracy, and to close with the digital transformation that turned out to run through nearly every interview in the book – a theme he noticed himself, and named as central to the collection. One section was on experts, common sense, and the chain of translation that carries specialised knowledge into public reasoning: the same distinction he’d first sketched over a hotel breakfast, worked out now in far more detail, at the very end of his working life.
