Updates to this post

We'll revise this post and mark it updated when any of these change: fixed kernels from Ubuntu or Red Hat, or AlmaLinux's production release; NVD's analysis; a KEV listing or any report of exploitation; the separate IPv6 fix.

- October 31, 5:15 PM CDT: Red Hat raised its rating from Important (8.1) to Critical (9.8) on October 30 and published a mitigation in bulletin RHSB-2026-016, so we replaced its earlier rating and its "no mitigation" statement. Added the Go Security Team's notice that Go programs open MPTCP listeners by default, AlmaLinux's fixed kernels in testing, Amazon Linux's fixes for its remaining kernels, openSUSE Leap 16.0's fix, steps to turn off MPTCP, an iptables version of the interim rule, and the interim rule's limits with bridged container traffic and CIPSO. 36 GitHub repositories now reference the CVE. Ubuntu, Debian, Oracle, NVD's status, and KEV are unchanged, and the stable kernels released October 30 don't include the IPv6 follow-up.
