Even assimilated Italians had their loyalty to their newly adopted lands questioned. It wasn’t just more recently arrived Italians like my great-grandfather who were interned as ‘enemy aliens’ during the Second World War. Some of the 4,000 men who were rounded up had spent most of their lives in Britain. This suspicion was mirrored, to a lesser degree, in Australia and the US – and this while half a million Italian Americans served in the US army during the Second World War, and 7,000 Anglo-Italians served in the British army.
