Each day that I wake up in this house, I am choosing to enact a different script, one in which other people’s concerns are more real to me. My housemate’s labour pains, this baby’s cries, are my business. Conversely, my occasional existential terror or my children’s school struggles are tangible to a wider group of people than they would otherwise be. We have a stake in each other.
