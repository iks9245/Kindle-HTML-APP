| Feel | L chuck, ; palm mute, Shift hard strum, Alt slow strum |

| Songs | The chord keys show the song's chords in order and the next one lights green. With My guitar on, Space plays and pauses the song |

| Solo mode | Each row is a string and each column a fret. Left and Right arrows move up and down the neck, Up bends, Down adds vibrato |

Learn chords

In the key of G these four chords sit on the middle row of keys, and together they play hundreds of pop and folk songs. Press each one and watch the neck: the numbers are your fingers, from 1 for the index to 4 for the little finger. An X marks a string you don't play, and a bar across the strings is a barre.
