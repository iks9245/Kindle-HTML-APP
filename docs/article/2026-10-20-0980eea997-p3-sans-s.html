Horrible, unmanly, indispensable

This militia, most often noted for the role of Niccolò Machiavelli in its foundation, was to be organised after the fashion of the Germans, in whose cities there was a centuries-old tradition of civic defence and a duty on every male citizen to maintain suitable arms for the purpose. In fact, one of the Florentine militia’s early constables was German (a Giovanni Tedesco); another (Giliberto Spagnolo) came from Spain, a country making the running in the use of small arms on the battlefield. Giovanni Tedesco arranged target practice for his company, which from 1508 was extended more generally among the militia, with exercises taking place on a monthly basis.
