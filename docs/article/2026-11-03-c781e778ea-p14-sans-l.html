Anthropic will further pad its cash reserves if its IPO is successful, but the brutal economics of its business are not likely to improve anytime soon. Even if demand picks up significantly, intense competition will apply unrelenting downward pressure on prices even as high costs and resource constraints squeeze it from the opposite direction. That is a deadly mix for Anthropic in light of its slowing revenue growth and massive off-balance-sheet debts, a vast and immovable fixed cost.
