小中大切換為無襯線

Where a land ethic blooms

Out in the field, he expressed his knowledge of the system and loyalty to the land through the language of conversation rather than the rhetoric of seminar or sermon. His knowledge was hard-won. He was now able to translate what he had observed in many a plough furrow to a subtle but discernible pattern in the field, which would otherwise appear as a uniform sea of green to the inattentive mind. He could make predictions, weigh costs and benefits, and chart a sustainable strategy without hardly pausing in his walk across the field. That kind of knowledge is not simply to be stored up, harboured and lost; it can be relayed along in the evolution of a locally adapted culture as he conveys, in situ, the knowledge to following generations.
