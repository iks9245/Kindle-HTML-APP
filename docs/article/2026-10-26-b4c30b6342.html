Venezuelan opposition leader María Machado returns after months in exile

- Published

Venezuelan opposition leader María Corina Machado has returned home to a fraught political landscape after more than 10 months in exile.

"I am here. With you," Machado wrote on X on Sunday. Her return comes after repeated prior efforts to re-enter the country came to no avail.

In January, a month after Machado left Venezuela on a speedboat under cover of darkness to accept the Nobel Peace Prize in Norway, the US seized her opponent, President Nicolás Maduro.
