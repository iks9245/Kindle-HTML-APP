LEVIN: The better the resolution.

DOELEMAN: The better the resolution, precisely. And so there are some objects that are so small, and black holes are the smallest objects predicted by Einstein’s theory of gravity, that you need extremely big telescopes, extremely fine angular resolution to see them, and you’ve gotta take this idea of turning the Earth into a telescope to the utmost extremes.

You have to operate it at wavelengths that had never been operated before ’cause you have to really go to smaller and smaller wavelengths to get the angular resolution you need. And that’s what took us 20 years, to push the technique to the absolute limit.
