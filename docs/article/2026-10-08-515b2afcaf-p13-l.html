Israelis mourn 7 October attack victims three years after deadly Hamas raid

There has been no end to Gaza's deadliest ever war, as Israel continues to carry out near-daily attacks.

More than 1,460 people have been killed there since the ceasefire took effect, according to Gaza health ministry. The Israeli military says at least seven Israeli soldiers have been killed in the strip over the same period.

Most of Gaza is in ruins, with nearly all the 2.1 million population still displaced amid dire humanitarian conditions.
