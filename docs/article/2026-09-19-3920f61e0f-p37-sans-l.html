Cosmic amnesia

This is the no-hair theorem twice over. First in the same sense as the room: zoom out, and the fine details wash into a handful of numbers, the history buried among a mass of indeterminate possibilities. But the black hole adds something new. The big picture is the only view we are ever allowed to have. The details disappear behind the event horizon. We therefore remain unremittingly ignorant of the black hole’s creation. The past becomes inaccessible. The black hole forgets.
