This provides the basic framework for solving the regress problem. Perceptual experiences are not themselves beliefs, yet they provide reasons for belief. And they do so without relying on antecedent justified beliefs and are not themselves in need of justification. Still, this leaves us with an important question: what is it about these experiences that allows them to justify belief in this way?
