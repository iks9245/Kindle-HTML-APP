US inmate Christa Pike says she woke up 'confused and in pain' after failed execution

Bethany Mann, a friend of Pike's and a founder of Child Arise Tennessee, which supports children and families affected by parental incarceration, told the BBC on Friday that she had been in direct contact with Pike since the botched execution attempt.

When asked about her reaction to Pike's statement, Mann said: "Truthfully, I don't know that I have anything to add to what Christa has already expressed so powerfully herself, and I want to be respectful of her privacy."

Tennessee Governor Bill Lee paused all executions for the remainder of the year and ordered an independent review to "determine exactly what occurred".
