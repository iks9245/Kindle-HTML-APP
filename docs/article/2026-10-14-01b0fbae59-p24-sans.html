FAQ

Yes. It runs in your browser with no download and no sign-up.

A video shows a teacher's hand from one angle, at one speed, and the hand hides which strings it hits. Guitoro plays the song's part close to the original recording on a guitar you can see whole: each string lights up as it's hit, the pick shows every down and up stroke, and you set the speed. Then you play it yourself and the song waits for you.
