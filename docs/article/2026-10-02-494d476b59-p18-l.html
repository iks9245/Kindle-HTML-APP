Such initiatives democratized the web as they helped a wide range of people to establish their own web sites and communities – local businesses, self-employed professionals, art and craft groups, neighborhood initiatives, schools and kindergartens, sports clubs, LGBTQ+ folks, fandoms etc.

Today, we often hear the word “democratize” from GenAI promoters, but it means the exact opposite. AI centralizes power and commodifies knowledge, instead of teaching skills and deep understanding.

The web is a radical democratic medium. Right now, tech oligarchs and fascist movements are trying to dismantle its radical potential. We must ensure that everyone can shape the web.
