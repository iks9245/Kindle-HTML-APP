小中大切換為襯線

Where a land ethic blooms

In the process of reorganisation, exchange in other ecological currencies multiplies, for countless organisms dwell in the palace of wildflowers. Most herbivores and omnivores are smaller than a bison and lack the microbial symbiosis that provides access to grass. Instead, they depend upon the more digestible leaves, pollen, nectar, sugary fruit, starchy tubers and seeds, and protein-rich leaf buds that wildflowers and flowering shrubs produce. Badgers, rabbits, mice and voles, prairie chickens, grouse, turtles and waterfowl can all benefit. The less efficient plants host many species of insect larvae, which in turn feed predatory insects, songbird hatchlings and others on the food web. And so the web of connections rolls out and across the prairies in this fashion.
