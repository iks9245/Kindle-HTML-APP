Deskilling the body

Consider what happens if you’re thirsty. The process starts before you consciously decide to have a drink. Rather, the osmoreceptor neurons in the hypothalamus shrink slightly as water leaves them. That physical change opens ion channels, letting sodium enter cells. This altered electrical state then opens calcium channels, and calcium flows in. Calcium triggers tiny sacs to release neurotransmitters into the space between cells, passing the signal to other neurons until the state of being thirsty turns to action. The movements through which we quench our thirst arise from minute physical changes in interconnected parts of our bodies. These signals help guide us towards water and make us aware of our thirst.
