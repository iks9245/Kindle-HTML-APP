- Su (2021) derives a scale of $\log M$ from keeping the attention’s entropy constant as $M$ grows, normalized to match standard attention at $M = L$. The logits are multiplied by $\log M / \log L$ at every length. Scalable-Softmax has the same form, multiplying the logits by $s \log M$ with $s$ learned.

- The original Qwen models use LogN-Scaling, which applies the same factor only once the context is longer than $L$.

- Qwen2.5-1M uses YaRN’s attention scaling, which multiplies the logits by $(0.1 \log(M/L) + 1)^2$, so it grows like $(\log M)^2$.
