DOELEMAN: It was pretty amazing. It was humbling. There were times when I was close to tears during part of this because you work for something so long in your career, and it winds up being so meaningful, and then to see that it’s impactful for others is startling. I mean, it was on the front page of most newspapers around the globe, and you realize it touches a core because you’re seeing something for the first time.

And so it was with awe, humility, but also wonder, a lot of relief because after 20 years, it’s a lot of work and effort under the bridge. But also the team that came together to realize it was very special. We needed 250 people to pull this off. And we’d been through a lot together.
