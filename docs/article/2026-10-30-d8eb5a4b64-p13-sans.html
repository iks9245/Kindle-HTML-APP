Bed Wars is a popular game format where teams fight to destroy each other’s bed while protecting their own. In our instance, teams started on separate islands, with currency spawners between them in no-man’s-land. Agents could build bridges to cross between islands and buy upgrades using currency collected in the center of the map.

A team wins by destroying the opposing side’s bed, then killing their remaining players. As long as a team’s bed is present, players respawn after dying. Once a team’s bed is destroyed, their players no longer respawn, meaning their next death eliminates them from the match. This may seem complicated, but think of the team’s main objective as defending their bed and destroying their opponent’s. That said, if no beds were destroyed in a given match, the team with the most kills won.
