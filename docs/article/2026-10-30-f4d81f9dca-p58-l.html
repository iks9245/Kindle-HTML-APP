DOELEMAN: So for example, the supermassive black hole in the center of the Milky Way galaxy is 4 million times the mass of the sun. At the distance of the center of the galaxy, about 27,000 light years, the whole lensed part of the event horizon subtends about 50 microarcseconds.

Now, that’s insanely small. That’s like, you know, if you’re in New York City and your friend is holding a quarter in Los Angeles, you can read the date on that quarter. Or if there’s a tangerine or really a citrus fruit of any variety on the moon, you could see that fruit. Even a pomelo, possibly a tangerine.
