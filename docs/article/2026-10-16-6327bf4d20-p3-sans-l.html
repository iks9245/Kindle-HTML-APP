Let's be clear: writing data to Apple Health via HealthKit happens locally on the phone. It does not require complex cloud compute, server costs, or any "AI connector". It's a basic API call. I know we can still manually export a CSV file, but let's be real—nobody wants to manually import CSV files in 2026. Even the absolute cheapest, no-name smart scales on the market offer automatic Apple Health synchronization for free.

Taking a fundamental feature that has always been free and locking it behind a paywall for new users is an obvious cash grab. It ruins the hardware experience and destroys the trust in the brand.
