Paternity is poetical

But not only poems. Because poems are short and often time-stamped, they make legible the changing way a father may look upon and respond to the deep changes of fatherhood. The same forces are surely powerful, if harder to trace, in creative work by parents more widely, and echo across our cultural moment. We live, in the West, in an age of sensitive dads, dad jokes, and dad dancing: all signs of a cultural revaluation of fatherhood, freed from the stigma of patriarchy. The consequences are political as well as poetic. In The Argonauts (2015), Maggie Nelson powerfully describes the imaginative work and possibility of artistic freedom that come with parenthood, quoting the feminist thinker Susan Fraiman’s call for ‘the decline of the domestic as a separate, inherently female sphere and the vindication of domesticity as an ethic, an affect, an aesthetic, and a public.’ The poetry of fatherhood may take a thousand forms: the tender strangeness of Coleridge, the jumpy brightness of Lowell, the moving and original memoirs and novels by contemporary poets and writers including Ben Lerner and Jack Underwood as well as Nelson. But for each, the material entanglement of creativity and parenthood is a prompt, not a hindrance, to wild new work.

延伸問答

文章挑戰的「父職與創造力」的傳統觀點是什麼？

傳統上，人們認為藝術創作需要男性藝術家與家庭生活保持距離和獨處，將家庭事務視為阻礙創意的因素。赫克托和高更等人的例子，以及「大廳裡的嬰兒車是好藝術的敵人」這句話，都體現了這種觀點。

文章從神經科學的角度如何解釋父職與創造力之間的關聯？

文章指出，神經科學研究顯示父職會提高情感與認知需求，促使大腦將多元經驗整合為有目的的意義感。這與創造性過程主要發生在大腦自動且無意識區域的觀點相符，暗示父職有助於激發深層的創造力。

除了詩歌，這種對父職的重新評估在更廣泛的文化或政治背景中如何體現？

文章指出，文化上體現在「敏感老爸」、「老爸笑話」和「老爸舞」的興起，擺脫了父權制偏見。政治上，它支持將家庭生活視為一種倫理和美學，不再是女性專屬領域的女性主義觀點。
