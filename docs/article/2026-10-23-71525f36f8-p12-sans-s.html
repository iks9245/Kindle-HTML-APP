Having tested the concept, we embarked on a labyrinthically complex joint house-purchase. It fell through on the day of a long-delayed exchange, after we’d reached the end of the notice period on our rental. For months, we had no fixed address and scattered to the sofas and spare beds of friends and family. My youngest child began having nightmares. As I held him at 4 am, he asked: ‘Why couldn’t we stay in our flat, just us?’ The script glowed temptingly. If living with others was the answer, surely it shouldn’t be this difficult. We held our nerve.
