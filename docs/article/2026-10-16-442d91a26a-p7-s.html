STROGATZ: Toby Kiers is her name. She is an evolutionary biologist at the Vrije University in Amsterdam, and a recent MacArthur Fellow. So if you’re ready, here we go.

[Music plays]

STROGATZ: Welcome to The Joy of Why, Toby.

TOBY KIERS: Thanks so much about having me.

STROGATZ: Well, this is an area that is not familiar to me at all, and I’m dying to hear about it. Can you describe what your subject area is first, just in very broad terms?

KIERS: I study the class of organisms called mycorrhizal fungi, and the word mycorrhizal means myco, which means fungus, and rhizal, which means root. So these are a class of fungi that form a very intimate association with the vast majority of plants. So over 80% of all plant species on Earth form these partnerships, and it’s a very old partnership.
