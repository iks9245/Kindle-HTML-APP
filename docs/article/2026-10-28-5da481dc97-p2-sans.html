Although the Finnish leader said the conversation with Putin had been "frank and substantive," Støre added that there were "big differences in views" with Putin.

Several European leaders have recently shown willingness to engage with Russia directly, but both Stubb and Støre pointed out their initiative was taken in their capacity as neighbours of Russia, rather than on behalf of Europe as a whole.

"We've not set out to speak on behalf of anyone other than ourselves," Støre said.

Stubb added the call had been prepared in consultation with Ukraine and the US following a meeting between Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky, the Nordic leaders and US special envoy Steve Witkoff in Oslo last week.
