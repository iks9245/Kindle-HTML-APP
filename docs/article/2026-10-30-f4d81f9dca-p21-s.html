How Do You Take a Picture of a Black Hole?

For the supermassive black holes, there people could see the energy coming out of these jets, and really the only mechanism that we could think of was magnetic fields twisting in the warped space time of the black hole itself. And like egg beaters twisting around, they fling these charged particles out at near the speed of light. And the observations were so extreme that really that’s the only model that fit. And miraculously, as you got better and better angular resolution, as you trace the jet all the way down to the center, it kept looking like this jet.

It didn’t break this model. You kept seeing this jet getting closer and closer and closer, and there was this mad idea that we had to see what was causing this.
