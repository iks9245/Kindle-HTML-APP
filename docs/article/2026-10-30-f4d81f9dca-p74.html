DOELEMAN: So there’s a couple things. So one is that spin is elusive. So, a, a black hole basically has only a few characteristics that define it. Its mass, its spin, maybe its charge, but most of the astrophysical black holes that we know of probably have charge equalized, because there’s plasma around it that would do that. So the mass is pretty easy. The size of this ring is directly related to mass. We’ve checked that one off.

But the spin of the black hole imprints its history, like how it grew over time, and the spin also tells you about the frame dragging, the warping of spacetime as it spins. And to get a handle on that, you need to really look at dynamics of matter.
