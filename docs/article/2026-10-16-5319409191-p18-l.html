The response in Vienna was disbelief, then dismay. The Circle had built its philosophy on the assumption that mathematics was analytic; Gödel had shown it wasn’t as simple as that. Carnap struggled for years to absorb the result. Wittgenstein dismissed it, but was already repudiating the Tractatus himself. Meaning, he came to argue, lay not in matching picture to reality but simply in how people discourse in public. The one-time prophet of the Circle left nothing of its philosophy standing.
