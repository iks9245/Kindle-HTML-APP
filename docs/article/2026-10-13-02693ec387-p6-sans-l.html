Is everything conscious?

Panpsychism is a remarkable thesis, motivated by the ‘perennial hope’ that the world is ultimately intelligible. To investigate whether panpsychism truly can make the world intelligible, we will explore three different arguments, each of which is grounded in a principle that crystallises the ambitious aim of universal intelligibility: the principle of sufficient reason (PSR). This simple and plausible principle of philosophical enquiry – that there exist satisfying explanations for everything – can lead to a whole new understanding of the nature of reality. And, though I’m sceptical about the availability or even desirability of explanations that dissolve all the world’s mysteries, there is exceptional value in the mere contemplation of such projects.
