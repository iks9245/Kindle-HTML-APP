Later in the century, larger cities had shops where guns might be mended and probably also purchased. A 1559 proclamation issued in Bologna in anticipation of riots declared that ‘shopkeepers, who sell those polearms and arquebuses, must store them in their shops in such a way that they are not ready to use’. There is no reason to think that the anonymous author who complained of wheellock-toting shepherds was greatly exaggerating the extent of proliferation.

Guns had a role to play in changing ideas about gender, both in general and with reference to images of rulership
