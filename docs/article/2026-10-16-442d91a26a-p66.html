And so what I wanted to do was start an organization, a nonprofit, that was able to draw attention to all of this incredible peer-reviewed research that had been being published, honestly, for decades showing the importance of mycorrhizal networks, but that just wasn’t being paid attention to.

So in 2021, I founded an organization called SPUN, which stands for the Society for the Protection of Underground Networks. And it had an audition, a very, very audacious mission, and that was to map the biodiversity of these mycorrhizal networks across Earth and start advocating for their protection.
