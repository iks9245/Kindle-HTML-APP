小中大切換為無襯線

Tear gas in Paris and Marseille as school protests grow across France

Prime Minister Sébastien Lecornu condemned the earlier violence and said since the protests had begun 215 students and 85 staff had been hurt as well as 715 police officers.

The government said there had been disruption in 891 high schools on Tuesday - almost a quarter of all lycées - and said more than 250,000 demonstrators had taken to the streets during the day.

Students' unions put the number at 450,000, and said the number of high schools blockaded was 1,800 on what the protesters dubbed "Act Three" of their movement.

In Marseille, thousands of demonstrators moved around this city, stopping momentarily to chant before moving on as police changed direction.
