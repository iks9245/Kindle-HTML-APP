Audio reveals exchanges between Spain train operators and drivers moments before deadly crash

The crash is one of the worst rail disasters in Europe's modern history, and Spain's worst crash since 2013. On Thursday, the country began three days of national mourning.

The moving train, run by state-owned operator Renfe, had 397 passengers on board. The stationary train was run by private operator Iryo, and carrying 147 passengers.

A reported issue with the automatic signalling system - a computer failure - had forced all trains to halt on the affected stretch.

But later the Renfe train was ordered to proceed in "error", according to Spain's transport minister.
