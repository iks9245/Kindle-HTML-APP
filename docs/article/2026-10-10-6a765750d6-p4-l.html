Barbara Ward’s vision

Barbara Ward was a remarkable thinker, a visionary who imagined a ‘planetary’ political, economic and environmental justice based on both material and spiritual principles. She was an unusual figure in the Cold War political sphere, a charismatic public intellectual who advised world leaders and the United Nations at a time when women’s voices were often marginalised. She was among the first thinkers to argue that human prosperity, environmental sustainability and global justice were inseparable political questions. Her distinctive vision of planetary politics, grounded in ecological interdependence, moral responsibility and global cooperation, still offers insights into some of the most pressing challenges of the 21st century.
