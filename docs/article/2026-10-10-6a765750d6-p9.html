One successful ‘revolution’ was the Marshall Plan for the development of Europe after the Second World War, which Ward praised as an example of international economic planning. She suggested that Europe and the US should apply a similar model and provide financial aid to promote local economic development in India. Historically, Europe and the US prospered from the continuous plunder of colonial capital and resources. Imperialism caused ‘not only a considerable degree of political immaturity and instability’ but ‘also a type of economic structure or layout which is unlikely to produce further growth of itself’. For Ward, these structural inequalities in the world economy prevented postcolonial countries from overcoming poverty on their own.

Her proposals for the development of postcolonial societies expressed a paternalistic, hierarchical worldview
