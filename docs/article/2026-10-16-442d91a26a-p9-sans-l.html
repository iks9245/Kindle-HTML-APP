STROGATZ: Well, so I spoke to someone recently that changed my perspective about these fungi under our feet and the wood wide web. Not the worldwide web, the wood wide web. It’s the mycorrhizal network of these vast networks of fungi that we never notice that are underground, and they’re incredibly important in connecting the roots and supplying nutrients to trees and other plants. Something like 80% to 90% of all the plant species all over the world are getting help from these fungi.
