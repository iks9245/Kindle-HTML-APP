Open a song with the strum you want and slow it to 50% or less. The pick swings down on the numbers and up on the "and"s: solid when it hits the strings, faint when it misses. The strings light up to show which ones each stroke hits.

Turn Auto off, turn on the Band switch or the metronome, and strum along with H and J or by swiping the strings.

Record a take and listen for uneven strokes. When it sounds steady, speed up.
