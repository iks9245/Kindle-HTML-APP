This evidence warrants reconsideration of current assessment and treatment paradigms. Implementation of routine circadian phenotyping in ADHD evaluation, coupled with evidence-based chronotherapeutic interventions, represents a pragmatic approach to improving outcomes. While not proposing that ADHD be reclassified exclusively as a circadian disorder, the evidence supports recognition of a prevalent circadian phenotype that, when present, may benefit from targeted chronotherapeutic intervention alongside standard ADHD treatments.
