Israelis mourn 7 October attack victims three years after deadly Hamas raid

In Tel Aviv's Dizengoff Square, Israeli anti-government protesters dyed the fountain red to mark the anniversary of the attack.

The fountain has been used as a memorial site for those killed, with pictures, flowers and messages laid around the basin wall.

Protesters also briefly blocked one of the main highways in the city to hold a moment of silence on Wednesday morning.

They held banners that read, "the people will remember their sons and daughters", along with others that said "a complete failure" and "guilty" next to an image of Netanyahu.
