In 2024, it allowed Alabama to proceed with a second execution of Kenneth Eugene Smith, using an experimental oxygen deprivation method, after their attempt to kill him by lethal injection failed two years earlier.

A notable Supreme Court case from 1947 allowed the state of Louisiana to try a second electrocution of Willie Smith, a teenager whose first execution attempt failed when he was 17 years old.

The court found a second execution would not violate the Constitution's prohibitions against double jeopardy or cruel and unusual punishment and Smith was executed aged 18.
