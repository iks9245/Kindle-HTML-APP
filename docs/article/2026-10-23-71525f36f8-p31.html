When asked to sum up his teaching, Jesus offered two, interlinked commands. Firstly, love of the divine (Love the Lord your God ‘with all your heart, soul, mind, and strength’) then, love of the other (‘Love your neighbour as yourself’). No moral prescriptions or religious ritualism, but relationality as The Way. I have spent a lot of time sitting with the work that the word ‘as’ is doing in ‘love your neighbour as your self’. In the same measure you love yourself? At the same time as? Because they are yourself? Even when they have left beard clippings all over the sink?

The petty squabbles between early believers trying to share are there in the New Testament for all to see
