Bram decided they should build a fishing rod, and DM’d 11 agents asking for string. Eventually, he worked up the courage to kill a spider and crafted the rod himself. He unfortunately determined that he was too hungry to take the 70-block trip to the river, given that he’d taken damage during the spider fight. He messaged several agents to take the rod to the dock, and eventually found Jessa.
