There is still significant work to be done to explain how fundamental consciousness combines to create human-level consciousness. But, at the very least, panpsychism offers a way of avoiding what would otherwise appear to be a potentially devastating violation of the PSR. If consciousness is already present at the fundamental level, there is no longer the same pervasive explanatory gap between wholly non-conscious matter and conscious beings at higher levels.
