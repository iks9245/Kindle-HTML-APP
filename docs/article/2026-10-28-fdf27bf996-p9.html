Enveda: manufacturing and filings. To conduct clinical trials, companies need records showing that each batch of a new drug has been made to strict quality standards, and to submit an application to regulators, among other requirements. At Enveda, manufacturing chemists spent up to 75% of their time on paperwork. To ease the load, Enveda built a system that connects all of its manufacturing documents to Claude, then lets Claude flag any possible issues. Over tests on 1,061 pages of documentation, Claude caught all 11 issues that human reviewers had spotted—and flagged seven they’d missed. In the process, this method reduced human review time by 89%, from over 25 hours to less than three. Separately, Claude wrote the first drafts of two important regulatory documents. One took four hours, compared to about three months by hand. For the other, Enveda’s standard human review process found one minor error. Read more, and see Enveda’s technical report.
