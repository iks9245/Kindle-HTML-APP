What Shall I Teach My Son?

What is the purpose of education in the age of AGI?

October 19, 2026

Yesterday, the title The End of Education by Neil Postman called to me from my bookshelf, whispering that it might be the right book at the right time. It was. Published in 1995, it was surprisingly appropriate balm for the soul of a confused programmer and soon-to-be father.

My wife and I are expecting our first child. My economically valuable skillset – programming – suddenly feels less valuable. What shall I teach my son? And to what end?

Mr. Edison says

That the radio will supplant the teacher.
