The move stunned many of Stevens' fellow Democrats who condemned her decision to break from the party and switch her support just two days before the election, with one calling it a "despicable betrayal".

The public feud between Stevens, a centrist Democrat, and El-Sayed, who represents the party's left-wing, has boiled over in recent days.

Stevens called on El-Sayed to denounce a campaign advert from an outside liberal group that linked US support for Israel with high prices. El-Sayed criticised the advert and said he had nothing to do with its contents.

Stevens had been scheduled to appear at an event with El-Sayed on Sunday in an effort to display party unity, but he reportedly asked her not to come and she chose to attend a church service with his Republican opponent instead.
