DOELEMAN: I remember being at the Large Millimeter Telescope in Mexico and watching as they winched one of these atomic clocks up the central part of a helical staircase, and just like wondering what I was doing at 15,000 feet watching this precision timepiece being winched up and into place.

And then we put it into a wooden enclosure because, you know, it needed to be at a certain temperature, and we used light bulbs inside to heat it to just the right temperature. So you get very imaginative when you’re at the top of a mountain, how am I gonna keep this atomic clock you know, swaddled?
