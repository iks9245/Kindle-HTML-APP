Producing spider silk in the lab could result in a new range of strong yet flexible materials. Kevlar, a synthetic fiber, is currently our toughest material, but it requires processing at very high temperatures and acidic conditions. “Here, you have an organism like a spider or a mussel,” Harrington said, “which is basically making a superior material under ambient conditions in seawater or in just plain air.”
