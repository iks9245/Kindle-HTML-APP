"We believe that a vehicle entered the Tiergarten park and hit several people and injured them," police posted on social media.

Images show a large emergency services presence at the scene, with one person seen entering an ambulance on a stretcher.

It is not yet clear how many people have been injured in the incident.

A number of police officers as well as rescue units from the fire department have been deployed to the area.

Earlier on Saturday, huge crowds took part in a parade throughout the city, which ended near the park for a series of events in the evening.
