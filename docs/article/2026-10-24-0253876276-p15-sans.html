It’s possible that the moving junction could be something of a skeleton key that could be used to treat other parasitic diseases. Plasmodium is a member of the apicomplexans, a large phylum of parasitic protists that also includes Toxoplasma gondii, a cat parasite, and Cyclospora, which causes explosive diarrhea. These single-celled organisms appear to enter using the same biochemical equipment as Plasmodium. “All of these are parasites that have this invasion machinery,” Deitsch said. The study of those conditions could in turn inform malaria treatment. A 2026 paper described the mechanism by which Toxoplasma senses the right moment to deploy its moving junction, and malaria may operate the same way.
