In a separate development on Wednesday, Zelensky said he talked "in great detail for an hour-and-a-half" with US President Donald Trump's peace envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner ahead of trilateral negotiations between Ukraine, Russia and the US on 10-11 November in Abu-Dhabi.

The Ukrainian leader - who is currently making a surprise visit to Spain - also said his team had a separate "meaningful" meeting in Brussels with representatives from the US, the EU, France, Britain, Germany, as well as Nato and the World Bank about "post-war reconstruction".

He added that the meeting in Belgium's capital would continue on Thursday to discuss "security guarantees" for Ukraine.
