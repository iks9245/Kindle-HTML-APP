"Sometimes a child comes back to us after a week, or two days," says Dr Mohammed. "Sometimes it is the same day. They come back and we admit them and the story begins again."

They return because parents cannot afford proper nutrition or vitamins or antibiotics.

"Every child here, his parents came because they don't have anything," she tells me. "They don't have money or insurance. They are here because they can't afford a private hospital."

She does her best for them, she says, and she cries for them.
