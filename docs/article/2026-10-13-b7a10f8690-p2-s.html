"Contacts are continuing," Peskov replied.

"As soon as we have anything to say about this, we will definitely inform you."

Peskov may not have had much to say on the matter, but the pro-Kremlin media has.

It has dubbed President Trump's announcement of an energy ceasefire "a gift" for Moscow.

"Of course we benefit," pro-government news outlet Moskovsky Komsomolets concluded.

"It means that all Russia has to do is not destroy Ukraine's last remaining oil refinery.

"That has already been attacked several times. It's barely functioning anyway.

"But Kyiv won't be allowed to hit the multitude of refineries we have.

"In effect it's a one-way deal that benefits us. That's why no one here has officially rejected it."
