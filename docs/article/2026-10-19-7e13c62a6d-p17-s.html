- Why are Florida condo assessments rising?

- After the Surfside collapse, Senate Bill 4-D (2022) required milestone structural inspections of older buildings and fully funded structural reserves that owners can no longer waive; the repairs and the reserves are paid by the owners through dues and special assessments.
