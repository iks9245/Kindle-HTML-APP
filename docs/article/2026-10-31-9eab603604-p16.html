小中大切換為無襯線

In praise of the dilettante

A personal example. A few years ago, I joined Mountain Haulers, a cycling group that meets on Tuesday evenings for a ride through Asheville, North Carolina. It is a ‘no drop’ ride: cyclists wait at points along the way for everyone to catch up, however slow or struggling, and the first ride of the month is always a chill ride along the river. This openness sets the group apart from a cycling culture of notorious cliques of devotees clamouring to outdo each other’s worship of the sport. Welcoming the less-experienced and the less-committed seems a more genuine way of loving the thing you claim to love. If biking is your thing, surely you want more butts on bike seats. The dilettante cyclists who show up for Tuesday-night rides make cycling better.
