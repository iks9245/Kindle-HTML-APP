Major fire at Canada military base leaves four dead, dozens injured

- Published

Four members of the Canadian military have died and dozens were injured in a major fire at a base in the province of Alberta.

Addressing the nation on Sunday from Ottawa, Prime Minister Mark Carney described it as "one of the worst tragedies for the forces on Canadian soil in peacetime".
