A life in episodes

The process matters far more to me than the result; even so, I have not ruled out publishing the final text as a book. But that intention sits uneasily with everything I have just argued: to edit the stream of episodes into a single volume risks imposing a coherence that was never there. Perhaps a narrative will emerge in the editing; perhaps it will not.

Autobiography, as I now practise it, is a series of soundings of how I relate to episodes from my life in light of today’s feelings, knowledge and relationships. What I resist is not coherence but the imperative to impose it.
