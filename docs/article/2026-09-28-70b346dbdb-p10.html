小中大切換為無襯線

Inside Yemen's front-line city as Houthis battle for control

Some patients were fighters, others were civilians. Twelve-year-old Ghana Nabeel had been herding goats when shrapnel struck her head. Her family said an incoming Houthi mortar had landed nearby.

She lay unconscious, her head wrapped in bandages, her face swollen and bruised. Her family were waiting at her bedside to learn what life might be like if she wakes up.

A doctor told me the injury had damaged her brain. "She will never be the same again," he said.

About 20km south of Taiz, at the al-Monig displacement camp, families who had fled Mokha and villages hit by fighting were arriving, among hundreds of tents.

We were told that 200 families had arrived in the previous 10 days.
