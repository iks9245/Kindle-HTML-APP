小中大切換為無襯線

A life in episodes

Looking back at my own feuilleton, I realise I am closer to Strawson’s episodic self-understanding than to the retrospective narrative of classical autobiography. I did not begin with the vision of telling a story about myself: I began with the basement archive, the memory fragments and the conversations with friends. I bring out letters, diary entries, remembered images and the voices of others, trying to do justice to a situation, a mood, a conflict, a crossroads. Each episode must be readable on its own; the reader need not know everything that has preceded it, nor guess what is to come. If there is a Bildungsreise, it is full of detours, interruptions, dead-ends and retreats. If there is a moral, it is local and provisional – not a step in an overarching arc of personal development.
