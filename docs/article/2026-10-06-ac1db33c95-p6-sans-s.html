A life in episodes

I can still vividly recall the thrill of exploring this unique archive. That afternoon set in motion a seven-year cooperation. At the outset, we discussed the possibility of weaving biography and autobiography together; we toyed with the idea of a book in a parallel format, with my biographical text on the left-hand pages and his personal recollections on the right – auto/biography as interlocution.

The vision of a side-by-side account soon faded, however, because Jerne – who was then in his mid-70s – lacked the stamina to draft his own memoir. Instead, through hundreds of hours of conversation, I managed to elicit a wealth of memory fragments that continuously enriched, and occasionally disrupted, my developing account of him.
