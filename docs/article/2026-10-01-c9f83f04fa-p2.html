小中大切換為無襯線

Passportless mess

In this entertaining short documentary from the Czech filmmaker Maja Penčič, truth and myth collide in the figure of Zoran, a former resident of a block of flats in Belgrade, Serbia, whose larger-than-life personality and convoluted biography have left behind accounts that can’t be reconciled. Penčič sets out to find Zoran, or at least to understand who he was, by surveying the people who claim to have known him at different stages of his life. Throughout, a rotating cast of acquaintances characterise him as everything from ‘warm’, ‘intelligent’ and ‘exceptional’ to someone who ‘never feels shame’ to, quite simply, ‘a dick’. The home-video aesthetic lends the proceedings an intimacy and a faintly surreal quality that evokes the imperfections of memory. The film never settles on a single version of events, instead watching the details rearrange themselves around a few fixed points, until what is left is less a man than a cautionary tale.

延伸問答

電影標題《無護照的混亂》暗示了什麼？

這個標題可能暗示佐蘭的人生缺乏清晰的身份或記錄，他的存在本身就是一種混亂和謎團，難以被官方或社會所定義。這與他複雜且前後矛盾的生平敘述相呼應。

為什麼影片最終將佐蘭描繪成一個「警世故事」？

佐蘭的故事警示我們，對於一個人的認識往往受到記憶的主觀性和敘事者立場的影響，真相可能難以捉摸。它也可能在提醒人們，當一個人的生平變得如此支離破碎和矛盾時，個人的身份和影響會如何被歷史與記憶重新塑造。

影片中的「家庭錄像美學」如何強化了其主題？

這種美學風格為影片增添了親密感和一絲超現實的特質，巧妙地呼應了記憶的不完美和不可靠性。它讓觀眾更能體會到不同敘述之間的不確定性，進而反思真相的多元與複雜。
