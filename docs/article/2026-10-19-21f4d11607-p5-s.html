A new unit

DynPkgTypes.pas

has been added to the RTL to help you with reading information from dynamic packages. The same tried and true RTTI information can be gather from a package to work with its contents. There are enough functions in the RTL to work with dynamic packages to do everything you need, including loading, enumerating, searching, and unloading packages at runtime.
