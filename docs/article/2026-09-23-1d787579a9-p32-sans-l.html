This is one of the tensions running through the modern conversation about psychedelics and plant medicines like Amanita muscaria. Some proponents of plant medicine argue that science should not be treated as the only legitimate way of evaluating the veracity of therapeutic claims. They point to traditional and Indigenous uses of psychoactive plants and fungi, sometimes over millennia, and ask why randomised controlled trials should outrank lived experience, ritual knowledge or community practice.
