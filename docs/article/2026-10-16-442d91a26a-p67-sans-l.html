STROGATZ: I notice you used the word “microbe” just now, and I feel like we’ve maybe been giving short shrift to, maybe in your way of thinking the fungi are microbes. I’m thinking here more in terms of the bacteria that I know you’ve sometimes studied and played with.

KIERS: Yeah, exactly. I mean, it’s, it’s actually a big debate within mycology in general is like, are these fungal networks, are they microbes? In some cases they can be centimeters to meters long, right? So that’s … but they’re invisible to the naked eye. And so are they a microbe? It really depends on your definition.
