More recently, we’ve also begun working with Bristol Myers Squibb (BMS) to find and address bottlenecks in clinical trial planning. BMS is using Claude to assess plans of an early-stage drug in clinical trials, and to estimate the likelihood of those trials succeeding. Together, we plan to expand this work in 2027 to understand its potential for increasing the probability of success in early development.
