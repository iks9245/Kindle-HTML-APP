小中大切換為襯線

The self-making world

Lamarck had set Aristotle’s ancient Scale of Nature not just in motion but in a great commotion. For two millennia, since the time of Aristotle, natural philosophers had imagined every being ranked from simplest to most complex in a fixed, unchanging order. Each form of life had its appointed place in the hierarchy. But now Lamarck announced that, rather than being arrayed along the Scale of Nature, living beings were themselves ever making and remaking the order of life by their own incessant activity, and in many directions at once.
