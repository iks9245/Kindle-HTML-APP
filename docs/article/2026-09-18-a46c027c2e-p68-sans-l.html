There is a quantum mechanical explanation. The quantum mechanical explanation is it’s described by a wave, and the wave is a non-local thing that spreads out over space. And it’s only when you try to think of it as two separate particles that it looks like two particles with different states. The wave is like a hologram, and every little bit of the hologram contains a whole. That’s the best analogy, actually.
