They were particularly skeptical of the significance of Lindsey’s second question. The steering technique at the heart of Lindsey’s experiment was originally developed as a way to shape the responses models give. Lindsey’s steering was weaker than it had been in the Golden Gate demo — more like gently nudging the model than hijacking its internal processes. Still, a nudge might be enough to get the model talking about secrets or vegetables no matter what prompt it received. Lederman and Mahowald suspected that this effect alone could explain the models’ vivid answers to the second question.
