There are, of course, features that work in the opposite direction. The delayed message and the pop-up that asks: ‘Are you sure you want to delete this?’ are two examples. Such features interrupt automatic action and return a fragment of judgment to the user. A nature documentary with linearity and the interpretive presence of an adult voice is a great model for children’s media; for example, in the TV show Prehistoric Planet (2022-25), David Attenborough’s voiceover and the camera’s zoom-ins encourage children to look closely and wonder about unfamiliar life. But these are exceptions within a larger system in which speed is treated as the default.
