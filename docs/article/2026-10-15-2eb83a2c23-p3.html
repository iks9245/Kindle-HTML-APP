Student protesters attack government buildings in Albania

Education Mirela Kumbaro said that while she respects the right to protest, "civic student protest and violence against public buildings and property are two different things".

Officials have criticised the behaviour of some protesters.

"Setting fire to an institution is not a peaceful protest. It is an act that endanger's peoples lives", said economy minister Delina Ibrahimaj, responding to a video appearing to show protesters throwing rocks at the finance ministry building.

Sixteen people were arrested "in connection with violent acts" during Tuesday's protest, Albania's state-run news agency said.

Sali Berisha, the leader of the opposition - the Democratic Party of Albania - has praised the protesters.
