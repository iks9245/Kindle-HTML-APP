Chasing la dolce vita

After an online census revealed my great-grandparents’ old address on Highbury Fields, the sorrow I experienced outside the Italian Hospital returns. But this time its source is evident: we used to take our children to the playground by that house, parking right by my great-grandparents’ front door, our shouts and chatting rebounding off the windows. All these coincidences can doubtless be rationally explained, yet they leave me feeling that I live in the wake of tumultuous decisions made long before I was born, that I’m in a web of unseen connections, in friction with the mysteries of an uprooted Italian past.
