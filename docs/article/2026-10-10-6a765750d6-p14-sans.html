Our planet is not much more than the capsule within which we have to live as human beings if we are to survive the vast space voyage upon which we have been engaged for hundreds of millennia – but without noticing yet our condition … We depend upon a little envelope of soil and a rather large envelope of atmosphere for life itself.

Yet, for her, the planetary perspective was not merely a celebration of extraterrestrial exploration but also an opportunity to highlight the limits of Cold War politics centred on the so-called ‘national interest’. The relevant scale of politics was planetary, not national.
