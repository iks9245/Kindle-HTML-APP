I packed the professional papers into removal boxes and set up an archive room in the basement at home, adding my private papers – school essays, diaries, personal letters and photographs – and also downloaded tens of thousands of work-related files and emails from the university server. I then spent three years reading and taking notes, year by year, page by page. Much was factual and emotionally neutral. Some old articles stirred a sense of pride; others were downright embarrassing. Angry letters from former intimate partners were particularly painful to revisit.
