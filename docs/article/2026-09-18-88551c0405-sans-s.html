To what extent can we ever understand the experiences of nonhuman animals? And to what extent do we even understand our own? These questions sit at the heart of the short poetic documentary Animal Eye, which probes the diverse visual worlds of creatures big and small. For the film, the US director Carlo Nasisse pairs the reflections of philosophers and scientists with ruminative in-the-lab images of a wide range of animals. Starting from the biology, beauty and mystery of the eye, Nasisse constructs a broader exploration of the limits of human understanding, and our drive to keep looking regardless.

The alien worlds beside our own – a journey into animal eyes

Director: Carlo Nasisse

Producers: Maria Luisa Santos, Dominic Yarabe
