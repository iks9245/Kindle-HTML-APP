小中大切換為無襯線

Love with nowhere to go

My mother is still alive. No one has died: there is no concrete, historical object that has been pulled beyond my grasp. But a whole set of possibilities that had been woven into the structure of my life and world, all bound up with having a mother who was present, responsive, good enough – those are gone. It has become impossible to call her and poke that mind of hers, so unusually free and unusually deep.
