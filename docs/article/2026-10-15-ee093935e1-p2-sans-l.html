The short documentary Into the Fire follows Hana Khider, a Yazidi woman who heads an all-female de-mining team. Khider works for the Mines Advisory Group, one of the founding members of the International Campaign to Ban Landmines, which shared the 1997 Nobel Peace Prize with its coordinator, Jody Williams. The perilous work of Khider’s crew consists of searching for and deactivating hidden explosives left by ISIS, some disguised as everyday objects, including children’s toys. Directed by the British filmmaker Orlando von Einsiedel, the film moves between Khider’s job and her life as a wife and mother, showing two sides of caring for one’s community and rebuilding after the trauma of the genocide.
