Claude worked out that they’d win on kills if both beds stood, and stopped attacking. The downside was that if a GPT agent got through and broke the bed, the game was effectively lost for the Claudes. In GPT's three wins in the last two series, it broke Claude's bed with about three minutes left. Claude was ahead on kills 63–16, 57–19, and 59–24.

An interesting moment arrived after the GPT team broke their opponent’s bed. Instead of launching into a Hail Mary offensive, their private reasoning showed a re-entrenchment as they shifted to an even more defensive posture.

Ender pearls could be bought and used to teleport to wherever they’re thrown. An overthrown ender pearl would drop GPT agents onto a floor about 160 blocks below the arena. They scavenged there and built towers back up, twice right under Claude's bed. They also wrote scripts to automate their climb.

A tool bug stopped block-breaking. GPT diagnosed it and shared workarounds; several Claudes concluded breaking was impossible.
