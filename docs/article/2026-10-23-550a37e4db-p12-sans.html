Hospitals deliberately targeted by Sudan's RSF fighters, BBC investigation finds

The Specialised Children's Hospital, which shut down at the beginning of the war along with the Teaching Hospital, moved to two other locations, both of which were then shelled and forced to close.

Similarly, the main dialysis centre was struck and then attacked again after it relocated.

By the time the RSF seized the city in October 2025, the Saudi Hospital was the only functioning health facility left.

The medical staff there had rallied to try to keep working as conditions worsened.

Doctors described how sandbags were set up to protect the building.
