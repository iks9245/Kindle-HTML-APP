So, I think the more we look, the more interdependence and nestedness we see. Now, that doesn’t mean there’s no tension, right? There’s always a tension. It’s a beautiful tension between organisms because they have different needs. They have different resources that they need to access, to reproduce, and that creates a beautiful opera of interactions that’s not always beneficial, not always cooperative, but instead it creates a beautiful tension, very much like an opera, where sometimes the characters are getting along, and sometimes they’re not.
