These skills are also useful to people who meet others in moments of crises. LaToya Baines* manages victim advocates on Chicago’s West Side who spend their days supporting people through the worst moments of their lives, and who bring their own histories to that work. She learned the same techniques through training and now teaches them to her staff. ‘Once you learn, you’re able to identify those triggers. I didn’t know that if you yelled at me, I was gonna break down. But now I’m able to [see]: it’s not you, it’s a past trigger. I just stepped back into the past, for something else that happened to me that has nothing to do with you.’ Chicago police officers are being taught to do the same thing, through a programme called Situational Decision-Making. Officers who went through it used less force and made fewer discretionary arrests.
