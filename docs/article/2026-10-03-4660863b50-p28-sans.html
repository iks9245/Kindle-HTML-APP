Questioning a reading also meant learning that a feeling of threat was not always about what was happening in the present; sometimes, it was a reaction to events in the past. The men practised noticing what fear was doing in their bodies, such as a racing pulse, tightened fists, pressure in the chest, and learning to treat those sensations as signals to investigate rather than signs of objective threats. Larry, 31, described it as learning to check in with his body: ‘Far as articulating the things that’s going on inside of my body and how I’m feeling, that’s what I’m really learning.’ What seemed to be a threat from the man across the street might, at times, be coming from somewhere much older.
