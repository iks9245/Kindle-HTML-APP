Take William, who had lost 11 close friends to gun violence by the age of 29. He came to see revenge not as something he did but as something he was. ‘I ain’t been a civilian since I was like eight…,’ he told us. ‘To sleep at night [with what I’ve done] you got to be a different type of monster.’ After his best friend was killed, he said: ‘I bought guns, a lot of them … I wasn’t going to feel safe until somebody was dead.’ Over time, the identity hardened: ‘To put that bet on somebody else, you got to be a cold-hearted motherfucker. Melatonin can’t even help you sleep after that.’
