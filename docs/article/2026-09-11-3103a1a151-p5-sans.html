Today, the Flashpoint archive catalogues 157,509 Flash works—and those are just the ones that happen to be preserved.

I’ll remember one other thing that heavily influenced me: a site called Born Magazine that paired writers and artists with designers and programmers, creating sometimes beautiful multimedia experiences that I haven’t forgotten to this day. If anyone knows anyone who has all that data backed up, oh my god, please get in touch with me. I’d be more than happy to revive all of it.

But I’m not here to praise Flash. It objectively sucked, and everyone knew it objectively sucked. Accessibility? Yeah, no. Unskippable intro animations? Browser back button? Everyone hated Flash even while using it everywhere for all kinds of new stuff. There are entire books about this.
