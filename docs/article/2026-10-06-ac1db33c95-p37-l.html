The reaction from my Facebook readers was swift and polarised. A younger colleague attacked the post as sordid, accusing me of reproducing today the same objectifying male gaze I claimed merely to be reporting from the past. Another woman read the student’s choice as a breaching experiment – a conscious subversion of the grey academic dress code. A third defended my right to report the past as I remembered it, noting the ‘Lutheran asceticism’ often demanded of female academics, and offered her own counter-anecdote about a male presenter whose thin linen trousers had distracted an entire audience.
